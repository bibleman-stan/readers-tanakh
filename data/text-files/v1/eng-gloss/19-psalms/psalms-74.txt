a poem
of Asaph
why? O God have you rejected us to perpetuity
does it smoke? your anger
on the sheep of your pasture

remember your congregation which you acquired antiquity
which you redeemed the tribe of your inheritance
the mountain of Zion

lift up ! your footsteps to the ruins of perpetuity
everything he has done harm to the enemy in the sanctuary

axes

with axes and crowbars

to the ground
they profaned the dwelling place of your name

74:8

they burned all the appointed places of God in the land

our signs
not we have seen
there not still is a prophet
and not is with us

this remember
