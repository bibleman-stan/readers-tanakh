6:1
to the choirmaster with stringed instruments on the sheminith
a psalm of David

6:2
O Yahweh
may not in your anger you rebuke me
and may not in your wrath you discipline me

6:3
show favor to me O Yahweh for am frail
I heal me O Yahweh
for they are disturbed my bones

6:4
and my self it is disturbed exceedingly
and you O Yahweh
until when?

6:5
return ! O Yahweh rescue ! my life
save me
on account of covenant your loyalty

6:6
for there not in death is remembrance of you
in Sheol
who? will he give thanks to you

6:7
I have become weary by my groaning
I make swim in every night my bed
with my tears
my couch I melt

6:8
it has wasted away from grief my eye
it has weak grown
with all my opposers

6:9
turn away from me O all those who do wickedness
for he has heard Yahweh
the sound of my weeping

6:10
he has heard Yahweh my supplication
Yahweh
my prayer he will accept

6:11
may they be ashamed and they may be disturbed exceedingly all my enemies
they will turn back
they will be ashamed a moment
