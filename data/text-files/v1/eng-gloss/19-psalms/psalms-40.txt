40:1
to the choirmaster
of David a psalm

40:2
certainly I waited for Yahweh
and he stretched out to me
and he heard cry for my help

40:3
and he brought up me from a pit of roaring from the mud of the mire
and he set on a rock my feet
he established my steps

40:4
and he put in my mouth a new song praise to our God
they will see many people so they may fear
and they may trust
in Yahweh

40:5
how blessed! is the man
who he has made Yahweh his trust
and not he has turned to proud people
and those who fall away of falsehood

40:6
many you have made you O Yahweh my God your wonders and your plans
to us
not to compare to you
I will declare and I will speak
they are too numerous
for recounting

40:7
sacrifice and offering not you desired
ears you dug for me
burnt offering and sin offering
not you asked for

40:8
then I said here! I have come
in the scroll of the book
it is written on me

40:9
to do your will O my God I desire
and your law
is in the midst of inward my parts

40:10
I have borne news righteousness in the great assembly
here! my lips not I restrain
O Yahweh
you know

40:11
your righteousness not I have concealed in the midst of my heart
your faithfulness and your salvation I have spoken
not I have hidden covenant your loyalty and your faithfulness
to the great assembly

40:12
you O Yahweh
not you will withhold your compassion from me
covenant your loyalty and your faithfulness
continually they preserve me

40:13
for they have surrounded me troubles until there not was number
they have overtaken me my iniquities and not I am able to see
they are numerous more than the hairs of my head
and my heart it has abandoned me

40:14
be pleased O Yahweh to deliver me
O Yahweh
to my help make haste !

40:15
may they be ashamed and they may be abashed altogether those who seek my life
to snatch away it
may they be turned back backwards and may they be humiliated
the people desirous of
my harm

40:16
may they be appalled on the consequence of their shame
those who say to me
aha! aha!

40:17
may they exult and they may rejoice in you
all those who seek you
may they say continually he is great Yahweh
those who love
your salvation

40:18
and I am poor and needy the Lord may he think
to me are my help and my deliverer you
O my God
may not you delay
