38:1
a psalm of David to make remembrance

38:2
O Yahweh
may not in your anger you rebuke me
and in your wrath may you chasten me

38:3
for your arrows they have penetrated me
and it has come down on me your hand

38:4
there not is soundness in my flesh because of your indignation
there not is health in my bones
because of my sin

38:5
for my iniquities they have passed over my head
like a burden heavy
they are too heavy for me

38:6
they stink they fester my wounds
because of
my folly

38:7
I am bent down I am bent over up to muchness
all the day
mourning I have gone about

38:8
for my loins they are full burning pain
and there not is soundness
in my flesh

38:9
I am numb and I am crushed up to muchness
I roar
from the groaning of my heart

38:10
O Lord is before you all my desire
and my sighing
from you not it is hidden

38:11
my heart it palpitates it left has me my strength
and the light of my eyes also they
not are with me

38:12
my friends and my companions
from before my plague they stand
and my kinsmen
from a distance they have stood

38:13
and they set traps those who seek my life
and those who seek my harm they have spoken destruction
and deceit
all the day they devise

38:14
and I am like a deaf person not I hear
and like a dumb person
who not he opens his mouth

38:15
and I have become
like a man who not is hearing
and there not in his mouth
are arguments

38:16
for you O Yahweh I have waited
you will answer
O Lord my God

38:17
for I said lest they should rejoice to me
when slips my foot
on me they magnified themselves

38:18
for I for stumbling am prepared
and my pain is before me continually

38:19
for my iniquity I tell
I am anxious
from my sin

38:20
and my enemies life they are numerous
and they are many those who hate me falsehood

38:21
and those who evil repay in place of good
they accuse me
in place of my good pursuing

38:22
may not you forsake me O Yahweh
O my God
may not you be distant from me

38:23
make haste ! to my help
O Lord
my salvation
