52:1
to the choirmaster
a poem of David

52:2
when came Doeg the Edomite and he told to Saul
and he said to him
he has gone David
to the house of Ahimelech

52:3
why? do you boast in evil
mighty O man
the covenant loyalty of God
is all the day

52:4
destruction
it plots your tongue
like a razor sharpened
O doer of deceit

52:5
you evil love more than good
falsehood more than speaking righteousness Selah

52:6
you love all words of swallowing
a tongue of deceit

52:7
also God he will tear down you to perpetuity
he will snatch up you and he will tear away you from a tent
and he will root up you from the land of living people Selah

52:8
so they may righteous see people so they may fear
and on him they will laugh

52:9
there! the man
who not he made God
his refuge
and he trusted
in the greatness of his riches
he was strong
in his destruction

52:10
and I am like an olive tree luxuriant
in the house of God
I trust in the covenant loyalty of God
forever and ever

52:11
I will give thanks to you for ever
for you have acted
and I may wait for your name for it is good
before faithful your people
