21:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

21:2
O Yahweh
in your strength he rejoices the king
and in your deliverance
how! he exults exceedingly

21:3
the desire of his heart you have given to him
and the request of his lips
not you have withheld Selah

21:4
for you meet him blessings of good things
you set to his head
a crown of pure gold

21:5
life he asked from you you gave it to him
length of days
forever and ever

21:6
is his great honor by your victory
splendor and majesty
you place on him

21:7
for you give him blessings for ever
you make rejoice him with gladness
with your presence

21:8
for the king is trusting in Yahweh
and in the covenant loyalty of the Most High
not he will be shaken

21:9
it will find your hand all your enemies
your right hand
it will find those who hate you

21:10
you will make them like a furnace of fire to the time of your presence
Yahweh in his anger he will swallow up them
and it will consume them fire

21:11
their descendants from the earth you will destroy
and their offspring
from the children of humankind

21:12
for they have extended on you harm
they have planned a scheme
not they will succeed

21:13
for you will make them a shoulder
on your bowstrings
you will prepare on their faces

21:14
rise up ! O Yahweh in your strength
we will sing and we will sing praises
your might
