102:1
a prayer
of an afflicted person if he grows faint
and before Yahweh
he pours out his complaint

102:2
O Yahweh
hear ! my prayer
and cry for my help
to you let it come

102:3
may not you hide your face from me on the day when it is distress
to me incline to me your ear
on the day when I will call out
hurry answer me

102:4
for they have come to an end in smoke my days
and my bones
like a hearth they have been burned

102:5
it has been struck like vegetation
and it has withered my heart
for I have forgotten
from eating my food

102:6
from the sound of my groaning
it clings my bones
to my flesh

102:7
I am like
a desert owl of the wilderness
I am
like an owl of the waste places

102:8
I am wakeful and I have become
like a bird
isolated on a roof

102:9
all the day
they have taunted me my enemies
those who mock me
by me they have sworn

102:10
for ashes
like food I have eaten
and my drinks
with weeping I have mixed

102:11
because of your indignation and your wrath
for you have picked up me
and you have thrown away me

102:12
my days
are like a shadow extended
and I
like vegetation I am withering

102:13
and you O Yahweh
for ever you sit
and remembrance of you
is to a generation and a generation

102:14
you you will arise
you will have compassion on Zion
for it is the time to show favor to it
for it has come the appointed time

102:15
for they take pleasure in your servants
its stones
and its dust they show favor to

102:16
and they may fear nations
the name of Yahweh
and all the kings of the earth
your glory

102:17
for he has built Yahweh Zion
he has appeared
in his glory

102:18
he has turned
to the prayer of the destitute person
and not he has despised
their prayer

102:19
let it be this written
for a generation later
and a people who will be created
it will praise Yahweh

102:20
for he has looked down
from the height of his holiness
Yahweh
from heaven to earth he has looked

102:21
to hear
the groaning of prisoners
to set free
the sons of death

102:22
to recount in Zion
the name of Yahweh
and his praise
in Jerusalem

102:23
when gather peoples together
and kingdoms
to serve Yahweh

102:24
he has humbled in the way my strength
he has my short cut days

102:25
I said
O my God
may not you lift up me
in the middle of my days
are in a generation of generations your years

102:26
before
the earth you founded
and were the work of your hands the heavens

102:27
they they will perish and you you will endure
and all of them
like garment they will wear out
like clothing you will change them and they may pass away

102:28
and you are he
and your years
not they will come to an end

102:29
the children of your servants they will dwell
and their offspring
before you it will be established
