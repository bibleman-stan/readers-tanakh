22:1
to the choirmaster on the doe of the dawn
a psalm of David

22:2
O my God my God why? have you forsaken me
are far from my deliverance
the words of cry of my distress

22:3
O my God
I call out by day and not you answer
and night
and not repose belongs to me

22:4
and you are holy
sitting
the praises of Israel

22:5
in you they trusted our ancestors
they trusted
and you delivered them

22:6
to you they cried out and they were delivered
in you they trusted and not they were ashamed

22:7
and I am a worm and not a man
a reproach of humankind
and despised of a people

22:8
all those who see me they mock me
they make a separation with a lip
they shake a head

22:9
roll to Yahweh let him rescue him
let him deliver him
for he delights in him

22:10
for you were drawing forth me from the belly
were making trust me
on the breasts of my mother

22:11
on you I was cast from the womb
from the belly of my mother
have been my God you

22:12
may not you be far from me for trouble is near
for there not is a helper

22:13
they have surrounded me bulls many
mighty bulls of Bashan they have encircled me

22:14
they have opened on me their mouth
a lion
tearing and roaring

22:15
like water I have been poured out and they have become dislocated
all my bones
it has become my heart like wax
it has melted
in the midst of inward my parts

22:16
it has dried up like earthenware my strength
and my tongue has been made to cling to my jaws
and to the dust of death you put me

22:17
for they have surrounded me
dogs
a company of evil-doers they have encircled me
like a lion
my hands and my feet

22:18
I count all my bones
they they look
they look on me

22:19
they distribute my clothes to themselves
and on my clothing
they cast a lot

22:20
and you O Yahweh may not you be distant
O my strength
to my help make haste !

22:21
deliver ! from the sword my life
from the hand of a dog
only my life

22:22
save me from the mouth of a lion
and from the horns of wild oxen you have answered me

22:23
I will recount your name to my brothers
in among the assembly I will praise you

22:24
O those fearing Yahweh praise him
O all the offspring of Jacob honor him
and be afraid from him
O all the offspring of Israel

22:25
for not he has despised and not he has detested the affliction of an afflicted person
and not he has hidden his face from him
and when cried for help he to him he heard

22:26
is from with you
my praise
in the great assembly
my vows I will pay
before those fearing him

22:27
they will eat humble people so they may be satisfied
they will praise Yahweh those who seek him
may it live your heart for ever

22:28
they will remember and they may turn to Yahweh all the ends of the earth
so they may bow down before you
all the clans of the nations

22:29
for belongs to Yahweh the kingship
and he is ruling
over the nations

22:30
they have eaten and they have bowed down all the fat people of the earth
before him they will bow down all those who are about to go down of dust
and his life
not he has preserved alive

22:31
offspring it will serve him
it will be recounted of the Lord to the generation

22:32
they will come and they may declare his righteousness
to a people about to be born
that he has acted
