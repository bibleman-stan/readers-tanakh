33:1
shout for joy righteous O people in Yahweh
for upright people
is fitting praise

33:2
give thanks to Yahweh with harp
with lyre of ten
make music to him

33:3
sing to him a new song
do well to play
with a shout of joy

33:4
for is upright the word of Yahweh
and all his work
is in faithfulness

33:5
he is loving righteousness and justice
the covenant loyalty of Yahweh
it is full the earth

33:6
by the word of Yahweh the heavens they were made
and by the breath of his mouth
all their host

33:7
he is gathering like heap the waters of the sea
he is putting in storehouses the deeps

33:8
let them fear from Yahweh all the earth
from him let them be afraid
all the inhabitants of the world

33:9
for he spoke and it was
he commanded
and it stood forth

33:10
Yahweh
he makes ineffectual the counsel of nations
he frustrates
the plans of peoples

33:11
the counsel of Yahweh for ever it stands
the plans of his heart
to a generation and a generation

33:12
how blessed! is the nation which Yahweh is its God
the people which he has chosen to an inheritance for himself

33:13
from the heavens he looks Yahweh
he sees
all the children of humankind

33:14
from the place of his dwelling he looks
to all the inhabitants of the earth

33:15
the one who forms together their heart
the one who attends
to all their deeds

33:16
not king is saved by greatness of army
a warrior
not he is delivered by greatness of strength

33:17
is a vain hope horse for victory
and by the greatness of its strength
not it delivers

33:18
here! the eye of Yahweh is to those fearing him
to those who hope for covenant his loyalty

33:19
to deliver from death their life
and to preserve alive them
in famine

33:20
our self it waits for Yahweh
is our help and our shield he

33:21
for in him it rejoices our heart
for in the name of his holiness we trust

33:22
may it be covenant your loyalty O Yahweh toward us
just as
we hope for you
