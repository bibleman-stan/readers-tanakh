45:1
to the choirmaster on Shoshannim of the sons of Korah
a poem
a song of love

45:2
it is aroused my heart a good word
am speaking I my work to the king
my tongue
is a stylus of a scribe skilled

45:3
you are handsome more than the sons of humankind
it has been poured grace on your lips
therefore he has blessed you God for ever

45:4
gird your sword on a thigh mighty O one
your splendor
and your majesty

45:5
and your majesty prosper ride forth
on a matter of truth and humility righteousness
and let it teach you awesome your right deeds hand

45:6
your arrows
are sharpened
peoples under you they will fall
in the heart of
the enemies of the king

45:7
your throne O God is forever and ever
is a scepter of uprightness
the scepter of your kingdom

45:8
you have loved righteousness and you have hated
wickedness therefore he has anointed you God your God the oil of joy
more than your companions

45:9
are myrrh and aloes cassia all your clothes
from palaces of ivory
stringed instrument they have made glad you

45:10
daughters of kings are among noble your women
she stands a queen-consort to your right hand
in the gold of Ophir

45:11
listen O daughter and see and incline your ear
and forget your people
and the house of your father

45:12
so may he desire the king your beauty
for he is your lord
and bow down to him

45:13
and the daughter of Tyre with a gift your face they will entreat
rich ones of a people

45:14
is all glorious a daughter of a king within
is from settings of gold her clothing

45:15
to embroidered robes she is brought to the king
virgins her behind female her companions
are being brought to you

45:16
they are brought with joy and rejoicing
they go
in the palace of the king

45:17
in place of your ancestors they will be your children
you will make them into princes
in all the land

45:18
I will cause to be remembered your name in every generation and a generation
therefore peoples they will give thanks to you
for ever and ever
