24:1
of David
a psalm
belongs to Yahweh the earth and what fills it
the world
and those who dwell in it

24:2
for he on the seas he founded it
and on the rivers
he established it

24:3
who? will he go up on the mountain of Yahweh
and who? will he stand
in the place of his holiness

24:4
a innocent person of hands
and a pure person of heart
who not he has lifted up to falsehood my desire
and not he has sworn an oath to deceit

24:5
he will lift up blessing from with Yahweh
and righteousness
from the God of his salvation

24:6
this is the generation of those who seek him
those who seek your face O Jacob Selah

24:7
lift up O gates your heads
and be lifted up O doors of antiquity
so he may come
the king of glory

24:8
who? is this the king of glory
strong Yahweh and mighty
Yahweh
mighty of battle

24:9
lift up O gates your heads
and lift up O doors of antiquity
so he may come
the king of glory

24:10
who? that is this the king of glory
Yahweh of hosts
he is the king of glory Selah
