54:1
to the choirmaster with stringed instruments
a poem of David

54:2
when came the Ziphites and they said to Saul
¿ not is David
hiding himself with us

54:3
O God by your name save me
and by your strength you will vindicate me

54:4
O God hear my prayer
give ear !
to the words of my mouth

54:5
for strangers they have risen up on me
and ruthless people they have sought my life
not they have set God to before themselves Selah

54:6
here! God is a helper of me
the Lord
is those who sustain my life

54:7
may he bring back the evil to my enemies
in your faithfulness
destroy them

54:8
in voluntariness I will sacrifice to you
I will give thanks to your name O Yahweh for it is good

54:9
for from every trouble he has delivered me
and on my enemies
it has looked my eye
