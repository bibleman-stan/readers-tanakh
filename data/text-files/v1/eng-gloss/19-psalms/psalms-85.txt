85:1
to the choirmaster of the sons of Korah a psalm

85:2
you showed favor to O Yahweh your land
you turned back
the captivity of Jacob

85:3
you forgave
the iniquity of your people
you covered all their sin Selah

85:4
you withdrew all your fury
you turned back
from the burning of your anger

85:5
restore us
O God of our salvation
and break your anger with us

85:6
¿ to forever will you be angry with us
will you prolong? your anger
to a generation and a generation

85:7
¿ not you
will you return will you give life? us
and your people
they will rejoice in you

85:8
show us O Yahweh covenant your loyalty
and your salvation
you will give to us

85:9
I will listen to
whatever he will say God Yahweh
for he will speak peace
to his people and to faithful his people
and may not they return to folly

85:10
surely is near to those fearing him his salvation
to dwell glory in our land

85:11
covenant loyalty and faithfulness they meet together
righteousness and peace they kiss

85:12
faithfulness
from the earth it springs up
and righteousness
from heaven it looks down

85:13
also Yahweh
he will give the good
and our land
it will give its produce

85:14
righteousness
before him it will go
and it will make to the way of his footsteps
