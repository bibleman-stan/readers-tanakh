79:1
a psalm
of Asaph
O God they have come the nations in your inheritance
they have made unclean the temple of your holiness
they have made Jerusalem into heaps of ruins

79:2
they have given the corpses of your servants
food to the birds of the heavens
the flesh faithful your people
to the animals of the earth

79:3
they have poured out their blood like water
round about Jerusalem
and there not was one who buried them

79:4
we have become a reproach to our neighbors
mockery and derision
to those around us

79:5
until when? O Yahweh will you be angry? to perpetuity
will it burn? like fire
your jealousy

79:6
pour out your anger against the nations which not they know you
and on kingdoms
which on your name
not they call

79:7
for it has consumed Jacob
and its habitation they have devastated

79:8
may not you remember to us iniquities former
surely quickly may they come to meet us your compassion
for we have low become exceedingly

79:9
help us O God of our salvation
on the matter of the glory of your name
and deliver us and atone on our sins
for the sake of your name

79:10
why? will they say the nations where? is their God
let it be known among the nations to our eyes
the vengeance of
the blood of your servants poured out

79:11
may it come before you the groaning of the prisoner
according to the greatness of your arm
leave over
the sons of death

79:12
and repay to our neighbors sevenfold into their bosom
their reproach which they reproached you O Lord

79:13
and we your people and the sheep of your pasture we will give thanks to you
for ever
to a generation and a generation
we will recount
your praise
