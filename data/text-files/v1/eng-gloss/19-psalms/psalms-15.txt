15:1
a psalm
of David
O Yahweh
who? will he sojourn in your tent
who? will he dwell
on the mountain of your holiness

15:2
one who walks blameless
and one who does righteousness
and one who speaks truth
in his heart

15:3
not he takes slander on his tongue
not he does to his evil neighbor
and reproach
not he lifts up on his kinsman

15:4
is despised in his view a rejected person
and those fearing Yahweh he honors
he swears to do harm
and not he changes

15:5
his money not he gives for interest and a bribe on the innocent
not he takes
one who these does things
not he will be shaken for ever
