87:1
of the sons of Korah a psalm a song
his foundation
is among the mountains of holiness

87:2
is loving Yahweh the gates of Zion
more than all
the dwelling places of Jacob

87:3
glorious things is spoken in you
O city of God Selah

I will bring to remembrance Rahab and Babylon
to those who know me
there! Philistia and Tyre with Cush
this one
it was born there

87:5
and of Zion it will be said
a person and a person he was born in it
and he will establish it the Most High

87:6
Yahweh
he will count when records peoples
this one he was born there Selah

and singers like pipers
all my springs are in you
