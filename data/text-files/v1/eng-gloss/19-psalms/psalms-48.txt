48:1
a song a psalm
of the sons of Korah

48:2
is great Yahweh and is to be praised exceedingly
in the city of our God
the mountain of his holiness

48:3
it is beautiful of height the joy of all the earth
the mountain of Zion the remotest parts of Zaphon
the town of
the great king

48:4
God is in its fortresses
he has made himself known to a refuge

48:5
for there! the kings they assembled
they passed on together

48:6
they saw thus they were astonished
they were terrified they hurried away

48:7
trembling it seized them there
anguish
like woman giving birth

48:8
with a wind of the east
you shatter
ships of Tarshish

48:9
just as we have heard so we have seen
in the city of Yahweh of hosts in the city of our God
God he will establish it until perpetuity Selah

48:10
we have reflected on O God covenant your loyalty
in the midst of
your temple

48:11
like your name O God
so your praise is over the ends of the earth
righteousness
it is your right full hand

48:12
it will rejoice the mountain of Zion
they will be glad the daughters of Judah
on account of
your judgments

48:13
go around Zion and go round it
count
its towers

48:14
set your heart to its rampart
walk through its fortresses
so that you may recount it
to a generation later

48:15
for this is God our God forever and ever
he will guide us on dying
