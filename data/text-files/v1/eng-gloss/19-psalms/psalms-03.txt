3:1
a psalm of David
when fled he
from before Absalom his son

3:2
O Yahweh
how! they are many my opponents
many people
are rising up on me

3:3
many people are saying of my self
there not is deliverance for him in God Selah

3:4
and you O Yahweh
are a shield behind me
my honor
and the one who lifts up my head

3:5
my voice
to Yahweh I called out
and he answered me from the mountain of his holiness Selah

3:6
I I lay down
and I slept !
I awoke
for Yahweh he sustains me

3:7
not I will be afraid
from ten thousands of people
who all around
they have taken a stand on me

3:8
arise ! O Yahweh save me O my God
for you have struck all my enemies jaw
the teeth of wicked people you broken have

3:9
belongs to Yahweh deliverance
toward is your people your blessing Selah
