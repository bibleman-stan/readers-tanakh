99:1
Yahweh he reigns let them tremble peoples
he is sitting cherubim
let it quake the earth

99:2
Yahweh in Zion is great
and is exalted he
over all the peoples

99:3
let them give thanks to your great name and to be feared
is holy he

99:4
and the strength of a king justice he loves
you have established uprightness
justice and righteousness
in Jacob you have done

99:5
exalt Yahweh our God
and bow down to the footstool of his feet
is holy he

99:6
Moses and Aaron were among his priests
and Samuel was among those who call on his name
they were calling out to Yahweh
and he answered them

99:7
in a pillar of cloud he spoke to them
they kept his testimonies
and a decree he gave to them

99:8
O Yahweh our God you answered them
a God forgiving you were them
and avenging yourself
on their deeds

99:9
exalt Yahweh our God
and bow down to the mountain of his holiness
for is holy
Yahweh our God
