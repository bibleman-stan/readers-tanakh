27:1
of David Yahweh is my light and my salvation from whom? will I be afraid
Yahweh is the refuge of my life
from whom? will I be in dread

27:2
when drew near on evil me-doers to devour my flesh
my opponents and my enemies to me
they stumbled and they fell

27:3
though it will encamp on me a camp not it will fear my heart
though it will arise on me warfare
in this
I will be trusting

27:4
one thing I have asked from with Yahweh it I will seek
to dwell I in the house of Yahweh all the days of my life
to look on the kindness of Yahweh
and to inquire in his temple

27:5
for he will conceal me in his refuge in a day of trouble
he will hide me in the hiding place of his tent
on a rock
he will raise up me

27:6
and now it will rise my head above my enemies around me
and I will sacrifice in his tent sacrifices of a shout of joy
I will sing and I will sing praises
to Yahweh

27:7
hear O Yahweh my voice I call out
and show favor to me and answer me

27:8
of you it has said my heart seek my face
your face O Yahweh I will seek

27:9
may not you hide your face from me may not you turn aside in anger
your servant
my help you have been
may not you abandon me and may not you forsake me
O God of my salvation

27:10
for my father and my mother they have forsaken me
and Yahweh he will receive me

27:11
teach me O Yahweh
your way
and lead me in a path of level ground
on account of
my enemies

27:12
may not you give me in the desire of my opponents
for they have arisen on me witnesses of falsehood
and a witness of violence

27:13
if not
I had believed to look on the goodness of Yahweh
in the land of living people

27:14
wait
to Yahweh
be strong so may it show strength your heart
and wait
to Yahweh
