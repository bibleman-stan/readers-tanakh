95:1
come
let us sing for joy to Yahweh
let us shout for joy
to the rock of our salvation

95:2
let us approach his presence with thanksgiving
with songs
let us shout for joy to him

95:3
for is a great God Yahweh
and a great king
above all gods

95:4
whom are in his hand
the depths of the earth
and the peaks of the mountains belong to him

95:5
whom belongs to him the sea
and he he made it
and the dry land
his hands they formed

95:6
come
let us bow down and let us kneel down
let us kneel
before Yahweh our maker

95:7
for he is our God
and we are the people of his pasture
and the flock of his hand
this day
if to his voice you will listen

95:8
may not you harden your heart
like Meribah
like the day of Massah
in the wilderness

95:9
when they put to the test me
your ancestors
they tested me
also they had seen my work

95:10
forty years I loathed a generation
and I said
are a people who go astray of heart they
and they
not they know my ways

95:11
that I swore an oath in my anger
if they will go !
into my rest
