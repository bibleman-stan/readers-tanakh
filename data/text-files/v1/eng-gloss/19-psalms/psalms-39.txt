39:1
to the choirmaster for Jeduthun
a psalm of David

39:2
I said
I will guard my ways from sinning with my tongue
I will keep to my mouth a muzzle
while still the wicked is to before me

39:3
I was dumb silence I was silent from good
and my pain it was stirred up

39:4
it became hot my heart in inner my being
in my sighing it burned fire
I spoke
with my tongue

39:5
make known to me O Yahweh my end
and the measure of my days what? is it
let me know
how! fleeting am I

39:6
here! handbreadths you have made my days
and my lifespan is like nothing before you
is only all vanity all humankind
which stands Selah

39:7
only an image he goes about a person
only vanity people are in turmoil !
he heaps up
and not he knows who? will be gathering them

39:8
and now what? do I wait for O Lord
my hope
is to you it

39:9
from all my transgressions deliver me
a reproach of the foolish
may not you make me

39:10
I am dumb not my open I mouth
for you have acted

39:11
remove from on me your plague
from the hostility of your hand
I have come to an end

39:12
with rebukes on iniquity you have disciplined a person
and you have melted like moth desired his thing
is only vanity all humankind Selah

39:13
hear ! my prayer O Yahweh and cry for my help give ear to ! to my tears
may not you be silent
for am a sojourner I with you
a resident alien
like all my ancestors

smear from me so let me be cheerful
