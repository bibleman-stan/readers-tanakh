121:1
a song
of the ascents
I lift up my eyes to the mountains
from where?
will it come my help

121:2
my help is from with Yahweh
maker of
heaven and earth

121:3
may not he permit to slip your foot
may not he slumber
your protector

121:4
there! not he will slumber and not he will sleep
the protector of
Israel

121:5
Yahweh is your protector
Yahweh is your shade
at the hand of your right

121:6
by day
the sun not it will strike you
and the moon in the night

121:7
Yahweh
he will protect you from all harm
he will protect
your life

121:8
Yahweh
he will protect going out you and coming you
from now
and until perpetuity
