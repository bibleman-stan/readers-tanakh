109:1
to the choirmaster of David a psalm
O God of my praise
may not you be silent

109:2
for the mouth of the wicked and a mouth of deceit on me they have opened
they have spoken with me
a tongue of falsehood

109:3
and words of hatred they have surrounded me
and they have fought against me without cause

109:4
in place of my love they accuse me
and I am prayer

109:5
and they have put on evil me in place of good
and hatred
in place of my love

109:6
appoint on him a wicked person
and an accuser
let him stand on his right hand

109:7
when is judged he let him come forth guilty
and his prayer
let it become sin

109:8
may they be his days few
his office
may he take another

109:9
may they be his children fatherless ones
and his wife
a widow

109:10
and really may they wander his children and they will beg
and they will seek
away from their ruins

109:11
may he strike a creditor to all that belongs to him
and may they plunder strangers his property

109:12
may not it belong to him one who prolongs loyalty
and may not it belong one who shows favor
to fatherless his children

109:13
may it be his posterity to cut off
in a generation another
may it be wiped out their name

109:14
may it be remembered the iniquity of his ancestors to Yahweh
and the sin of his mother
may not it be wiped out

109:15
let them be before Yahweh continually
and may he cut off from the earth their memory

109:16
because
that not he remembered to do loyalty and he harassed anyone poor and needy and disheartened of heart to put to them death

109:17
and he loved a curse and it came to him
and not he delighted in blessing
and it was far from him

109:18
and he put on a curse
like his garment
and it went like water in inward his parts
and like oil
in his bones

109:19
may it be for him like a garment which he wraps himself
and to a waistband
which continually he girds on it

109:20
this be the wages of my accusers from with Yahweh
and those who evil speak
on my self

109:21
and you O Yahweh O Lord
deal with me for the sake of your name
for is good covenant your loyalty
deliver me

109:22
for am poor and needy I
and my heart
someone has pierced in inner my being

109:23
like a shadow when stretches out it I have vanished
I have been shaken off
like locust

109:24
my knees they have staggered from fasting
and my flesh
it has become lean from fatness

109:25
and I have become a reproach to them
they see me
they shake ! their head

109:26
help me O Yahweh my God
save me according to covenant your loyalty

109:27
so they may know that is your this hand
you O Yahweh you have done it

109:28
they will curse they and you will bless
they have arisen and they have been ashamed
and your servant he will rejoice

109:29
may they be clothed my accusers ignominy
and may they be wrapped like robe their shame

109:30
I will give thanks to Yahweh exceedingly with my mouth
and in among many people I will praise him

109:31
for he stands to the right hand of the needy
to save him
from those who judge his self
