17:1
a prayer
of David
hear ! O Yahweh righteousness
pay attention to ! cry of my entreaty
give ear to ! my prayer
with not
lips of deceit

17:2
from before you
my judgment may it come forth
your eyes
may they see uprightness

17:3
you have tested my heart you have visited night
you have tested me not you will find
I have resolved
not it will transgress my mouth

17:4
to the deeds of humankind
by the word of your lips
I I have watched
the paths of violent persons

17:5
they have held firm my steps
in your tracks
not they have been shaken my footsteps

17:6
I I call out to you for you will answer me O God
incline your ear to me
hear my utterance

17:7
make wonderful covenant your loyalti
O deliverer of those who take refuge
from those who rise up
by your right hand

17:8
keep me
like the pupil of the daughter of an eye
in the shadow of your wings
you will hide me

17:9
from before wicked people
who they have assailed me
my enemies with soul
they surround me

17:10
their fat they have closed up
their mouth
they have spoken with pride

17:11
our steps
now they have surrounded us
their eyes they set
to bend down on the ground

17:12
his likeness
is like a lion
which it longs to tear to pieces
and like a young lion
which sits in hiding places

17:13
arise ! O Yahweh
confront ! his face
make bow down him
deliver ! my life
from the your wicked sword

17:14
from men your hand O Yahweh from men from the world
whom their portion is in the life and treasured your thing you fill their belly
they are satisfied sons
and they leave their abundance
to their children

17:15
I
in righteousness
I will see your face
I will be satisfied when awake
your likeness
