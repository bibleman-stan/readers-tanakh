141:1
a psalm
of David
O Yahweh I call out to you
make haste ! to me
give ear to ! my voice
when call I to you

141:2
may it be established my prayer incense before you
the raising of my hands
an offering of evening

141:3
set ! O Yahweh
a guard for my mouth
keep watch !
over the door of my lips

141:4
may not you turn my heart to evil anything to practice practices in wickedness
with people who do wickedness
and not I will eat
in their delicacies

141:5
let him strike me a righteous person loyalty and let him rebuke me
oil of head
may not it refuse my head
for still and my prayer
is on their evil deeds

141:6
they have been thrown down at the hands of a rock
their judges
and they will hear my words
for they are pleasant

141:7
like one who plows and who breaks up the earth
they have been scattered our bones
to the mouth of Sheol

141:8
for are to you O Yahweh O Lord my eyes
in you I take refuge
may not you lay bare my life

141:9
keep me
from the hands of the trap
which they have laid for me
and the snares of
those who do wickedness

141:10
let them fall in his own wicked nets people
together I
until I will pass by
