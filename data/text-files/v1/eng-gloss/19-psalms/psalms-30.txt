30:1
a psalm the song of the dedication of the house of David

30:2
I will exalt you O Yahweh for you have drawn up me
and not you have allowed to rejoice my enemies to me

30:3
O Yahweh my God
I cried for help to you
and you healed me

30:4
O Yahweh
you brought up from Sheol my life
you preserved alive me
from going my down the pit

30:5
sing praises to Yahweh O faithful his people
and give thanks
to the remembrance of his holiness

30:6
for a moment is in his anger life is in his favor
in the evening it passes the night weeping
and to the morning a shout of joy

30:7
and I I said in my prosperity
not I will be shaken for ever

30:8
O Yahweh
in your favor you made stand to my mountain
strength you hid your face
I was disturbed

30:9
to you O Yahweh I called out
and to the Lord
I sought favor

30:10
what? is the profit in my blood in going my down to the pit
¿ will it give thanks to you dust
¿ will it declare your faithfulness

30:11
hear O Yahweh and show favor to me
O Yahweh
be a helper of me

30:12
you turned my wailing into dancing to me you loosened my sackcloth
and you girded me joy

30:13
so that it may sing praises to you honor and not it may be silent
O Yahweh my God
for ever I will give thanks to you
