140:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

140:2
rescue me O Yahweh from a evil man
from a man of violence you will preserve me

140:3
those who they evil plan things in the heart
every day
they stir up trouble wars

140:4
they sharpen their tongue like a snake
the venom of a viper
is under their lips Selah

140:5
keep me O Yahweh from the hands of the wicked
from a man of violence you will preserve me
those who they have planned
to trip up my footsteps

140:6
they have hidden proud people a trap for me
and ropes
they have spread a net to the side of a track
snares they have set for me Selah

140:7
I say to Yahweh are my God you
give ear to ! O Yahweh
the sound of my supplications

140:8
O Yahweh O Lord the strength of my salvation
you cover my head
in a day of weaponry

140:9
may not you grant O Yahweh the desires of the wicked
his plot may not you promote
they will arise Selah

140:10
the head of my surroundings
the mischief of their lips may it cover them

140:11
may they be dropped on them
burning coals
in the fire let someone make fall them
in pits
which not they will arise

140:12
a person of tongue not let him be established in the land
a person of violence trouble
let it hunt him
for blows

140:13
I know
that he will do Yahweh the cause of the poor
the justice of
needy people

140:14
righteous surely people they will give thanks to your name
they will dwell upright people
with your presence
