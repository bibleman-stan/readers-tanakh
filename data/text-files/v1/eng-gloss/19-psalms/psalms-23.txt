23:1
a psalm of David
Yahweh is my shepherd
not I lack

23:2
in pastures of grass he makes lie down me
at waters of rest he leads me

23:3
my self he restores
he guides me in tracks of righteousness
for the sake of his name

23:4
also for I will go in a valley of deep darkness not I will fear harm
for you are with me
your rod and your staff
they they comfort me

23:5
you arrange before me a table
before my opposers
you have refreshed with oil my head
my cup is saturation

23:6
good surely and covenant loyalty they will pursue me all the days of my life
and I will return in the house of Yahweh
for length of days
