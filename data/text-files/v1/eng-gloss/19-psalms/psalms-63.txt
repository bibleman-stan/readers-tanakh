63:1
a psalm of David
when was he
in the wilderness of Judah

63:2
O God are my God you
I am seeking earnestly you
it is thirsty for you my self
it longs for you my flesh
in a land dry and weary not water

63:3
thus in the sanctuary I have seen you
to see your strength
and your glory

63:4
for is good covenant your loyalty more than life
my lips they will extol you

63:5
thus I will bless you in my life
in your name
I will lift up my hands

63:6
like fat and fatness it will be satisfied my self
and lips of cries of joy
it will praise you my mouth

63:7
if I have remembered you on my beds
in the night-watches
I meditate on you

63:8
for you are a help of me
and in the shadow of your wings I shout for joy

63:9
it clings my self after you
me
it your right supports hand

63:10
and they
to destruction they are seeking my life
they will go
in the lower parts of the earth

63:11
people will pour him over the hands of the sword
the portion of foxes they will be

63:12
and the king he will rejoice in God
he will boast every one who swears an oath by him
for it will be shut up
the mouth of those who speak falsehood
