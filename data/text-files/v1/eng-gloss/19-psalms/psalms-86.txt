86:1
a prayer
of David
incline O Yahweh your ear answer me
for am poor and needy I

86:2
preserve ! my life for am faithful I save your servant
you are my God
who trusts to you

86:3
show favor to me O Lord
for to you I call out
all the day

86:4
make glad
the self of your servant
for to you O Lord
my being I lift up

86:5
for you O Lord
are good and ready to forgive
and great of covenant loyalty
to all those who call out to you

86:6
give ear to ! O Yahweh
my prayer
and pay attention !
to the sound of my supplications

86:7
on the day of my distress
I call out to you
for you will answer me

86:8
there not is like you among the gods O Lord
and there not are like your works

86:9
all the nations which you have made
they will come and they may bow down before you O Lord
so they may glorify your name

86:10
for are great you
and one who does wonders
you are God to alone you

86:11
teach me O Yahweh your way
I will walk in your truth
unite my heart
to fear your name

86:12
I will give thanks to you O Lord my God
with all my heart
and I will glorify your name for ever

86:13
for covenant your loyalty
is great toward me
and you have delivered my life
from Sheol lowest

86:14
O God arrogant people they have risen up on me
and a company of ruthless people
they have sought my life
and not they have set you to before themselves

86:15
and you O Lord
are a God compassionate and gracious
long of anger
and great of covenant loyalty and faithfulness

86:16
turn to me
and show favor to me
give ! your strength to your servant
and save !
the son of your maidservant

86:17
do with me a sign
of goodness
so they may see those who hate me and they may be ashamed
for you O Yahweh
you have helped me and you have comforted me
