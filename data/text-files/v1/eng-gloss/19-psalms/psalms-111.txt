111:1
praise Yahweh I will give thanks to Yahweh with all the heart
in the council of upright people and the congregation

111:2
are great the works of Yahweh
they are studied
by all those delighting in them

111:3
is splendor and majesty his work
and his righteousness
is enduring for ever

111:4
remembrance he has appointed of his wonders
is gracious and compassionate Yahweh

111:5
food he gives to those fearing him
he remembers for ever his covenant

111:6
the power of his works he has declared to his people
to give to them
an inheritance of nations

111:7
the works of his hands are faithfulness and justice
are reliable
all his precepts

111:8
they are sustained for ever for ever
they are done
in faithfulness and uprightness

111:9
redemption he sent for his people
he ordained for ever his covenant
is holy and to be feared his name

111:10
is the beginning of wisdom the fear of Yahweh
an good understanding belongs to all those who do them
his praise
is enduring for ever
