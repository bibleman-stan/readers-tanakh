49:1
to the choirmaster of the sons of Korah a psalm

49:2
this hear O all the peoples
give ear
O all the inhabitants of the world

49:3
both the children of humankind as well as the children of man
alike
the rich and the needy

49:4
my mouth it will speak wisdom
and the meditation of my heart will be understanding

49:5
I will incline to a proverb my ear
I open will with a harp
my riddle

49:6
why? will I fear in days of trouble
the iniquity of my heels it surrounds me

49:7
those relying on their wealth
and in the greatness of their riches
they boast

49:8
a brother
not certainly he will ransom anyone
not he will give to God his ransom

49:9
so it may be costly the ransom of their life
and he will cease for ever

49:10
so he may live still to perpetuity
not he will see the pit

49:11
for anyone will see wise people they die
alike a fool and a stupid person they perish
and they leave to other people their wealth

49:12
inner their being their houses for ever
dwelling their places to a generation and a generation
they called with their names
on lands

49:13
and humankind in honor not he remains
he is like like the animals which they are destroyed

49:14
this is their way stupidity belongs to them
and after them in their mouth they take delight Selah

49:15
like sheep for Sheol they have been appointed death it will shepherd them
and they ruled over them upright people to the morning
and their form is for the consuming of Sheol
from lofty abode of him

49:16
surely God
he will redeem my life from the hand of Sheol
for he will take me Selah

49:17
may not you fear if he will become rich anyone
if it will increase
the honor of his house

49:18
for not in his death he will take anything
not it will go down after him his honor

49:19
for his self in his life he blessed
and people praise you
if you will do well to yourself

49:20
it will go to the generation of his ancestors
until perpetuity
not they will see light

49:21
humankind with honor and not he understands
he is like like the animals which they are destroyed
