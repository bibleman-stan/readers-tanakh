83:1
a song a psalm of Asaph

83:2
O God let not silence belong to you
may not you be silent and may not you be inactive O God

83:3
for here! your enemies
they are in an uproar !
and those who hate you
they have lifted a head

83:4
on your people
they make crafty counsel
so they may consult together
on treasured your ones

83:5
they have said
come
and let us annihilate them from a nation
and not it will be remembered the name of Israel again

83:6
for they have consulted heart together
on you
a covenant they are making

83:7
the tents of Edom
and the Ishmaelites
Moab and the Hagrites

83:8
Gebal and Ammon
and Amalek
Philistia
with the inhabitants of Tyre

83:9
also Assyria
it has joined itself with them
they have become an arm for the children of Lot Selah

83:10
do to them like Midian
like Sisera like Jabin
at the wadi of Kishon

83:11
they were destroyed at Endor
they became dung
for the ground

83:12
make them noble their people
like Oreb and like Zeeb
and like Zebah and like Zalmunna
all their princes

83:13
who they said
let us take possession of for ourselves

the pastures of God

83:14
O my God
make them like whirling dust
like chaff
before a wind

83:15
like a fire which it burns a forest
and like a flame
which it sets ablaze mountains

83:16
so
you will pursue them with your storm
and with storm-your wind you will terrify them

83:17
fill their faces shame
so they may seek your name O Yahweh

83:18
may they be ashamed and they may be dismayed until perpetuity
and may they be abashed and they may perish

83:19
and they may know
that you your name is Yahweh to alone you
the Most High
over all the earth
