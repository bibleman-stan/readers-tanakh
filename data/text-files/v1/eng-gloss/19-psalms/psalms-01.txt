1:1
how blessed! is the person
who not he walks in the counsel of wicked people
and in the way of sinners not he stands
and in the seat of mockers
not he sits

1:2
that except is in the law of Yahweh
his delight
and in his law he meditates
by day and night

1:3
and he is
like a tree planted at streams of
water which its fruit it gives at appropriate its time
and its leafage not it withers
and all that he does it succeeds

1:4
not are so the wicked people
that except they are like the chaff
which it drives about it wind

1:5
therefore not they will wicked stand people in the judgment
and sinners
in the congregation of righteous people

1:6
for is knowing Yahweh the way of righteous people
and the way of wicked people it will perish
