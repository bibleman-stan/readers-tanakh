25:1
of David to you O Yahweh
my desire I lift up

25:2
O my God
in you I trust may not I be ashamed
may not they exult my enemies to me

25:3
also all those who wait for you not they will be ashamed
they will be ashamed
those who act treacherously in vain

25:4
your ways O Yahweh make known to me
your paths teach me

25:5
lead me in your truth and teach me
for you are the God of my salvation
you I have waited for
all the day

25:6
remember your compassion O Yahweh and covenant your loyalti
for are from long ago they

25:7
the sins of my youth and my transgressions
may not you remember
according to covenant your loyalty remember me you
on account of your goodness O Yahweh

25:8
is good and upright Yahweh
therefore he teaches sinners the way

25:9
he leads humble people in justice
so he may teach humble people his way

25:10
all the paths of Yahweh are covenant loyalty and faithfulness
to those who keep his covenant
and his testimonies

25:11
for the sake of your name O Yahweh
you will forgive my iniquity
for is great it

25:12
who? this is the person fearing Yahweh
he teaches him
the way which he will choose

25:13
his self in good it will remain
and his offspring
it will possess the land

25:14
the counsel of Yahweh belongs to those fearing him
and his covenant
to make known to them

25:15
my eyes continually are to Yahweh
for he will bring out from the net my feet

25:16
turn to me and show favor to me
for am solitary and afflicted I

25:17
the troubles of my heart they have wide made
from my distresses
deliver me

25:18
consider my affliction and my trouble
and forgive
to all my sins

25:19
consider my enemies for they have become many
and hatred of violence they have hated me

25:20
preserve ! my life and deliver me
may not I be put to shame
for I have taken refuge in you

25:21
integrity and uprightness may they preserve me
for
I have waited for you Yahweh

25:22
redeem O God Israel
from all
its troubles
