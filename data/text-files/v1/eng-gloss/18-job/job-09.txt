9:1
and answered Job
and he said

9:2
truly I know that thus
and what? will he be justified a person with God

9:3
if someone desires to conduct a case with him
not he will answer him
one time from a thousand

9:4
wise of heart and strong of power
who? has he shown stubbornness to him
and he has remained unharmed

9:5
the one who removes mountains and not they know
the one who he overturns them in his anger

9:6
the one who shakes the earth from its place
and its pillars
they tremble !

9:7
the one who speaks to the sun and not it shines
and behind the stars he puts a seal

9:8
one who stretches out the heavens to only him
and one who treads
on the high places of the sea

9:9
one who makes the Bear Orion and Pleiades
and the chambers of the south

9:10
one who great does things until there not is inquiry
and wonders
until there not is number

9:11
there! he will pass by at me and not I will see him
and he may pass on
and not I will perceive him

9:12
there! he will snatch away who? will he turn back him
who? will he say to him
what? are you doing

9:13
God not he will turn back his anger
under him they lay prostrate
the helpers of Rahab

9:14
indeed? for I I will answer him
I will choose my words with him

9:15
I who though I am righteous not I will answer
to my judge
I will seek favor

9:16
if I called and he answered me
not I believe
that he gave ear to my voice

9:17
he who with a storm he crushes me
and he increases my wounds without cause

9:18
not he permits me to bring back my breath
for he surfeits me
bitter things

9:19
if to power a strong one there!
and if to justice
who? will he summon me

9:20
though I am my own righteous mouth it will condemn as guilty me
am blameless I
and it declared guilty me

9:21
am blameless I not I know my self
I reject my life

9:22
is one
it therefore I say
the blameless and the wicked
he is bringing to an end

9:23
if a scourge it kills suddenly
the despair of innocent ones he mocks

9:24
a land it has been given in the hand of a wicked person
the face of its judges he covers
if not then who? is it

9:25
and my days they have been swift more than a runner
they have fled
not they have good seen

9:26
they have passed on with ships of reed
like an eagle
which it rushes on food

9:27
if to say I I will forget my complaint
I will let loose my face and I will be cheerful

9:28
I dread all my pains
I know
that not you will acquit me

9:29
I I will be guilty
why? this
vanity will I labor

9:30
if I washed myself in water of snow
and I cleansed
with potash my hands

9:31
then in pit you will dip me
and they will abhor me
my own clothes

9:32
for not he is a human like me I will answer him
we will come together
in judgment

9:33
not there between us is an arbiter
he may put his hand on the two of us

9:34
let him remove from on me his rod
and his dread
may not it terrify me

9:35
I will speak and not I will fear him
for not am thus I
with myself
