and repeated Job to take up his discourse
and he said

27:2
by the life of God who he has taken away my right
and the Almighty
who he has made bitter my self

27:3
for all still my breath is in me
and the spirit of God is in my nostrils

27:4
if they will speak my lips injustice
and my tongue
if it will utter deceit

27:5
far be it to me if I will right declare you
until I will expire
not I will put aside my integrity from me

27:6
on my righteousness I have kept hold and not I will let go it
not it reproaches my heart
any of my days

27:7
may he be like a wicked person my enemy
and one who raises himself against me like an evil-doer

for what? is the hope of a godless person if he will cut off
if he will take away God his life

¿ his cry will he hear God
if it will come on him distress

or? on the Almighty will he take delight
will he call on? God at every time

I will instruct you in the hand of God
that which is with the Almighty
not I will hide

there! you all of you have seen
and why? this
vanity do you act in vain

27:13
this is the portion of a wicked person with God
and the inheritance of ruthless people
which from the Almighty they will receive

if they will increase his children for the sword
and his descendants
not they will be satisfied bread

his survivors by death they will be buried
and his widows
not they will weep

if he will heap up like dust silver

he will prepare clothing

he will prepare it and a righteous person he will wear it
and the silver

he has built like moth his house

night
