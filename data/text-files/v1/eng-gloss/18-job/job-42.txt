42:1
and answered Job Yahweh
and he said

42:2
I know that everything you are able
and not it is withheld from you a purpose

42:3
who? this is concealing counsel
not
knowledge therefore I have told and not I understood
things too wonderful for me
and not I knew

42:4
listen please and I I will speak
I will ask you
and make known to me

42:5
to the hearing of the ear I had heard of you
and now
my eye it has seen you

42:6
therefore I reject and I repent
on dust and ashes

42:7
and it was
after he had spoken these Yahweh words to Job
and said Yahweh to Eliphaz the Temanite
it has burned my anger on you and on the two your friends
for not you have spoken concerning me a reliable thing like my servant Job

42:8
and therefore take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams and go to my servant Job
and you will offer up a burnt offering for yourselves
and Job my servant
he will pray on you
that except his face I will lift up
to not to do with you punishment for folly
for not you have spoken concerning me a reliable thing like my servant Job

42:9
and went Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite
Zophar the Naamathite
and they did
just as he had spoken to them Yahweh
and lifted up Yahweh the face of Job

42:10
and Yahweh
he turned back the captivity of Job
when prayed he for his friends
and increased Yahweh all that belonged to Job to double

42:11
and they came to him all his brothers and all his sisters and all those who knew him before
and they ate with him bread in his house and they showed sympathy to him and they comforted him
on all the trouble
which he had brought Yahweh on him
and they gave to him
each one a piece of money one
and each one a ring of gold one

42:12
and Yahweh
he blessed the latter part of Job more than his beginning
and it belonged to him four- teen thousand sheep
and six thousand camels
and one thousand pairs of oxen and a thousand female donkeys

42:13
and it belonged to him seven sons and three daughters

42:14
and called the name of the one Jemimah
and the name of the second Keziah
and the name of the third Keren- Happuch

42:15
and not it was found women beautiful like the daughters of Job in all the land
and he gave to them their father an inheritance in among their brothers

42:16
and lived Job after this
one hundred and forty years
and he saw
his children and the children of his children
four generations

42:17
and died Job
old and satisfied of days
