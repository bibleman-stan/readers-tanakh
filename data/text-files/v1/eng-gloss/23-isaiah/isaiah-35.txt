35:1
they will rejoice ! the wilderness
and the dry region
and let it be glad the desert plain so it may bud
like a crocus

35:2
completely it will bud and let it be glad also gladness and shouting for joy
the glory of Lebanon it will be given to it
the splendor of Carmel
and Sharon
they will see the glory of Yahweh
the splendor of our God

35:3
strengthen
hands slack
and knees stumbling
make firm

35:4
say to people hasty of heart
be strong
may not you be afraid
here! your God vengeance he will come
the recompense of God
he will come
and he may deliver you

35:5
then they will be opened
the eyes of blind people
and the ears of deaf people
they will be unstopped

35:6
then he will leap like deer a lame person
so it may shout for joy
the tongue of a dumb person
for they will burst forth in the wilderness waters
and torrents
in the desert plain

35:7
and will become the parched ground a pool
and thirsty ground
springs of water
in a habitation of jackals resting its place
herbage
reeds and papyrus

35:8
and it will be there a highway and a way and the way of holiness it will be called to it
not he will pass along it the unclean
and it will belong to them
one who walks a way and fools
not they will wander about

35:9
not it will be there a lion and a violent one of living creatures not it will go up on it
not it will be found
there
and they will walk
redeemed people

35:10
and the people ransomed of Yahweh they will return ! and they will come Zion with a shout of joy
and gladness of perpetuity
will be on their head
joy and gladness they will overtake them
and they will flee
sorrow and sighing
