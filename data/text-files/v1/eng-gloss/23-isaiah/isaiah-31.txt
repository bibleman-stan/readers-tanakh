31:1
woe to! those who go down Egypt for help
on horses
they depend
and they have relied on chariotry for it is many and on horsemen for they are numerous exceedingly
and not they look on the holy one of Israel
and Yahweh
not they seek

31:2
and also he is wise and he has brought calamity
and his words
not he has turned aside
and he will rise up on the house of evil-doers
and on the help of
those who do wickedness

31:3
and Egypt is humankind and not God
and their horses are flesh
and not spirit
and Yahweh he will stretch out his hand and he will stumble one who helps and he will fall one who is helped
and together
all of them they will come to an end !

31:4
for thus he has said Yahweh to me just as it growls the lion and the young lion over its prey that it is summoned on it a multitude of shepherds
from their voice not it is dismayed
and from their tumult
not it cringes
so he will come down Yahweh of hosts
to wage war on the mountain of Zion
and on its hill

31:5
like birds flying
so he will defend Yahweh of hosts
Jerusalem
he will defend it and he will rescue it
he will pass over it and he will deliver it

31:6
return to the one whom they have made deep rebellion
the people of Israel

31:7
for in that day
they will reject ! everyone the idols of his silver
and the idols of
his gold
which they have made for you your hands
sin

31:8
and will fall Assyria by a sword not a human
and a sword not a human
it will consume it
and it will flee itself from before a sword
and its young men
forced labor they will become

31:9
and its rock from terror it will pass away
and they will be dismayed from a standard
its commanders
the utterance of Yahweh who fire belongs to him in Zion
and an oven belongs to him
in Jerusalem
