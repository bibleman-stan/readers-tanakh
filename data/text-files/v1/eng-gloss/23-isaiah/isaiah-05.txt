5:1
let me sing please to my beloved one
a song of my beloved
of his vineyard
a vineyard it belonged to my beloved one
on a horn of a son of oil

5:2
and he dug it and he cleared of stones it and he planted it a choice vine
and he built a tower in the middle of it
and also a wine-vat
he dug out in it
and he waited to produce grapes
and it produced wild grapes

5:3
and therefore O inhabitants of Jerusalem
and everyone of Judah
judge please
between me
and between my vineyard

5:4
what? is to do still for my vineyard
and not I have done
for it
why? did I wait to produce grapes
and did it produce wild grapes

5:5
and therefore let me make know please you
that which I am about to do
to my vineyard
I will remove its hedge and it will become grazing
I will break down its wall
and it will become a trampling place

5:6
and I will make it a desolation not it will be pruned and not it will be hoed
and it will grow up thorns
and bushes
and to the clouds I will command
from raining on it
rain

5:7
for the vineyard of Yahweh of hosts is the house of Israel
and everyone of Judah
is the plantation of
his delight
and he waited for justice and there! bloodshed
for righteousness
and there! an outcry

5:8
woe to! those who make touch a house a house
a field on a field
they bring near
until there not is room
and you are made to dwell to alone you
in the midst of the land

5:9
in my ears
Yahweh of hosts
if not houses many a waste they will become
great houses and good houses
from not inhabitant

5:10
for ten acres of vineyard
they will produce
a bath one
and seed of a homer
it will produce an ephah

5:11
woe to! those who rise early in the morning
strong drink they pursue
those who delay in the twilight
wine
it inflames them

5:12
and it will be harp and lyre tambourine and flute and wine
their feast
and the deeds of Yahweh not they pay attention to
and the work of his hands
not they see

5:13
therefore it will go into exile my people
because not knowledge
and its honor will be men of hunger
and its multitude
will be parched of thirst

5:14
therefore it will enlarge Sheol its throat
and it will its open wide mouth
to not limit
and it will go down its splendor and its multitude and its uproar
and one exulting in it

5:15
and he has been humbled everyone
and he has low become everyone
and the eyes of proud people
they will low become

5:16
and was exalted Yahweh of hosts
by judgment
and holy God
will show holy himself
by righteousness

5:17
and they will graze lambs
like their own pasture
and the ruins of fatlings
sojourners they will eat

5:18
woe to! those who draw along iniquity
with the cords of emptiness
and like the rope of cart
sin

5:19
those who say let it hurry let it make haste ! his work
so that we may see
and it may draw near and let it come ! the plan of the holy one of Israel
so we may know

5:20
woe to! those who say of evil good
and of evil good
those who make darkness into light and light into darkness
those who make bitter into sweet
and sweet into bitter

5:21
woe to!
people wise in their own eyes
and before their own faces
discerning people

5:22
woe to!
mighty men
for drinking wine
and people of ability
for strong mixing drink

5:23
those who justify a wicked person
the consequence of a bribe
and the righteousness of righteous people
they take away from him

5:24
therefore as consumes stubble a tongue of fire and dry grass a flame it sinks down
their root like rottenness it will be
and their blossom
like dust it will go up
for they have rejected the law of Yahweh of hosts
and the word of the holy one of Israel
they have spurned

5:25
therefore it has burned the anger of Yahweh on his people and he has stretched out his hand on it and he has struck it and they have quaked the mountains
and it was their corpse like offal
in the middle of the streets
for this all not it has turned away his anger
and still
his hand is stretched out

5:26
and he will lift up a standard for the nations from a distance
and he will whistle for it
from the end of the earth
and there! quickly
swift it will come

5:27
there not is a weary one and there not is one who stumbles among it
not anyone will slumber
and not anyone will sleep
and not it has been opened the waistband of its loins
and not it has been torn apart
a thong of its sandals

5:28
which its arrows are sharpened
and all its bows
are bent
the hooves of its horses like flint they are regarded
and its wheels
like storm-wind

5:29
roaring of it
is like lion
it will roar like young lions so it may growl and it may seize prey
so it may carry it off
and there not is a deliverer

5:30
and it may growl over it in that day
like the growling of the sea
and someone will look to the land and there! darkness
distress and light
it has become dark
by its clouds
