49:1
listen O islands to me
and pay attention O peoples
from a distance
Yahweh from the womb he called me
from the inward parts of my mother
he has brought to remembrance my name

49:2
and he made my mouth like a sword sharp
in the shadow of his hand
he hid me
and he made me an arrow polished
in his quiver
he concealed me

49:3
and he said to me
are my servant you
Israel
whom by you
I will get glory for myself

49:4
and I said to emptiness I have labored
to nothingness and futility
my strength I have caused to fail
nevertheless my justice is with Yahweh
and my recompense
is with my God

49:5
and now he says Yahweh who formed me from the womb a servant of him
to bring back Jacob to him
and Israel
to him it will be gathered
and I may be honored in the eyes of Yahweh
and my God
he has been my strength

49:6
and he said it is too trifling for being you of me servant
to raise up the tribes of Jacob
and the preserved ones of Israel
to bring back
and I will make you a light of the nations
to be my salvation
to the end of the earth

49:7
thus he says Yahweh the redeemer of its holy Israel one to despise life to one who abhors a nation to a servant of rulers
kings they will see and they will stand up
princes
so they may bow down
on account of Yahweh who is faithful
the holy one of Israel
and he has chosen you

49:8
thus he says Yahweh in a time of favor I will answer you
and on a day of salvation
I will help you
and I will watch over you and I will make you a covenant of the people
to raise up the land
to give as inheritance
inheritances desolate

49:9
to say to the prisoners come out
to those who are in darkness
reveal yourselves
on roads they will graze
and will be on all bare heights
their pasture

49:10
not they will be hungry and not they will be thirsty
and not it will strike them burning heat
and sun
for one who has compassion on them he will guide them
and to springs of water
he will lead them

49:11
and I will make all my mountains
into road
and my highways
they will be lifted up !

49:12
here! these
from a distance
they will come
and here! these from the north and from the west
and these
from the land of Sinim

49:13
shout for joy O heaven and be glad O earth
and break forth O mountains
a shout of joy
for he has comforted Yahweh his people
and afflicted his people
he has compassion on

49:14
and said Zion
he has forsaken me Yahweh
and the Lord
he has forgotten me

49:15
¿ will she forget a woman sucking her child
from having compassion on
the child of her womb
these also they will forget
and I
not I will forget you

49:16
here! on palms of hands
I have inscribed you
your walls are before me
continually

49:17
they have made haste
your children
those who tear down you and those who make desolate you
from you they will go out

49:18
lift up all around your eyes and see
all of them
they have gathered together they have come to you
by the life of me the utterance of Yahweh that all of them like ornaments you will put on
and you will tie on them
like bride

49:19
for waste your places and desolate your places
and the land of
your destruction
for now you will be too cramped for the inhabitants
and they will be far away
those who swallowed up you

49:20
yet they will say in your ears
the children of
your bereavement
it is cramped for me the place
draw near ! for me so let me dwell

49:21
and you will say in your heart who? did he father for these me children
and I was bereaved
and barren
gone into exile and thrust away and these children who? did he bring up
here! I remained to alone me
these children
where? are they

49:22
thus he says the Lord Yahweh here! I will lift up to the nations my hand
and to peoples
I will raise my standard
and they will bring your sons in a bosom
and your daughters
on a shoulder they will be carried

49:23
and they will be kings foster-your fathers and their princesses suckling-your nurses
faces ground they will bow down to you
and the dust of your feet
they will lick up
and you will know that I am Yahweh
whom not they will be ashamed
those who wait for me

booty
and or? the captives of the righteous
will it escape

for thus he says Yahweh also a captive of a warrior he will be taken
and the booty of a ruthless person
it will be delivered
and your opponent I will oppose
and your children
I will save

49:26
and I will cause to eat those who maltreat you their own flesh
and like sweet wine
their own blood they will become drunk !
