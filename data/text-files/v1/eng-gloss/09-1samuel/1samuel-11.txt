11:1
and went up Nahash the Ammonite
and he encamped
on Jabesh Gilead
and they said all the men of Jabesh to Nahash
make to us a covenant
and we will serve you

11:2
and he said to them Nahash the Ammonite
by this I will make to you
by gouging out of you
every eye of the right side
and I will make it a reproach
on all Israel

11:3
and they said to him the elders of Jabesh refrain to us seven days
so let us send messengers
in all
the territory of Israel
and if there not is one who delivers us
and we will come out to you

11:4
and came the messengers Gibeah of Saul
and spoke the words
in the ears of the people
and they lifted up all the people their voice
and they wept

11:5
and there! Saul was coming after the cattle from the field
and said Saul
what? is to the people
that they are weeping
and they recounted to him
the words of
the men of Jabesh

11:6
and rushed the spirit of God on Saul
just as heard he
these words
and it burned his anger
exceedingly

11:7
and he took a pair of oxen and he cut in pieces it and he sent them in all the territory of Israel by the hand of the messengers saying
the one who not he is coming out after Saul and after Samuel
thus it will be done
to his oxen
and fell the dread of Yahweh on the people
and they came out
like a man one

11:8
and he mustered them
at Bezek
and were the people of Israel three hundred thousand
and the man of Judah
was thirty thousand

11:9
and they said to the messengers who had come thus you will say ! to the man of Jabesh Gilead
tomorrow it will belong to you deliverance
like the heat of the sun
and went the messengers and they told to the men of Jabesh
and they were glad

11:10
and said the men of Jabesh
tomorrow
will we go out to you
and you will do to us
according to all the good
in your view

11:11
and it was from the next day and he made Saul the people three companies
and they went in the midst of the camp in the watch of the morning
and they struck down Ammon
until the heat of the day
and it was those who remained and they were scattered
and not they remained among them
two together

11:12
and said the people to Samuel
who? is the one who said
Saul
will he reign? over us
deliver up the men
so we may put to death them

11:13
and said Saul
not he will be put to death anyone
on this day
for this day he has done Yahweh deliverance
in Israel

11:14
and said Samuel to the people
come
so let us go Gilgal
so we may renew there
the kingship

11:15
and they went all the people Gilgal and they made king there Saul before Yahweh in Gilgal
and they sacrificed there sacrifices peace offerings
before Yahweh
and he rejoiced there Saul and all the men of Israel
up to muchness
