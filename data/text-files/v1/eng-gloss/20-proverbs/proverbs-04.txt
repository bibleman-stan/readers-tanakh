4:1
listen to O children
the correction of a father
and be attentive
to know understanding

4:2
for good instruction
I give to you
my instruction
may not you neglect

4:3
if a son
I was of my father
tender and only child
before my mother

4:4
and he taught me
and he said to me
let it hold fast to my words your heart
keep my commandments and live

4:5
acquire wisdom
acquire understanding
may not you forget and may not you turn aside
from the utterances of my mouth

4:6
may not you her abandon and she will keep you
her love and she will guard you

4:7
is first wisdom
acquire wisdom
and by all your acquisition
acquire understanding

4:8
her esteem and she will exalt you
she will honor you
if you will her embrace

4:9
she will put to your head
a wreath of grace
a crown of beauty she will give you

4:10
listen O my son
and receive my words
so they may increase for you
years of life

4:11
the way of wisdom
I taught you
I led you
in tracks of uprightness

4:12
when walking you
not anyone will hinder your steps
and if you will run
not you will stumble

4:13
take hold on discipline may not you let go
her keep
for she is your life

4:14
in the path of wicked people
may not you go
and may not you advance
in the way of evil people

4:15
avoid it may not you pass along on it
turn aside from on it and pass on

4:16
for not they will sleep
if not they do harm
and it is taken away their sleep
if not they bring injury

4:17
for they eat
the bread of wickedness
and the wine of violence they drink

4:18
and the path of righteous people
is like a light of brightness
which goes and which shines
until the established part of the day

4:19
the way of wicked people
is like darkness
not they know
on what? are they stumbling

4:20
O my son
to my words be attentive !
to my utterances
incline your ear

4:21
do not let them depart from your eyes
keep them
in the midst of your heart

4:22
for are life they
to those who find them
and to all his flesh health

4:23
more than all guarding
guard your heart
for are from it
the sources of life

4:24
remove from yourself
crookedness of mouth
and deviousness of lips
put far away from yourself

4:25
your eyes
before let them look
and your eyelids
let them look straight before you

4:26
make level
the track of your foot
and all your ways let them be steadfast

4:27
may not you turn right aside and left
turn aside your foot from evil
