25:1
these also are the proverbs of Solomon
which they copied
the men of Hezekiah the king of Judah

25:2
is the glory of God to conceal a matter
and is the glory of kings
to search out a matter

25:3
heaven for height and earth for depth
and the heart of kings
there not is inquiry

25:4
remove dross from silver
and it came out for the metalsmith a vessel

25:5
remove the wicked before a king
so it may be established in righteousness his throne

25:6
may not you claim honor before a king
and in the place of great people
may not you stand

25:7
for is better to say to you
come up
here than to low set you before a noble person
whom they have seen your eyes

25:8
may not you go forth to contest a lawsuit
quickly
lest what? will you do at its end
when puts to shame you your neighbor

25:9
your own case conduct with your neighbor
and the secret of another person may not you reveal

25:10
lest he should bring shame you one who hears
and your evil report
not it will turn back

25:11
apples of gold in settings of silver
a word
spoken on proper its times

25:12
an earring of gold and an ornament of pure gold
one who reproves wise
on an ear listening

25:13
is like the cold of snow on a day of harvest
an envoy faithful to those who sent him
and the life of his masters he will restore

25:14
clouds and wind and is rain there not
a person who boasts
in a gift of falsehood

25:15
by length of anger he will be persuaded a ruler
and a tongue soft
it will break bone

25:16
honey you have found eat your sufficiency
lest you should be surfeited with it
and you will vomit up it

25:17
make rare your foot from the house of your neighbor
lest he should be surfeited with you
and he will hate you

25:18
a war-club and a sword and an arrow sharpened
a person who testifies against his neighbor
a witness of falsehood

25:19
a broken tooth and a foot slipped
the trust of one who acts treacherously
in a day of trouble

25:20
one who removes a garment on a day of coldness vinegar on soda
and one who sings the songs
to a heart sad

25:21
if is hungry one who hates you give to eat him food
and if thirsty
give to drink him water

25:22
for burning coals
you will snatch up on his head
and Yahweh
he will repay to you

25:23
a wind of the north it brings forth rain
and faces indignant
a tongue of secrecy

25:24
is good
to dwell on the corner of a roof
more than a woman of contentions
and a house of association

25:25
water cool on a person weary
and a good report
from a land of distance

25:26
a spring fouled and a fountain ruined
a righteous person
who sways before a wicked person

25:27
to eat honey much not is good
and the search of their own honor is an honor

25:28
a broken city into there not is a wall
a person
whom not control belongs to his spirit
