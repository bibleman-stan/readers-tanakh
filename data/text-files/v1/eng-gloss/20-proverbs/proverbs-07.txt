7:1
O my son keep my words
and my commandments
you will treasure with you

7:2
keep my commandments and live
and my instruction
like the pupil of your eyes

7:3
bind them on your fingers
write them
on the tablet of your heart

7:4
say to wisdom are my sister you
and a relative
to discernment you will call

7:5
to keep you from a woman strange
from a foreign woman
who her words she makes smooth

7:6
for at the window of my house
through my lattice I looked down

7:7
and I saw among the naive people
I observed among the sons
a youth lacking of heart

7:8
who was passing by in the street beside her corner
and the direction of her house he strode

7:9
at twilight in the evening of the day
in the middle of the night
and darkness

7:10
and there! a woman to meet him
the garment of a prostitute
and guarded of heart

7:11
is boisterous she and rebellious
in her house
not they stay her feet

7:12
a time in the street
a time in the open places
and beside every corner she lies in wait

7:13
and she takes hold on him and she kisses him
she has made firm her face
and she said to him

7:14
sacrifices of peace offerings are on me
this day
I have paid my vows

7:15
therefore I have come out to meet you
to seek your face
and I have found you

7:16
coverings I have spread my couch
colored fabrics
linen of Egypt

7:17
I have sprinkled my bed
myrrh aloes
and cinnamon

7:18
come ! let us our take fill of love until the morning
let us delight ourselves
with love

7:19
for not the husband is in his house
he has gone
on a journey from a distance

7:20
the bag of money he has taken in his hand
to the day of the full moon
he will come his house

7:21
she turns him with the greatness of her persuasiveness
with the seductiveness of her lips
she impels him

7:22
he is walking her behind
suddenly
like an ox which to the slaughter it goes
and like an anklet
to the chastening of a fool

7:23
until it will pierce an arrow his liver
as hurries a bird into a trap
and not he knows
that is for his life it

7:24
and therefore O children listen to me
and be attentive
to the words of my mouth

7:25
may not it turn aside to her ways your heart
may not it go astray
in her pathways

7:26
for many slain ones she has made fall
and are numerous
all killed her men

7:27
is the ways of Sheol her house
which go down
to the chambers of death
