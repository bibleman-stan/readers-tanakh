3:1
on my bed in the nights I sought the one whom it loves my self
I sought him
and I will not found him

3:2
I will arise please and I will go around in the city in the streets
and in the open places I will seek
the one whom it loves my self
I sought him
and I will not found him

3:3
they found me the watchmen who go around in the city the one whom it loves my self
have you seen?

3:4
like a little that I had passed away from them until that I found the one whom it loves my self
I grasped him and I will not let him go
until that I had brought him to the house of my mother
and to the room of the one who conceived me

3:5
I adjure you O daughters of Jerusalem by gazelles or
by the does of the field if you will awaken and if you will arouse love
until that it will please

3:6
who this is coming up from the wilderness
like columns of smoke she is perfumed of myrrh and frankincense from all the scent-powder of a trader

3:7
there! his bed which belongs to Solomon
sixty warriors
are around it some of the warriors of Israel

3:8
of them all are grasped of a sword
trained of warfare each one his sword is on his thigh from dread in the nights

3:9
a litter he has made for himself the king Solomon from wood of Lebanon

3:10
its posts he made silver back its gold seat its purple wool
its middle was fitted out leather from the daughters of Jerusalem

3:11
come out and look O daughters of Zion on the king Solomon
on the crown which she crowned him his mother on the day of his wedding
and on the day of the rejoicing of his heart
