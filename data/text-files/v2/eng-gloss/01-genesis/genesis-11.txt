and they said each to his neighbor give ! let us make bricks and let us burn them to burning
and became for them brick to stone
and bitumen became for them to mortar

11:4
and they said give ! let us build for ourselves a city and a tower and its top will be in the heavens
and we may make for ourselves a name lest we should be scattered over the surface of all the earth

11:5
and came down Yahweh
to see the city and the tower which they had built the children of humankind

11:6
and said Yahweh here! a people one and a language one belongs to all of them
and this
is beginning they to do
and now not it will be withheld from them all that they will plan to do

11:7
give ! let us go down
and let us confuse there their language that not they will hear each
the language of his neighbor

11:8
and scattered Yahweh them from there over the surface of all the earth
and they ceased to build the city

therefore someone called its name Babel for there he confused Yahweh
the language of all the earth and from there he scattered them Yahweh over the surface of all the earth
