4:1
and the man he knew Eve his wife
and she conceived and she bore Cain
and she said I have acquired a man with Yahweh

4:2
and she repeated to give birth to his brother Abel
and he was Abel a herdsman of sheep
and Cain he was one who tilled the ground

4:3
and it was from the end of days
and Cain brought some of the fruit of the ground an offering to Yahweh

4:4
and Abel he brought also he some of the firstborn(s) his flock and some of their fat(s)
and Yahweh looked to Abel
and to his offering

4:5
and to Cain
and to his offering not he looked
and it burned to Cain exceedingly
and they fell his face

4:6
and Yahweh said to Cain
why does it burn to you
and why have they fallen your face

4:7
not if you will do well lifting up
and if not you will do well to the door sin is crouching
and is to you its desire and you will rule over it

4:8
and Cain said to Abel his brother
and it was when were they and he rose up in the field Cain
and he killed him against Abel his brother


4:9
and Yahweh said to Cain where is Abel your brother
and he said not I know the keeper my brother am I

4:10
and he said what have you done the voice of the blood(s) your brother
are crying out to me from the ground

4:11
and now are cursed you from the ground which it has opened its mouth
to receive the blood(s) your brother from your hand

4:12
when you will till the ground not it will repeat to give its produce to you
a wanderer and a fugitive you will be on the earth

4:13
and Cain said to Yahweh is too great my punishment for bearing

4:14
here! you have driven away me this day from upon the surface of the ground and from before you
I will be hidden
and I will be a wanderer and a fugitive on the earth
and it will be every [one who] finds me
he will kill me

4:15
and Yahweh said to him therefore any one who kills Cain sevenfold he will be avenged
and Yahweh appointed for Cain a sign to not to strike him any [one who] found him

4:16
and Cain went out
from before Yahweh and he dwelt in the land of Nod the east of Eden

4:17
and Cain knew his wife
and she conceived
and Enoch bore
and he was building a city
and he called the name of the city according to the his name his son Enoch

4:18
and he was born to Enoch Irad
and Irad he fathered Mehujael
and Mehujael he fathered Methushael and Methushael
he fathered Lamech

4:19
and he took for himself Lamech two wives of the name of the one was Adah
and the name of the second
was Zillah

4:20
and she bore Adah Jabal
he was the father of dwellers of tent and livestock

4:21
and the his name his brother was Jubal
he was the father of every player of harp
and flute

4:22
and Zillah also she bore Tubal-Cain Tubal-Cain a sharpener
of every worker of bronze and iron
and was the sister of Tubal-Cain
Naamah

4:23
and Lamech said to his wives O Adah and Zillah listen to my voice
O wives of Lamech give ear to my speech
for a man I have killed for my bruise and a young man for my wound

4:24
if sevenfold
he will be avenged Cain and Lamech
seventy and seven

4:25
and Adam knew again his wife
and she bore a son
and she called his name Seth
for he has appointed for me God offspring another in place of Abel
because he killed him Cain

4:26
and to Seth also he was born a son and he called his name Enosh
then it was begun to call on the name of Yahweh
