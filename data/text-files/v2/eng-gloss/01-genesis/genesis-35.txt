35:1
and God said to Jacob
arise go up Bethel and dwell there and make there an altar
to the God who appeared to you when fled you from before Esau your brother

35:2
and Jacob said to his household
and to all who were with him remove the gods of (the) foreignness which are among you
and purify yourselves
and change your clothes

35:3
and let us arise and let us go up Bethel
and I will make there an altar to the God who answered me on the day my distress
and he has been with me on the journey which I have gone

35:4
and they gave to Jacob all the gods of (the) foreignness which were in their hand
and the rings which were in their ears
and he hid them Jacob under the terebinth which was near Shechem

35:5
and they set out
and it was the terror of God on the cities which were around them
and not people pursued after the sons of Jacob

and Jacob came to Luz which is in the land of Canaan
