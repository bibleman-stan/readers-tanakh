16:1
and Sarai the wife of Abram
not she had borne children to him
and belonged to her a maidservant Egyptian and her name was Hagar

16:2
and Sarai said to Abram
behold now he has restrained me Yahweh from bearing children
go please into my maidservant
perhaps I will be built up from her and he listened Abram to the voice of Sarai

16:3
and Sarai took the wife of Abram Hagar the Egyptian her maidservant
from the end of ten years of the dwelling of Abram in the land of Canaan and she gave her to Abram her husband to him to a wife

16:4
and he went into Hagar
and she conceived
and she saw that she had conceived
and she became insignificant her mistress in her eyes

16:5
and Sarai said to Abram my injustice
is on you I put my maidservant in your bosom
and she has seen that she had conceived
and I have become insignificant in her eyes
may he judge of Yahweh between me and between you

16:6
and Abram said to Sarai
there! your maidservant is in your hand do to her the good in your eyes
and she her afflicted Sarai
and she fled from before her

16:7
and the angel of Yahweh found her at the spring of water in the wilderness
at the spring on the way of Shur

16:8
and he said O Hagar the maidservant of Sarai
where from this have you come
and where are you going
and she said from before Sarai my mistress I am fleeing

16:9
and the angel of Yahweh said to her
return to your mistress
and humble yourself under her hands

16:10
and the angel of Yahweh said to her
greatly I will increase your offspring
and not it will be counted from multitude

16:11
and the angel of Yahweh said to her here you are pregnant and you will bear a son
and you will call his name Ishmael for Yahweh has listened to your affliction

16:12
and he will be a wild donkey of a man
his hand will be on everyone and the hand of everyone will be on him
and on the face of all his brothers he will dwell

16:13
and she called the name of Yahweh who spoke to her you are a God of seeing
for she said also here have I seen the back of the one who sees me

16:14
therefore someone called the well Beer Lahai Roi
there! it is between Kadesh
and between Bered

16:15
and Hagar bore to Abram a son
and Abram called the name of his son whom Hagar had borne Ishmael

16:16
and Abram
was a son of eighty years
