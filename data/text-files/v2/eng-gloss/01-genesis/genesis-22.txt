22:1
and it was after the things these
and God he put to the test Abraham
and he said to him O Abraham
and he said here I am

22:2
and he said
take please son of your only son whom you love of Isaac
and go for yourself to the land of Moriah
and offer him up there to a burnt offering on one of the mountains
which I will tell to you

22:3
and he rose early Abraham in the morning
and he saddled his donkey
and he took two his servants with him
and Isaac his son
and he cut wood of burnt offering
and he arose and he went to the place which God had said to him

22:4
on the day third and he lifted up Abraham his eyes
and he saw the place from a distance

22:5
and Abraham said to his servants
remain for yourselves here with the donkey and I and the lad
we will go to thus and we will bow down and we will return to you

22:6
and Abraham took the wood of the burnt offering
and he put it on Isaac his son
and he took in his hand the fire and the knife
and they went the two of them together

22:7
and Isaac said to Abraham his father
and he said my father
and he said here I am my son
and he said
here! the fire and the wood and where is the sheep for a burnt offering

22:8
and Abraham said
God he will see for himself the sheep for a burnt offering my son
and they went the two of them together

22:9
and they came to the place which God had said to him
and he built there Abraham the altar
and he arranged the wood
and he bound Isaac his son
and he put him on the altar from above the wood

22:10
and Abraham stretched out his hand
and he took the knife to slaughter his son

22:11
and the angel of Yahweh called to him from the heavens
and he said O Abraham Abraham
and he said here I am

22:12
and he said do not stretch out your hand against the lad
and do not do to him anything
for now I know that are one fearing God you
and you have not withheld son of your only son from me

22:13
and Abraham lifted up his eyes
and he saw and there! a ram
behind it was caught in the thicket by its horns
and Abraham went
and he took the ram
and he offered it up to a burnt offering in place of his son

22:14
and Abraham called the name of the place that
Yahweh he will see that it is said this day on the mountain of Yahweh
it will be seen

22:15
and the angel of Yahweh called to Abraham a second time from the heavens

22:16
and he said by myself I swear the utterance of Yahweh
that because that you have done this thing
and you have not withheld son of your only son

22:17
that surely I will bless you and greatly I will increase offspring of your like the stars of the heavens
and like the sand which is on the shore of the sea
and it will take possession of your offspring the gate of its his enemies

22:18
and they will bless themselves by your offspring
all the nations of the earth consequence that you have listened to my voice

22:19
and Abraham returned to his servants
and they arose and they went together
to Beer Sheba and he dwelt Abraham in Beer Sheba

22:20
and it was after the things these
and it was told to Abraham saying
there! she has borne Milcah also she children to Nahor your brother

22:21
Uz his firstborn
and Buz his brother and Kemuel
the father of Aram

22:22
and Kesed and Hazo and Pildash and Jidlaph
and Bethuel

22:23
and Bethuel he fathered Rebekah
eight sons these she bore Milcah to Nahor the brother of Abraham

22:24
and his concubine
and her name was Reumah
and she bore also she Tebah and Gaham and Tahash and Maacah
