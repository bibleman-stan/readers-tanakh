40:1
and Yahweh spoke to Moses to

40:2
on the day of the month first on day one of the month you will set up the tabernacle of the tent of meeting

40:3
and you will put there the ark of the testimony
and you will cover over the ark the curtain

40:4
and you will bring the table
and you will arrange its arrangement
and you will bring the lampstand
and you will lift up its lamps

40:5
and you will put the altar of (the) gold for incense before the ark of the testimony and you will put the screen of the entrance
of the tabernacle

40:6
and you will put the altar of the burnt offering before the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting

40:7
and you will put the laver between the tent of meeting
and between the altar and you will put there water

40:8
and you will put the courtyard all around and you will put the screen of the gate of the courtyard

40:9
and you will take the oil of (the) anointing and you will anoint the tabernacle and all that is in it
and you will consecrate it and all its articles
and it will be a holy thing

40:10
and you will anoint the altar of the burnt offering and all its articles
and you will consecrate the altar and it will be the altar holiness of holiness(es)

40:11
and you will anoint the laver and its base
and you will consecrate it

40:12
and you will bring near Aaron and his sons to the entrance of the tent of meeting and you will wash them with water

40:13
and you will clothe Aaron the garments of (the) holiness
and you will anoint him and you will consecrate him
and he will serve as a priest me

40:14
and his sons
you will bring near
and you will clothe them tunics

40:15
and you will anoint them just as you anointed their father
and they will serve as priests me
and it will become to be for them their anointing (into) a priesthood of perpetuity to their generations

40:16
and Moses did
according to all that he had commanded Yahweh him
so he did

40:17
and it was in the month first in the year second on day one of the month it was set up
the tabernacle

40:18
and Moses set up the tabernacle
and he put its bases
and he set up its frames
and he put its bars
and he set up its pillars

40:19
and he spread out the tent over the tabernacle
and he put the covering of the tent over it from above
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:20
and he took and he put the testimony into the ark
and he put the poles on the ark
and he put the atonement cover on the ark from above

40:21
and he brought the ark into the tabernacle
and he set up the curtain of the screen
and he covered over the ark of the testimony
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:22
and he put the table in the tent of meeting on the side of the tabernacle
northward from the outside of the curtain

40:23
and he arranged on it an arrangement of bread before Yahweh
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:24
and he put the lampstand in the tent of meeting opposite to
the table on the side of the tabernacle southward

40:25
and he lifted up the lamps before Yahweh
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:26
and he put the altar of (the) gold in the tent of meeting before the curtain

40:27
and he made smoke on it incense of perfume(s)
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:28
and he set up the screen of the entrance of the tabernacle

40:29
and the altar of the burnt offering
he set up the entrance of the tabernacle of the tent of meeting and he offered up on it the burnt offering and the grain offering
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:30
and he put the laver between the tent of meeting
and between the altar
and he put there water for washing

40:31
and they washed from it Moses
and Aaron and sons of his their hands and their feet

40:32
when went they into the tent of meeting
and when they drew near to the altar they washed
just as he had commanded Yahweh Moses

40:33
and he set up the courtyard around the tabernacle
and the altar and he put the screen

and it covered the cloud the tent of meeting
and the glory of Yahweh it filled the tabernacle

40:35
and not he was able Moses to go into the tent of meeting
because it had settled down over it the cloud
and the glory of Yahweh it had filled the tabernacle

and when was taken up the cloud from over the tabernacle they set out
the people of Israel in all out their settings

and if not it was taken up the cloud
