2:1
and he went a man of the house of Levi
and he took the daughter of Levi

2:2
and she conceived the woman
and she bore a son
and she saw him that was good he
and she hid him three months

2:3
and not she was able still to hide him
and she took for him a basket of papyrus
and she sealed it with bitumen and with pitch
and she put in it the child
and she put it among the rushes at the bank of the River

2:4
and she stood his sister from a distance to know what will it be done to him

2:5
and she went down the daughter of Pharaoh to bathe at the River and her female attendants were walking on the side of the River
and she saw the basket among the rushes
and she sent her maid
and she fetched it

2:6
and she opened it and she saw him the child
and there! a lad was crying and she had compassion on him and she said
is one of the children of the Hebrews this

2:7
and she said his sister to the daughter of Pharaoh
will I go and will I call for you a woman a wet nurse from the Hebrew women
so that she may suckle for you the child

2:8
and she said to her the daughter of Pharaoh go
and she went the young woman
and she called the mother of the child

2:9
and she said to her the daughter of Pharaoh take the child this and suckle him for me
and I will pay your wages
and the woman took the child
and she suckled him

2:10
and he grew up the child
and she took him to the daughter of Pharaoh
and he became for her (into) a son
and she called his name Moses
and she said because from the water I drew him

2:11
and it was in the days those and he grew up Moses
and he went out to his brothers
and he looked on their forced labor
and he saw a man Egyptian striking a man Hebrew one of his brothers

2:12
and he turned thus and thus
and he saw
that there was not anyone and he struck down the Egyptian
and he hid him in the sand

2:13
and he went out on the day second and there! two men of Hebrew were struggling together
and he said to the guilty one why are you striking your companion

2:14
and he said who did he appoint you to a man a chief and a judge over us to kill me are you saying
just as you killed the Egyptian
and he was afraid Moses and he thought
truly it is known the matter

2:15
and Pharaoh heard the matter this
and he sought
to kill Moses
and Moses fled from before Pharaoh
and he dwelt in the land of Midian
and he sat at the well

2:16
and belonged to (the) priest of Midian seven daughters
and they came and they drew and they filled the drinking troughs to water the flock their father

2:17
and they came the shepherds
and they drove them away
and Moses arose and he delivered them
and he watered their flock

2:18
and they went to Reuel their father
and he said why did you hurry to come this day

2:19
and they said
a man Egyptian he rescued us from the hand of the shepherds
and also actually (draw) he drew for us
and he watered the flock

2:20
and he said to his daughters
and where is he why this have you left the man
invite him so that he may eat food

2:21
and he was willing Moses
to dwell with the man and he gave Zipporah his daughter to Moses

2:22
and she bore a son
and he called his name Gershom
for he said
a sojourner I have become in a land foreign

2:23
and it was in the days many those and he died the king of Egypt
and they groaned the people of Israel from the labor
and they cried out
and it went up for help their cry to God from the labor

2:24
and God heard their groaning
and God remembered his covenant with Abraham with Isaac and with Jacob

2:25
and God saw the people of Israel
and God knew
