20:1
and God spoke all the words these
to
20:2
I am Yahweh your God who I brought you out from the land of Egypt
from a house of slaves

20:3
not it will belong to you gods other on my face

20:4
not you must make for yourself an image and any form which is in the heavens above
and which is on the earth beneath and which is in the waters from under to the earth

20:5
not you must bow down to them and not you must be enticed to serve them
for I Yahweh your God am a God jealous
who visits the iniquity of fathers on children on a third generation
and on a fourth generation of those who hate me

20:6
and who does covenant loyalty to thousands of those who love me
and of those who keep my commandments

20:7
not you must take the name of Yahweh your God for emptiness
for not he will leave unpunished Yahweh one who he will take his name for emptiness

20:8
remember the day of the sabbath to set it apart as holy

20:9
six days you will labor
and you will do all your work

20:10
and the day seventh is a sabbath
to Yahweh your God not you must do any work you
and your son and daughter of your servant your male and your female servant
and your livestock
and your sojourner who is in your gates

20:11
for six days he made Yahweh the heavens and the earth the sea and all that is in them
and he rested on the day seventh
therefore he blessed Yahweh the day of the sabbath
and he set it apart as holy

20:12
honor of your father and your mother
so that they may be long your days on the land
which Yahweh your God is about to give to you

20:13
not you must murder

20:14
not you must commit adultery

20:15
not you must steal

20:16
not you must testify against neighbor your testimony of falsehood

20:17
not you must covet the house of your neighbor not you must covet the wife of your neighbor
and his male servant and his female servant
and his ox
and his donkey
and all that belongs to your neighbor

20:18
and all the people were seeing the thunder claps and the lightning and the sound of the ram's horn
and the mountain smoking
and the people saw and they trembled
and they stood from a distance

20:19
and they said to Moses
speak you with us and we will listen
and not let him speak with us God
lest we should die

20:20
and Moses said to the people
do not be afraid for in order to put to the test you
God has come
and so that it may be his fear on your faces
to not you will sin

20:21
and the people stood from a distance
and Moses he drew near to the thick darkness where was there God

20:22
and Yahweh said to Moses
thus you will say to the people of Israel
you have seen
that from the heavens I have spoken with you

20:23
not you must make with me gods of silver and gods of gold
not you must make for yourselves

20:24
an altar of earth you will make for me
and you will sacrifice on it offerings your burnt and offerings your peace your sheep and your cattle
in every place where I will cause to be remembered my name
I will come to you and I will bless you

20:25
and if an altar of stones you will make for me
not you must build them hewn stone
if your tool you have wielded on it and you have profaned it

20:26
and not you must go up on steps to my altar that not it will be uncovered your nakedness on it
