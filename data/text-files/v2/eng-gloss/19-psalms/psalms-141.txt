141:1
a psalm of David O Yahweh I call out to you make haste to me
give ear to my voice
when call I to you

141:2
may it be established my prayer incense before you the raising of my palms an offering of evening

141:3
set O Yahweh a guard for my mouth
keep watch over the door of my lips

141:4
do not incline my heart to anything evil
to practice practices in wickedness
with people who do wickedness
and I will not eat in their delicacies

141:5
let him strike me a righteous person loyalty and let him rebuke me
oil of head not let it refuse my head yet still and my prayer is on their evil deeds

141:6
they have been thrown down at the hands of a rock their judges
and they will hear my words
that they are pleasant

141:7
like one who plows and one who breaks up the earth they have been scattered our bones to the mouth of Sheol

141:8
but are to you O Yahweh Lord my eyes
in you I take refuge
do not pour out my life

141:9
keep me
from the hands of the trap which they have laid for me
and the snares of those who do wickedness

141:10
let them fall in his own nets wicked people together I
until I will pass by
