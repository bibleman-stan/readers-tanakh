35:1
of David contend ! O Yahweh with my opponents
fight
with those who fight with me

35:2
take hold of shield and body shield
and arise !
my help

35:3
and draw out a spear and lance to meet my pursuers
say to my self
am your salvation I

35:4
may they be ashamed and they may be humiliated those who seek my life
may they be turned back backwards and may they be abashed
those who plot
my harm

35:5
may they be
like chaff before a wind
and the angel of Yahweh pushing

35:6
may it be their way
darkness and slippery places
and the angel of Yahweh
pursuing them

35:7
for without cause they have hidden for me the pit of their net
without cause
they have dug for my life

35:8
may it come to him destruction which not he knows
and his net which he hid may it catch him
in destruction
may he fall in it

35:9
and my self it will rejoice in Yahweh
it will exult
in his salvation

35:10
all my bones they will say O Yahweh
who? is like you
one who delivers a poor person from a person strong too for him
and the poor and the needy
from one who robs him

35:11
they rise up ! witnesses of violence
that which not I know
they ask me

35:12
they repay me harm in place of good
bereavement belongs to my self

35:13
and I when were sick they my clothing was sackcloth
I humbled with fasting my self
and my prayer
to my bosom it returned

35:14
like a friend like a brother of me I went about
like a mourner of a mother
mourning I bowed

35:15
and at my stumbling they rejoiced and they gathered
they gathered on me smitten people and not I knew
they tore and not they were still

35:16
godless ones of mockers of cake
they toward gnashed me their teeth

35:17
O Lord long how? will you look
bring back ! my life from their ravages
from young lions
only my life

35:18
I will give thanks to you in the great assembly
among a people numerous I will praise you

35:19
may not they rejoice to me my enemies falsehood
those who hate me without cause
let them wink an eye

35:20
for not peace
they speak
and on the quiet people of the land
words of deceit
they plan !

35:21
and they have wide opened on me
their mouth
they have said aha! aha!
it has seen our eyes

35:22
you have seen O Yahweh may not you be silent
O Lord
may not you be distant from me

35:23
wake up ! and awake ! for my judgment
O my God and O Lord for my cause

35:24
vindicate me according to your righteousness O Yahweh my God
and may not they rejoice to me

35:25
may not they say in their heart aha! our desire
may not they say
we have swallowed him

35:26
may they be ashamed and they may be abashed altogether the people joyful of my distress
may they be clothed shame and ignominy
who magnify themselves on me

35:27
may they shout for joy and they may rejoice the people desirous of my righteousness
and may they say continually may he be great Yahweh
desiring
the welfare of his servant

35:28
and my tongue it will utter your righteousness
all the day
your praise
