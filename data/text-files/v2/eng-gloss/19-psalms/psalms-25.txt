25:1
of David to you O Yahweh
my desire I lift up

25:2
my God
in you I trust not let me be ashamed
not may they exult my enemies to me

25:3
also all [those who] wait for you not they will be ashamed
they will be ashamed
[those who] act treacherously without cause

25:4
your ways O Yahweh make known to me
paths of your teach me

25:5
lead me in your truth and teach me
for you are the my God my salvation
you I have waited for all the day

25:6
remember your compassion(s) O Yahweh and your covenant loyalti(es)
for are from long ago they

25:7
the sins my youth(s) and my transgressions
do not remember
according to your covenant loyalty remember me you
on account of your goodness O Yahweh

25:8
is good and upright Yahweh
therefore he teaches sinners the way

25:9
he leads humble people in justice
and he teaches humble people of his way

25:10
all the paths of Yahweh are covenant loyalty and faithfulness
to those who keep covenant (of) his and his testimonies

25:11
for the sake of your name O Yahweh
(and) you will forgive my iniquity
for is great it

25:12
who this is the person fearing (of) Yahweh
he teaches him
(in) the way which he will choose

25:13
his self in good it will remain and his offspring
it will possess the land

25:14
the counsel of Yahweh belongs to those fearing him and his covenant to make known to them

25:15
eyes of my continually are to Yahweh
for he will bring out from the net my feet

25:16
turn to me and show favor to me
for am solitary and afflicted I

25:17
the distresses my heart they have made wide from my troubles
deliver me

25:18
consider my affliction and my trouble
and forgive to all my sins

25:19
consider my enemies that they have become many
and hatred of violence they have hated me

25:20
preserve my life and deliver me not let me be put to shame
for I have taken refuge in you

25:21
integrity and uprightness may they preserve me for
I have waited for you

25:22
redeem O God Israel from all its troubles
