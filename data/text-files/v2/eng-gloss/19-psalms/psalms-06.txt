6:1
to the choirmaster with stringed instruments on the sheminith
a psalm of David

6:2
O Yahweh
do not in anger of your rebuke me
and do not in wrath of your discipline me

6:3
show favor to me O Yahweh for am frail
I heal me O Yahweh
for they are disturbed my bones

6:4
and my self it is disturbed exceedingly

6:5
return O Yahweh rescue my life
save me
on account of loyalty your covenant

6:6
for there is not in death your remembrance
in Sheol
who will he give thanks to you

6:7
I have become weary by my groaning
I make swim in every night of my bed
with my tears
my couch I melt

6:8
it has wasted away from grief of my eye
it has grown weak
with all my foes

6:9
turn away from me O all those who do (of) wickedness
for he has heard Yahweh
the sound my weeping

6:10
he has heard Yahweh my supplication
Yahweh
my prayer he will accept

6:11
they will be ashamed and they will be disturbed exceedingly all my enemies
they will turn back
they will be ashamed a moment
