3:1
a psalm of David when fled he from before Absalom his son

3:2
O Yahweh how! they are many my foes many people are rising up on me

3:3
many people are saying of self my there is not deliverance for him in God Selah

3:4
and you O Yahweh are a shield behind me my honor
and the one who lifts up my head

3:5
my voice to Yahweh I called out
and he answered me from the mountain his holiness Selah

3:6
I lay down
and I slept
I awoke
because Yahweh he sustains me

3:7
not I will be afraid from ten thousands of people who all around
they have taken a stand on me

3:8
arise O Yahweh save me my God
for you have struck all enemies my jaw
the teeth of wicked people you have broken

3:9
belongs to Yahweh deliverance is towards people of your blessing Selah
