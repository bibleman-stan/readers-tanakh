24:1
of David a psalm
belongs to Yahweh the earth and what fills it the world
and those who dwell in it

24:2
for he on the seas he founded it
and on the rivers he established it

24:3
who will he go up on the mountain of Yahweh
and who will he stand in the his place his holiness

24:4
a person innocent of palms and a person pure of heart who he has not lifted up to falsehood my desire
and he has not sworn an oath to deceit

24:5
he will lift up blessing from with Yahweh and righteousness from the God of his salvation

24:6
this is the generation

24:7
lift up O gates of your heads
and be lifted up O doors of antiquity
so that he may come the king of glory

24:8
who this is the king of glory
Yahweh strong and mighty Yahweh
mighty of battle

24:9
lift up O gates of your heads
and lift up O doors of antiquity
so that he may come the king of glory

24:10
who that this is the king of glory
Yahweh of hosts
