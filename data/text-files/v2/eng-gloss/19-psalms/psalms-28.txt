28:1
of David to you O Yahweh I call out
O my rock may not you be deaf from me
lest you should be silent from me
and I will be equal
with those who go down the pit

28:2
hear the sound of my supplications when cry for help I to you
when lift up I my hands
to the innermost room of your holiness

28:3
may not you drag away me with wicked people and with those who do
wickedness who speak peace with their neighbors
and evil
is in their heart

28:4
requite to them according to their work and according to the wickedness of their deeds
according to the work of their hands requite to them
repay their recompense to them

28:5
for not they consider to the deeds of Yahweh and to the work of his hands
he will tear down them
and not he will rebuild them

28:6
be blessed Yahweh
for he has heard
the sound of my supplications

28:7
Yahweh is my strength and my shield in him it has trusted my heart
and I was helped
and it exulted my heart
and from my song I will give thanks to him

28:8
Yahweh is strength of them
and is the refuge of the salvation of his anointed he

28:9
save ! your people
and bless your inheritance
and shepherd them and carry them
until perpetuity
