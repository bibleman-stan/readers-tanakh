100:1
a psalm for thanksgiving
shout for joy to Yahweh
O all the earth

100:2
serve obj. Yahweh with joy
come before him
with cries of joy

100:3
know
that Yahweh he is God
he made us and belong to him we
his people
and the flock of his pasture

100:4
go his gates with thanksgiving
his courts with praise
give thanks to him
bless his name

100:5
for is good Yahweh is for ever covenant of his loyalty
and is to a generation and a generation
his faithfulness
