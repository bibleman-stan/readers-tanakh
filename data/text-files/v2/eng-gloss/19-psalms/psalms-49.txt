49:1
to the choirmaster of the sons of Korah a psalm

49:2
hear this O all the peoples
give ear
O all the inhabitants of the world

49:3
both the children of humankind as well as the children of man
alike
the rich and the needy

49:4
my mouth it will speak wisdom(s)
and the meditation my my heart will be understanding(s)

49:5
I will incline to a proverb my ear
I will open with a harp
my riddle

49:6
why will I fear in days of trouble
the iniquity my my heels it surrounds me

49:7
[those who] rely on their wealth
and in the greatness their their riches
they boast

49:8
a brother
not certainly (ransom) he will ransom anyone
not he will give to God his ransom

49:9
and it is precious the ransom their their life
and he will cease forever

49:10
and he will live still to perpetuity
not he will see the pit

49:11
for anyone will see wise people they die
alike a fool and a stupid person they perish
and they leave to other people of their wealth

49:12
being their inner houses are their forever
places their dwelling to a generation and a generation
they called with their names
on lands

49:13
and humankind in honor not he remains
he is like like the animals which they are destroyed

49:14
this way is their stupidity belongs to them
and after them in their mouth they take delight Selah

49:15
like sheep for Sheol they have been appointed death it will shepherd them
and they ruled over them upright people to the morning



49:16
surely God
he will redeem my life from the hand of Sheol
for he will take me Selah

49:17
do not fear when he will become rich anyone
when it will increase
the honor his his house

49:18
for not in his death he will take anything
not it will go down after him his honor

49:19
though his self in his life he blessed
and people praise you
when you will do well to yourself

49:20
it will go to the generation his his ancestors
until perpetuity
not they will see light

49:21
humankind with honor and who not he understands
he is like like the animals which they are destroyed
