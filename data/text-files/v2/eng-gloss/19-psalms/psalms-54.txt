to the choirmaster with stringed instruments a poem of David

54:2
when came the Ziphites and they said to Saul not is David hiding himself with us

54:3
O God by your name save me
and by your strength you will vindicate me

54:4
O God hear my prayer
give ear to the words of my mouth

54:5
for strangers they have risen up on me
and ruthless people they have sought my life
not they have set God to before themselves Selah

54:6
here! God is a my helper
Lord the is those who sustain my life
