83:1
a song a psalm of Asaph

83:2
O God let not rest belong to you
do not be silent and do not be inactive O God

83:3
for here! your enemies they are in an uproar
and [those who] hate you
they have lifted a head

83:4
on your people they make crafty counsel
and they consult together
on your treasured ones

83:5
they have said
come and let us annihilate them from a nation
and not it will be remembered the name of Israel again

83:6
for they have consulted heart together
on you
a covenant they are making

83:7
the tents of Edom and the Ishmaelites
Moab and the Hagrites

83:8
Gebal and Ammon and Amalek
Philistia
with the inhabitants of Tyre

83:9
also Assyria it has joined itself with them
they have become an arm for the children of Lot Selah

83:10
do to them like Midian
like Sisera like Jabin
at the wadi of Kishon

83:11
they were destroyed at Endor
they became dung
for the ground

83:12
make them their noble people like Oreb and like Zeeb
and like Zebah and like Zalmunna
all their princes

83:13
who they said let us take possession of for ourselves
the pastures of God

83:14
my God
make them like whirling dust
like chaff
before a wind

83:15
like a fire which it burns a forest
and like a flame
which it sets ablaze mountains

83:16
so you will pursue them with your storm
and with your storm-wind you will terrify them

83:17
fill faces of their shame
so that they may seek your name O Yahweh

83:18
may they be ashamed and may they be dismayed until perpetuity
and may they be abashed and may they perish

83:19
and may they know
that you name who your is Yahweh you alone
are the Most High
over all the earth
