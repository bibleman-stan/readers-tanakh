137:1
at the rivers of Babylon
there we sat also we wept when remembered we Zion

137:2
on poplars in the midst of it we hung up our harps

137:3
for there they asked us captors our words of a song and mockers our gladness
sing for us one of the songs of Zion

137:4
how will we sing the song of Yahweh on ground of foreignness

137:5
if I will forget you O Jerusalem
may it forget my hand right

137:6
may it cleave my tongue to my palate if not I will remember you
if not I will lift up Jerusalem above the chief of my joy

137:7
remember O Yahweh to the people of Edom the day of Jerusalem
who were saying lay bare lay bare to the foundation in it

137:8
O daughter of Babylon which is about to be destroyed
how blessed! is the one who he will repay to you
your dealing
which you dealt to us

137:9
how blessed! is the one who he will seize and he will smash your children to the rock
