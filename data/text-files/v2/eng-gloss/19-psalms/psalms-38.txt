38:1


38:2
A Psalm of David, to bring to remembrance. O LORD,
rebuke me not in thy wrath: neither chasten me in thy hot displeasure.

38:3
For thine arrows stick fast
in me, and thy hand presseth me sore.

38:4
There is no soundness in my flesh because of thine anger;
neither is there any rest in my bones because of my sin.

38:5
For mine iniquities are gone over mine head:
as an heavy burden they are too heavy for me.

38:6
My wounds stink and are corrupt because of my foolishness.

38:7
I am troubled; I am bowed down greatly; all the day long.
I go mourning

38:8
For my loins are filled with a loathsome
disease: and there is no soundness in my flesh.

38:9
I am feeble and sore broken:
I have roared by reason of the disquietness of my heart.

38:10
Lord, all my desire is before thee; and my groaning
is not hid from thee.

38:11
My heart panteth, my strength faileth me: as for the light of mine eyes, it also is gone from me.

38:12
My lovers and my friends
stand aloof from my sore; and my kinsmen stand afar off.

38:13
They also that seek after my life lay snares
for me: and they that seek my hurt speak mischievous things, deceits
and imagine all the day long.

38:14
But I, as a deaf man, heard
not; and I was as a dumb man that openeth not his mouth.

38:15
Thus I was as a man that heareth
not, and in whose mouth are no reproofs.

38:16
For in thee, O LORD, do I hope:
thou wilt hear,
O Lord my God.

38:17
For I said, Hear me, lest otherwise they should rejoice over me: when my foot slippeth,
they magnify themselves against me.

38:18
For I am ready to halt,
and my sorrow is continually before me.

38:19
For I will declare mine iniquity;
I will be sorry for my sin.

38:20
But mine enemies are lively, and they are strong:
and they that hate me wrongfully are multiplied.

38:21
They also that render evil for good are mine adversaries;
because I follow the thing that good is.

38:22
Forsake me not, O LORD: O my God, be not far from me.

38:23
Make haste to help me, O Lord my salvation.
