80:1
to the choirmaster to Shoshannim
eduth of Asaph a psalm

80:2
O shepherd of Israel give ear !
O you who lead like flock Joseph
O you who sit the cherubim shine forth !

80:3
before Ephraim and Benjamin and Manasseh
stir up ! your might
and come ! for salvation of us

80:4
O God restore us
and make shine your face
so let us be delivered

80:5
O Yahweh God of hosts
until when? have you smoked
at the prayer of your people

80:6
you have fed them bread of tears
and you have made drink them
tears a third of a measure

80:7
you make us a contention to our neighbors
and our enemies
they mock themselves

80:8
O God of hosts restore us
and make shine your face
so let us be delivered

80:9
a vine from Egypt you uprooted
you drove out nations
and you planted it

80:10
you made clear before it
and it took root its roots
and filled the land

80:11
they were covered mountains of its shade
and its branches
the cedars of God

80:12
it stretched out its branches to the sea
and to the river
its young shoots

80:13
why? have you broken down its walls
and they have plucked it
all those who pass by of the road

80:14
it eats away it wild boar from the forest
and moving creatures of the field it grazes on it

80:15
O God of hosts return please
pay attention from heaven and see
and attend to
this vine

80:16
and the root which it your right planted hand
and on the son
whom you strong made for yourself

80:17
it is burned with fire it is cut down
from the rebuke of your face they will perish

80:18
may it be your hand on the man of your right hand
on the son of humankind
whom you have strong made for yourself

80:19
and not we will turn back from you
you will give life us
and on your name we will call

80:20
O Yahweh God of hosts restore us
make shine your face
so let us be delivered
