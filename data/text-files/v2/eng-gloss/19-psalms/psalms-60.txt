60:2
when struggled he with Aram Naharaim and with Aram Zobah
and he turned back Joab
and he struck down Edom in the Valley of Salt
two plus ten thousand

60:3
O God you have rejected us you have broken out against us
you have been angry
you will restore us

60:4
you have made quake the land you have split it open
repair its fractures for it is shaking

60:5
you have made see your people a hard thing
you have made us drink wine of staggering

60:6
you have set up for those fearing you a standard to be displayed
because of the bow Selah

60:7
so that they may be rescued your beloved ones
save hand of your right

60:8
God he has spoken in his sanctuary I will exult
I will divide up Shechem
and the Valley of Succoth I will measure off

60:9
belongs to me Gilead and belongs to me Manasseh
and Ephraim is the protection my head Judah is my commander's staff

60:10
Moab is the pot my washing
over Edom I throw my sandal on me
O Philistia shout

60:11
who will he bring me a city of fortification
who has he led me to Edom

60:12
not you O God have you rejected us
and not you go out O God with our armies

60:13
give to us help from the foe and is worthlessness
the deliverance of humankind

60:14
