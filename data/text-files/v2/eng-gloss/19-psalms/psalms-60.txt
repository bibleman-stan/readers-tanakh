60:1


60:2





60:3
O God, thou hast cast us off, thou hast scattered
us, thou hast been displeased;
To the chief Musician upon Shushaneduth, Michtam of David, to teach; when he strove with Aramnaharaim and with Aramzobah, when Joab returned, and smote of Edom in the valley of salt twelve thousand. O turn thyself to us again.

60:4
Thou hast made the earth to tremble; thou hast broken
it: heal the breaches thereof; for it shaketh.

60:5
Thou hast shewed thy people hard things:
thou hast made us to drink the wine of astonishment.

60:6
Thou hast given a banner to them that fear
thee, that it may be displayed because of the truth. Selah.

60:7
That thy beloved may be delivered; save with thy right hand, and hear me.

60:8
God hath spoken in his holiness; I will rejoice, I will divide Shechem,
and mete out the valley of Succoth.

60:9
Gilead
is mine, and Manasseh
is mine; Ephraim also is the strength of mine head; Judah is my lawgiver;

60:10
Moab is my washpot;
over Edom will I cast out my shoe:
Philistia, triumph thou because of me.

60:11
Who will bring me into the strong city?
who will lead me into Edom?

60:12
Wilt not thou, O God, which hadst cast us off?
and thou, O God, which didst not go out with our armies?

60:13
Give us help from trouble: for vain
is the help of man.

60:14
Through God we shall do valiantly:
for he it is that shall tread down our enemies.
