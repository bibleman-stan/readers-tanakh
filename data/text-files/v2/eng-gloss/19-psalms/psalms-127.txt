127:1
the song of the ascents of Solomon
if Yahweh he will not build a house
vanity they have toiled its builders in it if Yahweh he will not watch over a city
vanity he has kept awake the watchman

127:2
vanity belongs to you who rise early to rise who delay to sit
who eat the bread of hard labors
thus he gives to his beloved one sleep

127:3
here! the inheritance of Yahweh is sons is a reward
the fruit of the womb

127:4
like arrows in the hand of a warrior so are the sons of youth

127:5
how blessed! is the man who he has filled his quiver from them
they will not be ashamed
when they will speak with enemies in the gate
