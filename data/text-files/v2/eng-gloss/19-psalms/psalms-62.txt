62:1
to the choirmaster on Jeduthun a psalm of David

62:2
only to God is silence being my is from him my salvation

62:3
only he rock is my and my salvation my refuge
not I will be shaken much

62:4
until when will you attack on a person you will be murdered of you all
like a wall bent down a wall pushed down

62:5
surely from his loftiness they have planned to thrust him down they are pleased with falsehood
with his mouth they bless and in their inner being they curse of Selah

62:6
only to God be silent my self for is from him my hope

62:7
only he rock is my and my salvation my refuge
not I will be shaken

62:8
is on God my salvation and my honor
the my rock my strength my refuge is in God

62:9
trust in him at every time O people
pour out before him your heart
God is a our refuge Selah

62:10
only are a breath the children of humankind are falsehood the children of everyone in the balances to go up they are more than a breath together

62:11
do not trust in extortion
and in robbery do not put vain hope
wealth if it will bear fruit
do not set heart

62:12
one time he has spoken God
two times this I have heard that strength belongs to God

62:13
and belongs to you Lord my covenant loyalty
for you repay to a person according to his work
