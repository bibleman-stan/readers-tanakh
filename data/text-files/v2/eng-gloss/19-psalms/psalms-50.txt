50:1
a psalm of Asaph God Yahweh
he has spoken and he has summoned the earth from the rising of the sun to its setting

50:2
from Zion perfection of beauty God he has shone forth

50:3
he comes God our
and not let him be silent
fire before him it consumes and around him
it is tempestuous exceedingly

50:4
he summons to the heavens above
and the earth to judge of his people

50:5
gather to me O my faithful people those who made my covenant with sacrifice

50:6
and they declared the heavens of his righteousness
for God is judge he Selah

50:7
hear my people and I will speak O Israel and I will warn you
am God your God I

50:8
not on your sacrifices I rebuke you
and your burnt offerings are to before me continually

50:9
not I will accept from your household a young bull from folds your goats

50:10
for belong to me every creature living of the forest the animals on hills of a thousand

50:11
I know every bird of the mountains
and moving creatures of the field are with me

50:12
if I will be hungry not I will tell to you for belong to me the world and what fills it

50:13
do I eat the flesh of mighty bulls
and the blood of goats do I drink?

50:14
sacrifice to God a thank-offering
and pay to the High Most your vows

50:15
and call out to me in a day of trouble
I will rescue you
and you will honor me

50:16
and to the wicked he says God
what is to you to recount my decrees
and you have taken my covenant on your mouth

50:17
and you hate discipline
and you have thrown my words behind you

50:18
if you saw a thief and you were pleased with him
and was with adulterers your portion

50:19
your mouth you let loose in evil
and your tongue it frames deceit

50:20
you sit on your brother you speak on the child of your mother you give a fault

50:21
these things you have done and I kept silent
you imagined
exactly I am like you
I will rebuke you and I will arrange to your eyes

50:22
consider please this O you who forget God lest I should tear to pieces
and there will not be a deliverer

50:23
one who sacrifices a thank-offering he honors me
and one who sets a way I will let him look on the salvation of God
