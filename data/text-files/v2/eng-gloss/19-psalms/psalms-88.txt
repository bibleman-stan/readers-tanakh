88:1
a song a psalm
of the sons of Korah to the choirmaster on machalath- leannoth a poem of Heman the Ezrachite

88:2
O Yahweh the God of my salvation
day I have cried out in the night before you

88:3
may it come before you my prayer
incline your ear to my cry of entreaty

88:4
for it is surfeited with troubles being my and my life to Sheol they have approached

88:5
I am reckoned with those who go down of the pit
I am like a man who there is not strength

88:6
among the dead people set free like those slain who lie of the grave
whom not you remember them again and they from your hand they are cut off

88:7
you have put me in a pit of lowest parts in the dark places in the depths

88:8
on me it has lain your anger
and all your breakers you have afflicted me Selah

88:9
you have removed far away my acquaintances from me
you have made me abominations to them I am shut up
and not I will go out

88:10
my eye it has become faint
from affliction I have called out to you O Yahweh on every day
I have spread out to you my palms

88:11
for the dead people will you do wonders or the shades
will they arise will they give thanks to you Selah

88:12
will it be recounted in the grave loyalty of your covenant your faithfulness in Abaddon

88:13
will it be known in the darkness of your wonders
and your righteousness in the land of forgetting

88:14
and I to you O Yahweh I cry for help and in the morning
my prayer it comes to meet you

88:15
why O Yahweh do you reject my self
do you hide your face from me

88:16
have been afflicted I and about to die from youth
I have borne your terrors I am perplexed

88:17
over me they have passed anger of your terrors
they have destroyed me

88:18
they have surrounded me like water all the day
they have surrounded me altogether

88:19
you have removed far away from me one who loves and a friend my acquaintances is a dark place
