64:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

64:2
hear O my God voice in my complaint
from dread of an enemy
you will preserve my life

64:3
you will hide me from the secret counsel of evil-doers
from the unrest
of those who do (of) wickedness

64:4
who they sharpen like sword their tongue
they bend their arrow
a word bitter

64:5
to shoot in (the) secret places the blameless
suddenly they shoot at him
and not they are afraid

64:6
they strengthen (for themselves a matter evil
they speak to hide snares
they say
who will he see them

64:7
they plot injustice(s)
we have finished a plot plotted
and the inner being of a person
and the heart is deep

64:8
and he shot at them
God
an arrow suddenly
they will be
their wounds

64:9
and they caused him to stumble over them their tongue
they will shudder
every one who looks on them

64:10
and they were afraid
all humankind
and they declared the deeds of God
and his work they will consider

64:11
let him rejoice the righteous in Yahweh and he will take refuge in him
and let them boast
all people upright of heart
