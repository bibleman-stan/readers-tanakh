52:1
to the choirmaster
a poem of David

52:2
when came Doeg the Edomite and he told to Saul
and he said to him
he has gone David
to the house of Ahimelech

52:3
why do you boast in evil O mighty man
the covenant loyalty of God
is all the day

52:4
destruction(s) it plots your tongue
like a razor sharpened
O you who do (of) deceit

52:5
you love evil more than good
falsehood more than speaking righteousness of Selah

52:6
you love all words of swallowing
a tongue of deceit

52:7
also God he will tear you down to perpetuity
he will snatch you away and he will tear you away from a tent
and he will root you up from the land of living people Selah

52:8
and they will see righteous people and they will fear
and on him they will laugh

52:9
there! the man
who not he made God
his refuge
and he trusted in the greatness his his riches
he sought safety
in his destruction

52:10
and I am like an olive tree luxuriant in the house of God
I trust in the covenant loyalty of God
forever and ever

52:11
I will give thanks to you forever because you have acted
and I will wait for your name for it is good
before your faithful people
