11:1
to the choirmaster of David
in Yahweh I have taken refuge
how will you say to my self

11:2
for there! the wicked they bend a bow
they have prepared their arrow on the bowstring
to shoot in gloom to people upright of heart

11:3
when the foundations they will be torn down the righteous
what does he do

11:4
Yahweh is in the temple his holiness
Yahweh is in the heavens his throne
his eyes they see
his eyelids they test the children of humankind

11:5
Yahweh the righteous he tests
and the wicked and one who loves (of) violence
it hates his self

11:6
he will rain down on wicked people snares
will be fire and sulfur and a wind of burning heat(s)
the portion their cup

11:7
for is righteous Yahweh righteousness(es) he loves the upright
they will see his face
