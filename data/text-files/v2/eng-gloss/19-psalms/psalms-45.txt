45:1
to the choirmaster on Shoshannim of the sons of Korah a poem
a song of love

45:2
it is aroused my heart a word good
I am speaking my work to the king my tongue
is a stylus of a scribe skilled

45:3
you are handsome more than the sons of humankind
it has been poured grace on your lips
therefore he has blessed you God forever

45:4
gird your sword on a thigh O mighty one your splendor
and your majesty

45:5
and majesty of your prosper ride forth on a matter of truth and humility righteousness
and let it teach you awesome deeds hand of your right

45:6
your arrows are sharpened
peoples under you they will fall
in the heart of the enemies of the king

45:7
your throne O God is forever and ever
is a scepter of uprightness
the scepter of your kingdom

45:8
you have loved righteousness and you have hated wickedness therefore he has anointed you God your God the oil of joy
more than your companions

45:9
are myrrh and aloes cassia all your clothes from palaces of ivory
stringed instrument they have made you glad

45:10
daughters of kings are among your noble women she stands a queen-consort to your right hand in gold of Ophir

45:11
listen O daughter and see and incline your ear
and forget your people and the house of your father

45:12
so that he may desire the king your beauty for he lord is your and bow down to him

45:13
and the daughter of Tyre with a gift your face they will entreat rich ones of a people

45:14
is all glorious a daughter of a king within is from settings of gold her clothing

45:15
to multicolored material she is brought to the king virgins are behind her companions her female are being brought to you

45:16
they are brought with joy and rejoicing
they go in the palace of the king

45:17
in place of your ancestors they will be your children
you will make them into princes in all the land

45:18
I will cause to be remembered your name in every generation and a generation
therefore peoples they will praise you forever and ever
