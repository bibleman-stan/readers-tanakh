85:1
to the choirmaster of the sons of Korah a psalm

85:2
you showed favor to O Yahweh your land
you turned back

85:3
you forgave the iniquity of your people
you covered all their sin Selah

85:4
you withdrew all your fury
you turned back from the burning of your anger

85:5
restore us O God our salvation
and break your anger with us

85:6
forever will you be angry with us will you prolong your anger to a generation and a generation

85:7
you will not you return will you give us life?
and your people they will rejoice in you

85:8
show us O Yahweh loyalty of your covenant and your salvation you will give to us

85:9
I will listen to whatever he will say God Yahweh
for he will speak peace to his people
and to his faithful people and not let them return to folly

85:10
surely is near to those fearing him his salvation
to dwell glory in our land

85:11
covenant loyalty and faithfulness they meet one another
righteousness and peace they kiss

85:12
faithfulness from the earth it springs up and righteousness from heaven it looks down

85:13
also Yahweh he will give the good and our land
it will give its produce

85:14
righteousness before him it will go
and it will make into a way of his footsteps
