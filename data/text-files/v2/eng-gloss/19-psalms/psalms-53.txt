53:1
to the choirmaster on machalath a poem of David

53:2
he says a fool in his heart there is not a god
they behave corruptly
and they do abominably unrighteousness there is not one who does good

53:3
God
from heaven he looks down on the children of humankind
to see is there one who has insight one who seeks God

53:4
of it all he backslides all together they are corrupt there is not one who does good there is not also one

53:5
do they not know those who do wickedness those who devour my people they eat bread
God
they will not call on

53:6
there they fear a fear it is not fear for God
he has scattered the bones of one who encamped against you
you have put them to shame for God he has rejected them

53:7
who will he give will be from Zion the salvation of Israel
when turns back God the captivity of his people
let it be glad Jacob
let it rejoice Israel
