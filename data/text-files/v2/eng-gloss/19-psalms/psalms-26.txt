26:1
of David vindicate me O Yahweh for I in my integrity I have walked
and in Yahweh I have trusted
not I will slip

26:2
test me O Yahweh and put me to the test

26:3
for loyalty of your covenant is to before my eyes
and I walk about in your faithfulness

26:4
not I sit with men of falsehood
and with those who conceal themselves
not I go

26:5
I hate of the assembly of evil-doers and with wicked people
not I sit

26:6
I wash in innocence my palms and I go around your altar O Yahweh

26:7
to make heard (in) a sound of thanksgiving
and to recount all your wonders

26:8
O Yahweh I love of the habitation your house
and the place of the dwelling your glory

26:9
do not remove with sinners my life
and with people of blood(s) my life

26:10
who is in hands of their wickedness and their right hand it is full a bribe

26:11
and I in my integrity I will walk
redeem me and show favor to me

26:12
my foot it has stood on level ground in assemblies
I will bless Yahweh
