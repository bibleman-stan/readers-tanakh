61:1
to the choirmaster on a stringed instrument of David

61:2
hear ! O God cry of my entreaty
pay attention to !
my prayer

61:3
from the end of the earth to you I call when grows faint my heart
on a rock which it is high more than me you will lead me

61:4
for you have been a refuge of me
a tower of strength
from before the enemy

61:5
let me sojourn in your tent forever
let me take refuge in the shelter of your wings Selah

61:6
for you O God you have listened to my vows
you have given the possession of
those fearing your name

61:7
days to the days of the king you will add
his years
be like a generation and a generation

61:8
may he sit forever before God
covenant loyalty and faithfulness
appoint they will preserve him

61:9
thus I will sing praises to your name for ever
to pay I my vows
