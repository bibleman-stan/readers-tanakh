58:1
to the choirmaster al- tashcheth of David a miktam

58:2
really silence of righteousness do you speak
uprightness do you judge of the children of humankind

58:3
also in the heart injustice you do
in the land the violence of your hands you weigh out

58:4
they turn aside wicked people from the womb
they go astray from the belly those who speak falsehood

58:5
venom of them is like the likeness of the venom of a snake like a cobra deaf
which it shuts its ear

58:6
which it will not listens to the sound of charmers one who casts spells skillful

58:7
O God
break away their teeth in their mouth the teeth of young lions
break off O Yahweh

58:8
may they flow like waters which they go about for themselves
may he bend

58:9
like a snail which melting away it goes a miscarriage of a woman
which they have not seen the sun

58:10
before they will perceive your pots a thorn bush
like alive like burning he will sweep it away

58:11
he will rejoice the righteous when he has seen vengeance
his feet he will bathe in the blood of the wicked

58:12
and he will say anyone surely fruit belongs to the righteous
surely there is a God
who judges on the earth
