96:1
sing to Yahweh a new song
sing to Yahweh O all the earth

96:2
sing to Yahweh bless his name
bear news from day to day of his salvation

96:3
recount among the nations of his glory among all the peoples of his wonders

96:4
for is great Yahweh and is to be praised exceedingly is to be feared he above all gods

96:5
for all the gods of the peoples are worthless idols and Yahweh
the heavens he made

96:6
splendor and majesty are before him
strength and splendor are in his sanctuary

96:7
ascribe to Yahweh O clans of peoples
ascribe to Yahweh glory and strength

96:8
ascribe to Yahweh the glory of his name lift up an offering
and come to his courts

96:9
bow down to Yahweh in adornment of holiness
tremble from before him O all the earth

96:10
say among the nations Yahweh he reigns
also it is established the world it will not be shaken
he will judge peoples with uprightness

96:11
let them rejoice the heavens and let it be glad the earth
let it thunder the sea and what fills it

96:12
let it exult the field and all that is in it
then they will shout for joy all the trees of the forest

96:13
before Yahweh for he is coming
for he is coming to judge of the earth he will judge of the world in righteousness and peoples in his faithfulness
