84:1
to the choirmaster on the Gittith
of the sons of Korah a psalm

84:2
how! lovely are dwelling your place
O Yahweh of hosts

84:3
it has longed and also it is my exhausted being for the courts of Yahweh
my heart and my flesh
they shout for joy
to living God

84:4
also a bird it has found a home and a swallow a nest for itself which it has its young put ones
with your altars O Yahweh of hosts
my king
and my God

84:5
how blessed! are those who dwell of your house
still
they praise you Selah

84:6
how blessed! is a person who strength to him is in you
whom highways
are in their heart

84:7
those who pass in the valley of Baca a spring they make it
also blessings
it covers it early rain

84:8
they go from strength to strength
he presents himself to God in Zion

84:9
O Yahweh God of hosts hear ! my prayer
give ear ! O God of Jacob Selah

84:10
our shield look at O God
and pay attention to
the face of your anointed

84:11
for is good a day in your courts
more than a thousand
I have chosen
to stand at the threshold in the house of my God
more than dwelling
in the tents of wickedness

84:12
for is a sun and a shield Yahweh God
favor and honor he gives Yahweh
not he good withholds
to those who walk in blamelessness

84:13
O Yahweh of hosts
how blessed! is anyone
who trusts in you
