42:1
to the choirmaster
a poem of the sons of Korah

42:2
like a deer
which it toward pants channels of water
so my being it pants to you O God

42:3
it my thirsts being for God for the living God when? will I go
and I may appear?
the presence of God

42:4
it has been of me my tears food by day and night
when say to me all the day
where? is your God

42:5
these things I will remember and I will pour out on myself my soul
that I passed on with the crowd I led them
to the house of God
with the sound of a shout of joy and thanksgiving
a multitude celebrating a festival

42:6
why? are you bowed down O my soul and were you in turmoil? on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him
the salvation of his presence

42:7
O my God
on me my being it is bowed down
therefore
I will remember you from the land of the Jordan
and Hermon
from mount Mizar

42:8
deep to deep is calling to the sound your waterfalls
all your breakers and your waves
over me they have passed

42:9
by day he commands Yahweh covenant of his loyalty
and in the night his song is with me
a prayer
to the God of my life

42:10
I will say to God my rock why? have you forgotten me
why? mourning do I walk
in the oppression of an enemy

42:11
with a shattering in my bones
they have taunted me my opposers
when say they to me all the day
where? is your God

42:12
why? are you bowed down O my soul and why? are you in turmoil on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him
the salvation of my face
and my God
