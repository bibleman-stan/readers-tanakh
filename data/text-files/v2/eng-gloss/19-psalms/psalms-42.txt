42:1
to the choirmaster
a poem of the sons of Korah

42:2
like a deer
which it pants towards channels of water
so being my it pants to you O God

42:3
it is thirsty being my for God for the God living when will I go
and will I present myself the presence of God

42:4
it has been my tears my food by day and night
when say to me all the day
where God is your

42:5
these things I will remember and I will pour out on myself my soul
that I passed on with the crowd I led them
to the house of God
with the sound of a shout of joy and thanksgiving
a multitude celebrating a festival

42:6
why are you bowed down my soul and were you in turmoil on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him
the salvation(s) his presence

42:7
my God
on me being my it is bowed down therefore
I will remember you from the land of the Jordan and Hermon(s)
from mount Mizar

42:8
deep to deep is calling to the sound your waterfalls
all your breakers and your waves
over me they have passed

42:9
by day he commands Yahweh loyalty of his covenant
and in the night

42:10
I will say to God my rock why have you forgotten me
why mourning do I walk
in the oppression of an enemy

42:11
with a shattering in my bones
they have taunted me my foes
when say they to me all the day
where God is your

42:12
why are you bowed down my soul and why are you in turmoil on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him
the salvation(s) my face and my God
