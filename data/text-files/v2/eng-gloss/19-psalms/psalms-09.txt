9:1
to the choirmaster alumoth labben
a psalm of David

9:2
I will give thanks to Yahweh with all my heart I will recount
all your wonders

9:3
I will rejoice and I will exult in you
I will sing praises to your name O Most High

9:4
when turn enemies my backwards
they stumble and they perish
from before you

9:5
for you have done my justice and my cause
you have sat to a throne
judging (of) righteousness

9:6
you have rebuked nations you have destroyed the wicked
their name you have wiped out
forever and ever

9:7
the enemy they have come to an end ruins to perpetuity
and cities you have plucked up
it has perished their remembrance they

9:8
and Yahweh forever he remains
he has established for judgment of his throne

9:9
and he will judge of the world with righteousness
he will judge of the nations with uprightness(es)

9:10
and he will be Yahweh a refuge for the oppressed a refuge
to times of trouble

9:11
and they will trust in you those who know name (of) your
for not you have forsaken [those who] seek you O Yahweh

9:12
sing praises
to Yahweh who dwells Zion
declare among the peoples his deeds

9:13
for one who seeks blood(s) them he has remembered
not he has forgotten the cry of distress

9:14
show favor to me O Yahweh
see my affliction from [those who] hate me O you who raise me
from the gates of death

9:15
so that I may recount all your praises
in the gates of the daughter of Zion I will rejoice
in your deliverance

9:16
they have sunk nations in the pit which they made
in the net which they had hidden
it has been caught their foot

9:17
he has made himself known Yahweh justice he has done
by the work his hands he is striking down the wicked
Higgaion Selah

9:18
they will return wicked people to Saul Sheol all the nations
forgetful of God

9:19
for not to perpetuity he will be forgotten the needy
the hope

9:20
arise O Yahweh not let it prevail humankind
let them be judged the nations
on your face

9:21
appoint O Yahweh terror for them
may they know the nations are human beings they Selah
