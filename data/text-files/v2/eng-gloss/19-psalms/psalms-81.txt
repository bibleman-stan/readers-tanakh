81:1
to the choirmaster on the Gittith of Asaph

81:2
sing for joy to God our strength
shout for joy to the God of Jacob

81:3
lift up music and beat a tambourine a harp pleasant with a lyre

81:4
blow at the new moon a ram's horn at the full moon to the day our festival

81:5
for is a decree for Israel it an ordinance
of the God of Jacob

81:6
a testimony in Joseph he established it
when he went out on the land of Egypt
a language (of) which not I knew I heard

81:7
I removed from a burden shoulder his palms from a basket they passed away

81:8
in trouble you called out
and I rescued you
I answered you in the hiding place of thunder
I tested you at the waters of Meribah Selah

81:9
listen my people and let me warn you O Israel if you will listen to me

81:10
not it will be among you a god strange
and not you must bow down to a god of foreignness

81:11
I am Yahweh your God
who brought up you from the land of Egypt
make large your mouth
and I will fill it

81:12
and not it listened my people to my voice and Israel
not it yielded to me

81:13
and I gave it over in the stubbornness their heart
they walked in their own schemes

81:14
if my people will be listening to me Israel in my ways they will walk

81:15
like a little their enemies I will subdue
and on their foes I will turn my hand

81:16
those who hate (of) Yahweh they will cringe to him and may it be time of their forever

81:17
and he fed him from the best of the wheat
and from a rock honey I will satisfy you
