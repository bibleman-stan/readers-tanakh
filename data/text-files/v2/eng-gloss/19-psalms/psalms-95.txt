come let us sing for joy to Yahweh let us shout for joy to the rock of our salvation

95:2
let us come to meet his face with thanksgiving with songs
let us shout for joy to him

95:3
for is a great God Yahweh
and a great king above all gods

95:4
whom are in his hand the depths of the earth
and the peaks of the mountains belong to him

95:5
whom belongs to him the sea and he made it and the dry land
his hands they formed

come let us bow down and let us kneel down let us kneel before Yahweh our maker

for he God is our

forty years I felt loathing at a generation
and I said
