139:1
to the choirmaster of David a psalm O Yahweh you have examined me
and you have known me

139:2
you know my sitting and my standing
you understand my thoughts from a distance

139:3
my traveling and my lying down you sift and all my ways you know intimately

139:4
even there is not a word on my tongue
there! O Yahweh
you know of it all

139:5
back part and front you enclose me
and you have put on me your palm

139:6

139:7
where will I go from your spirit and where from your presence will I flee

139:8
if I will ascend heavens are there you and I will spread as a bed Sheol there you are

139:9
I will rise up the wings of dawn I dwell at the end of the sea

139:10
also there your hand it will lead me
and it will grasp me hand of your right

139:11
and I said surely darkness it will crush me and will be night of the light behind me

139:12
also darkness not it is too dark for you
and night like the day it shines as the darkness as the light

139:13
for you created my kidneys
you wove me together in the womb my mother

139:14
I will give thanks to you
on that awesome I am wonderful are wonderful your works and my self
is knowing exceedingly

139:15
not it was hidden my bones from you
who I was made in the secret place
I was formed in the lower parts of the earth

139:16
form my shapeless they saw your eyes
and on your scroll of them all they were written
days which they were ordained

139:17
and to me how! they are precious your thoughts O God
how! they are vast their sum(s)

139:18
I will count them more than sand they will be numerous
I awoke
and still I am with you

139:19
if you will kill O God the wicked
and O men of blood(s)
depart from me

139:20
those who they speak of you to wickedness they lift up to falsehood your enemies

139:21
not [those who] hate you O Yahweh do I hate and at your rebels do I feel loathing?

139:22
completeness of hatred I hate them enemies
they have become to me

test me
and know thoughts my disquieting
