19:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

19:2
the heavens
are recounting the glory of God
and the work his hands
is telling the firmament

19:3
day to day it pours forth speech
and night to night
it declares knowledge

19:4
there is not speech and there are not words
not
is heard their voice

19:5
in all the earth it has gone out their measuring-line
and at the end of the world their words
for the sun
he has put a tent in them

19:6
and it
is like a bridegroom who comes forth from his chamber
it rejoices like a mighty man
to run a path

19:7
is from the end of the heavens forth its going
and its circuit is to their ends
and there is not a thing hidden
from its heat

19:8
the instruction of Yahweh is perfect restoring (of) life
the testimony of Yahweh is reliable
making wise (of) a simple person

19:9
the precepts of Yahweh are upright making glad (of) the heart
the commandment of Yahweh is pure
enlightening (of) eyes

19:10
the fear of Yahweh is pure enduring forever
the judgments of Yahweh are truth
they are righteous altogether

19:11
desirable
more than gold and more than pure gold much
and sweet more than honey
and the honey of honeycombs

19:12
also your servant is warned by them
is in keeping them
consequence great

19:13
errors who will he discern
from hidden sins acquit me

19:14
also from presumptuous sins keep back your servant
not may they rule over me then I will be blameless
and I will be acquitted
from transgression great

19:15
let them be for acceptance the words my mouth and the meditation my heart before you
O Yahweh
my rock and my redeemer
