63:1
a psalm of David when was he in the wilderness of Judah

63:2
O God are my you I am seeking you earnestly
it is thirsty for you my self
it longs for you my flesh in a land of dryness and weary not water

63:3
thus in the sanctuary I have seen you
to see your strength
and your glory

63:4
because is good loyalty of your covenant more than life
my lips they will extol you

63:5
thus I will bless you in my life in your name
I will lift up my palms

63:6
like fat and fatness it will be satisfied my self
and lips of cries of joy it will praise you my mouth

63:7
if I have remembered you on my beds in the night-watches I meditate on you

63:8
because you are help a my
and in the shadow of your wings I shout for joy

63:9
it clings my self after you me
it supports hand of your right

63:10
and they to devastation they are seeking my life
they will go in the lower parts of the earth

63:11
people will pour him over the hands of the sword
the portion of foxes they will be

63:12
and the king he will rejoice in God
he will boast every one who swears an oath by him
for it will be shut up the mouth of those who speak falsehood
