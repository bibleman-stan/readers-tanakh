13:1
to the choirmaster a psalm of David

13:2
until when O Yahweh will you forget me perpetuity
until when will you hide your face from me

13:3
until when will I set counsel(s) in my soul sorrow in my heart by day
until when will he be exalted my enemy over me

13:4
pay attention answer me O Yahweh my God
give light to my eyes
lest I should sleep the death

13:5
lest he should say my enemy I have prevailed over him
my foes they will rejoice
because I am shaken

13:6
and I in your covenant loyalty I have trusted may it rejoice my heart in your deliverance I will sing to Yahweh
because he has dealt bountifully towards me
