41:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

41:2
how blessed! is one who considers for the poor
in a day of trouble
he will deliver him Yahweh

41:3
Yahweh he will keep him and he will preserve him alive



41:4
Yahweh
he will support him on a couch of illness
all his bed
you have changed in his sickness

41:5
I said O Yahweh show favor to me
heal my self
for I have sinned to you

41:6
my enemies
they speak evil of me
when will he die
and will it perish his name

41:7
and if someone came to see falsehood it speaks
his heart
it gathers wickedness for itself
he goes out to the outside he speaks

41:8
together
on me they whisper to one another all [those who] hate me
on me they plot trouble for me

41:9
a thing of worthlessness has been poured out on him
and the one who he lay down
not he will repeat to rise

41:10
also a person my peace whom I trusted in him who ate my food
he has made great on me a heel

41:11
and you O Yahweh
show favor to me and raise me up
so that I may repay to them

41:12
by this I know that you delight in me
because not he shouts in triumph my enemy over me

41:13
and I
in my integrity you have supported me
and you have set me before you forever

41:14
be blessed Yahweh the God of Israel
from antiquity and until perpetuity
amen and amen
