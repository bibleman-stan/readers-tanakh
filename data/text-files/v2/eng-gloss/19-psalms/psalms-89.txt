89:1
a poem of Ethan the Ezrachite

89:2
the covenant loyalti of Yahweh forever I will sing
to a generation and a generation I will make known your faithfulness with my mouth

89:3
for I have said
forever covenant loyalty it will be built
the heavens you establish your faithfulness in them

89:4
I have made a covenant to my chosen one I have sworn an oath to David my servant

89:5
until perpetuity I will establish your offspring
and I will build to a generation and a generation of your throne Selah

89:6
and they praise the heavens of your wonders O Yahweh also your faithfulness in the assembly of the holy ones

89:7
for who in the clouds is he comparable to Yahweh
is he like Yahweh among the sons of gods

89:8
God is awesome in the council of the holy ones much and he is to be feared above all those around him

89:9
O Yahweh God of hosts who like you is mighty O Yahweh and your faithfulness
is around you

89:10
you are ruling over the rising up of the sea
when rise up its waves you calm them

89:11
you crushed like one slain Rahab
with the arm of your strength you scattered your enemies

89:12
belongs to you heaven also belongs to you the earth
the world and what fills it you founded them

89:13
the north and the south you created them Tabor and Hermon at your name they shout for joy

89:14
belongs to you an arm with strength it is strong your hand
it is exalted hand of your right

89:15
is righteousness and justice the foundation of your throne
covenant loyalty and faithfulness
they come to meet your face

89:16
how blessed! is the people which know a shout of joy O Yahweh
in the light of your face they walk

89:17
in your name they rejoice all the day
and in your righteousness they are exalted

89:18
for are the splendor of their strength you
and in your favor

89:19
for belongs to Yahweh our shield
and belongs to the holy one of Israel our king

89:20
then you spoke in a vision to your faithful people
and you said I have placed help on a warrior
I have raised up a chosen one from the people

89:21
I have found David my servant with the oil of my holiness I have anointed him

89:22
whom my hand it will be steadfast with him
also my arm it will strengthen him

89:23
he will not deceive an enemy him and a son of injustice
he will not afflict him

89:24
and I will crush from before him his foes and those who hate him I will strike down

89:25
and my faithfulness and my covenant loyalty will be with him and in my name
it will be exalted his horn

89:26
and I will set on the sea of his hand
and on the rivers of his hand right

89:27
he will call out to me father are my you my God
and the rock of my salvation

89:28
also I firstborn I will make him most high to the kings of the earth

89:29
forever

89:30
and I will establish forever his offspring and his throne like the days of the heavens

89:31
if they will forsake sons of his my law and in my judgments
they will not walk

89:32
if my statutes they will violate and my commandments
they will not keep

89:33
and I will punish with a rod their transgression
and with plagues their iniquity

89:34
and my covenant loyalty I will not break off from with him
and I will not deal falsely with my faithfulness

89:35
I will not violate my covenant
and the utterance of my lips
I will not change

89:36
one time I have sworn an oath by my holiness
if to David I will lie

89:37
offspring of his forever it will be
and his throne like the sun before me

89:38
like the moon it will be established forever
and a witness in the clouds
which is reliable Selah

89:39
and you have spurned and you have rejected
you have been furious with your anointed

89:40
you have repudiated the covenant of your servant
you have profaned to the ground of his crown

89:41
you have broken through all his walls
you have made his strongholds a ruin

89:42
they have plundered him all those who pass by of the road
he has become a reproach to his neighbors

89:43
you have exalted the his right hand of his foes
you have allowed to rejoice all his enemies

89:44
also you turn back the flint of his sword
and you have not raised him up in battle

89:45
you have made cease from his luster and his throne to the ground you have thrown

89:46
you have cut short the days of his youth
you have wrapped on him shame Selah

89:47
until when O Yahweh will you hide yourself to perpetuity
will it burn like a fire your anger

89:48
remember I what is lifespan concerning what emptiness have you created all the children of humankind

89:49
who is the man who he will live and he will not see death
who he will deliver his life from the hand of Sheol Selah

89:50
where covenant loyalti are your former Lord my
which you swore an oath to David in your faithfulness

89:51
remember Lord my the reproach of your servants your my bearing in my bosom
all many peoples

89:52
which they have reproached your enemies O Yahweh which they have reproached the footprints of your anointed

89:53
be blessed Yahweh forever
amen and amen
