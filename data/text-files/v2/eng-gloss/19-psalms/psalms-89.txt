89:1
a poem
of Ethan the Ezrachite

89:2
the covenant loyalti of Yahweh forever I will sing
to a generation and a generation I will make known your faithfulness with my mouth

89:3
for I have said
forever covenant loyalty it will be built
the heavens you establish your faithfulness in them

89:4
I have made a covenant to my chosen one
I have sworn an oath
to David my servant

89:5
until perpetuity I will establish your offspring
and I will build to a generation and a generation of your throne Selah

89:6
so they may praise the heavens your wonders O Yahweh
also your faithfulness
in the assembly of the holy ones

89:7
for who? in the clouds is he comparable to Yahweh
is he like? Yahweh
among the sons of gods

89:8
God is awesome in the council of the holy ones much
and to be feared
above all those around him

89:9
O Yahweh God of hosts
who? like you is mighty O Yahweh
and your faithfulness
is around you

89:10
you are ruling over the swelling of the sea
when rise up its waves
you calm them

89:11
you crushed like one slain Rahab
with the arm of your strength
you scattered your enemies

89:12
belongs to you heaven also belongs to you the earth
the world and what fills it
you founded them

89:13
the north and the south you created them
Tabor and Hermon
at your name they shout for joy

89:14
belongs to you an arm with strength
it is your strong hand
it is your right exalted hand

89:15
is righteousness and justice the foundation of your throne
covenant loyalty and faithfulness
they come to meet your face

89:16
how blessed! is the people which know a shout of joy
O Yahweh
in the light of your presence they walk !

89:17
in your name they rejoice ! all the day
and in your righteousness they are exalted

89:18
for are the splendor of their strength you
and in your favor
it is exalted our horn

89:19
for belongs to Yahweh our shield
and belongs to the holy one of Israel our king

89:20
then you spoke in a vision to faithful your people
and you said I have placed help on a warrior
I have raised up a chosen one from the people

89:21
I have found David my servant
with the oil of my holiness I have anointed him

89:22
whom my hand it will be steadfast with him
also my arm it will strengthen him

89:23
not he will deceive an enemy him
and a son of injustice
not he will afflict him

89:24
and I will crush from before him his opponents
and those who hate him I will strike down

89:25
and my faithfulness and covenant of my loyalty will be with him
and in my name
it will be exalted his horn

89:26
and I will set on the sea his hand
and on the his right rivers hand

89:27
he will call out to me are my father you
my God
and the rock of my salvation

89:28
also I firstborn I will make him
high most
to the kings of the earth

89:29
for ever
I will keep to him covenant of my loyalty
and my covenant
will be reliable to him

89:30
and I will establish for ever his offspring
and his throne
like the days of the heavens

89:31
if they will forsake his sons my law
and in my judgments
not they will walk !

89:32
if my statutes they will violate
and my commandments
not they will keep

89:33
and I will punish with a rod their transgression
and with plagues their iniquity

89:34
and covenant of my loyalty not I will break off from with him
and not I will deal falsely
with my faithfulness

89:35
not I will violate my covenant
and the utterance of my lips
not I will change

89:36
one time I have sworn an oath by my holiness
if to David I will lie

89:37
his offspring for ever it will be
and his throne like the sun before me

89:38
like the moon it will be established forever
and a witness in the clouds
will be reliable Selah

89:39
and you have spurned and you have rejected
you have been furious
with your anointed

89:40
you have repudiated the covenant of your servant
you have profaned to the ground his crown

89:41
you broken have through all his walls
you have made his strongholds a ruin

89:42
they have plundered him all those who pass by of the road
he has become a reproach
to his neighbors

89:43
you have exalted the right hand of his opponents
you have made to rejoice
all his enemies

89:44
also you turn back the flint of his sword
and not you have raised up him
in battle

89:45
you have made cease from his luster
and his throne
to the ground you have thrown

89:46
you have short made the days of his youth
you have wrapped on him shame Selah

89:47
until when? O Yahweh will you hide yourself? to perpetuity
will it burn? like a fire your anger

89:48
remember I what? is lifespan
concerning what? emptiness
have you created all the children of humankind

89:49
who? is the man who he will live and not he will see death
who he will deliver his life from the hand of Sheol Selah

89:50
where? are covenant of your loyalti former O Lord
which you swore an oath to David
in your faithfulness

89:51
remember O Lord the reproach of your servants
my bearing in my bosom
all many peoples

89:52
which they have reproached your enemies O Yahweh
which they have reproached
the footprints of your anointed

89:53
be blessed Yahweh for ever
amen and amen
