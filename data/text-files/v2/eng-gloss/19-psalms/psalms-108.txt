108:1
a song a psalm of David

108:2
is steadfast my heart O God
I will sing and I will sing praises also my honor

108:3
awake O lyre and harp
I will waken the dawn

108:4
I will give thanks to you among the peoples O Yahweh
and I will sing praises to you not nations

108:5
for is great above the heavens loyalty of your covenant
and to the clouds your faithfulness

108:6
be exalted above the heavens O God
and be over all the earth your glory

108:7
so that they may be rescued your beloved ones
save hand of your right and answer me

108:8
God he has spoken in his sanctuary
I will exult I will divide up Shechem
and the Valley of Succoth I will measure off

108:9
belongs to me Gilead belongs to me Manasseh
and Ephraim is the protection my head Judah is my commander's staff

108:10
Moab is the pot my washing
over Edom I throw my sandal over Philistia
I shout in triumph

108:11
who will he bring me a city of fortification
who has he led me to Edom

108:12
not O God have you rejected us
and not you go out O God with our armies

108:13
give to us help from the foe and is worthlessness
the deliverance of humankind

108:14
in God we will do strength
and he will tread down our foes
