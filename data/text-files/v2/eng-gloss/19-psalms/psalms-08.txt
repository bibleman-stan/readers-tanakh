8:1
to the choirmaster on the Gittith
a psalm of David

8:2
O Yahweh our lord(s)
how! majestic name is your in all the earth
the one who set your splendor
above the heavens

8:3
from the mouth of children and sucking children you have established
strength on account of your foes
to put an end to an enemy
and an avenger

8:4
when I will see your heavens the works your fingers
the moon and the stars
which you have established

8:5
what is humankind that you will remember him
and a son of humankind
that you will pay attention to him

8:6
and you have made him lack little from God
and honor and majesty you crown him

8:7
you make him rule over the your works your hands
everything
you have put under his feet

8:8
sheep and cattle(s) of them all
and also
the animals of the field

8:9
the birds of the heavens and the fish(es) of the sea
that which passes through
the paths of the seas

8:10
O Yahweh our lord(s)
how! majestic name is your
in all the earth
