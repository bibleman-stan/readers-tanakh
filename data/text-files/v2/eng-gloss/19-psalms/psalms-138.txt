138:1
of David I will give thanks to you with all my heart before the gods I will sing praises to you

138:2
I will bow down to your holy temple and I will give thanks to your name on loyalty of your covenant
and on your faithfulness for you have made great over all your name your word

138:3
on the day when I called out and you answered me you made me bold in my soul strength

138:4
they will give thanks to you O Yahweh all the kings of the earth
when they have heard the words of your mouth

138:5
and they will sing in the ways of Yahweh
for is great the glory of Yahweh

138:6
though is exalted Yahweh and the lowly he sees and the proud from a distance he knows

138:7
if I will walk in the midst of trouble
you preserve me alive
on the anger of my enemies you stretch out your hand
and it saves me hand of your right

138:8
Yahweh he will avenge for me O Yahweh loyalty of your covenant is forever
the works of your hands do not abandon
