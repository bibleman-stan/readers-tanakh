75:1
to the choirmaster al- tashcheth
a psalm of Asaph a song

75:2
we give thanks to you O God
we give thanks and is near your name
people recount
your wonders

75:3
when I will take an appointed time
I
uprightness(es) I will judge

75:4
are melting away
the earth and all its inhabitants
I put in order its pillars Selah

75:5
I say to [those who] boast do not boast
and to the wicked people
do not lift up a horn

75:6
do not lift up to the height your horn
speak with a neck arrogant

75:7
for not is from the going out and from the setting place
and not
is from the wilderness exaltation

75:8
but God is the one who judges
this one he brings low
and this one he lifts up

75:9
for a cup is in the hand of Yahweh and wine it is foaming full spiced drink and he has poured out some of this
surely its dregs they will drain they will drink
all
the wicked people of the earth

75:10
and I will declare forever
I will sing praises
to the God of Jacob

75:11
and all the horns of wicked people I will cut off
they will be lifted up
the horns of the righteous
