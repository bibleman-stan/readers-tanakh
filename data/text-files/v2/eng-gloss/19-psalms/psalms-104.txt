104:1
bless my self Yahweh O Yahweh my God you are great exceedingly splendor and majesty you are clothed

104:2
you are wrapping yourself light like garment
you are stretching out the heavens like tent curtain

104:3
the one who makes beams for on the waters rooms his upper
the one who makes clouds his chariot the one who walks on the wings of the wind

104:4
he is making messengers of his winds his servants
a fire blazing

104:5
he established the earth on its foundations not it will be shaken forever and ever

104:6
the deep like clothing you covered it above the mountains they stood the waters

104:7
from your rebuke they fled from the sound of your thunder they hurried away

104:8
they went up mountains they went down valleys to the place
which you had established for them

104:9
a boundary you set not they will pass
not they will return to cover the earth

104:10
you are the one who sends forth springs in the wadis between the mountains
they go

104:11
they give drink to every his animal of the field
they break wild donkeys their thirst

104:12
at them the birds of the heavens it dwells from between foliage they give forth voice

104:13
he is watering mountains from his upper rooms from the fruit of your works
it is satisfied the earth

104:14
he is causing to grow grass for the cattle
and vegetation for the labor of humankind
to bring forth food from the earth

104:15
and wine which it makes glad the heart of humankind
to make shine faces from oil and food
which the heart of humankind it sustains

104:16
they are satisfied the trees of Yahweh the cedars of Lebanon which he planted

104:17
where there birds they make nests the stork is fir trees of its home

104:18
the high mountains belong to the mountain goats crags are a refuge for the rock badgers

104:19
he made the moon for appointed times of the sun
it knows its setting

104:20
you make darkness and it is night
in it moves about every his animal of the forest

104:21
the young lions are roaring for the prey
and to seek from God their food

104:22
it rises the sun they gather and into their dens
they lie down

104:23
he goes out a person to his work
and to his labor until evening

104:24
how! they are many your works O Yahweh of them all with wisdom you have made
it is full the earth your creatures

104:25
this the great sea is and broad of both sides
are there moving things and there is not a number
living creatures small with large

104:26
there ships they go about Leviathan
which you formed to play in it

104:27
of them all to you they hope
to give their food at its appropriate time

104:28
you give to them they gather
you open your hand
they are satisfied good things

104:29
you hide your face they are terrified
you withdraw their breath they die
and to their dust they return

104:30
you send forth your spirit they are created
and you renew the surface of the ground

104:31
may it be the glory of Yahweh forever
may he rejoice Yahweh in his works

104:32
who looks to the earth and it trembled
he touches the mountains and they smoke

104:33
I will sing to Yahweh in my life
I will sing praises to my God in my duration

104:34
may it be pleasing to him my musing
I rejoice in Yahweh

104:35
let them be destroyed sinners from the earth and wicked people be again not they
bless my self Yahweh praise Yahweh
