101:1
of David a psalm
covenant loyalty and justice I will sing to you O Yahweh I will sing praises

101:2
I will give attention to a way blameless
when will you come to me
I will walk about in the integrity of my heart in the midst of my house

101:3
I will not set to before my eyes a thing of worthlessness the doing of swerving deeds I hate
it will not cling to me

101:4
a heart perverse it will depart from me evil
I will not know

101:6
my eyes will be on the faithful people of the earth to dwell with me one who walks in a way blameless
he will serve me

101:7
he will not dwell in the midst of my house one who does deceit one who speaks lies
he will not be established to before my eyes

101:8
to the mornings I will destroy all the wicked people of the land
to cut off from the city of Yahweh
all those who do wickedness
