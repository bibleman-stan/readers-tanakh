143:1
a psalm
of David
O Yahweh hear my prayer
give ear ! to my supplications
in your faithfulness answer me
in your righteousness

143:2
and may not you go in judgment with your servant
for not he is righteous before you living any person

143:3
for he has pursued an enemy my self
he has crushed to the ground my life
he has caused to dwell me in dark places
like dead people of antiquity

143:4
and it has fainted away on me my spirit
in the midst of me
it is appalled my heart

143:5
I have remembered days from antiquity
I have meditated on every your deed
on the work of your hands I meditate

143:6
I have spread out my hands to you
my throat like a land exhausted is to you Selah

143:7
hurry answer me O Yahweh it is exhausted my spirit
may not you hide your face from me
and I will be equal
with those who go down of the pit

143:8
make hear me in the morning covenant of your loyalty for in you I have trusted
make known to me
the way which I will go
for to you
I lift up my desire

143:9
deliver me from my enemies O Yahweh
to you I have covered

143:10
teach me to do your will for you are my God
your good spirit
may it lead me
in a land of level ground

143:11
for the sake of your name O Yahweh you will preserve alive me
in your righteousness you will bring out from trouble my life

143:12
and in covenant of your loyalty you will destroy my enemies
and you will destroy all the opposers of my life
for
I am your servant
