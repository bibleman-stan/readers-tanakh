7:1
a Shiggaion
of David
which he sang to Yahweh
on the words of Cush
a Ben- jaminite

7:2
O Yahweh my God in you I have sought refuge
save me from all my pursuers
and deliver me

7:3
lest he should tear to pieces like a lion my self
tearing
and there not is a deliverer

7:4
O Yahweh my God if I have this done
if there is unrighteousness in my hands

7:5
if I have repaid one who is at peace with evil me
and I have rescued ! my opposer without cause

7:6
may he pursue an enemy my life and may he overtake it
and he may trample to the earth my life
and my honor to the dust may he lay Selah

7:7
arise ! O Yahweh in your anger
raise yourself on the furi of my opposers
and awake ! to me
justice you have commanded

7:8
and an assembly of nations may it surround you
and over it
to the height return !

7:9
Yahweh let him judge peoples
vindicate me O Yahweh
according to my righteousness and according to my integrity on me

7:10
may it come to an end please the evil of wicked people and may you establish the righteous
and who tests hearts
and kidneys
O righteous God

7:11
my shield is on God
a deliverer
of people upright of heart

7:12
God is a righteous judge
and God
is indignant on every day

7:13
if not anyone will repent his sword he will sharpen
his bow he has bent
and he has prepared it

7:14
and for him he has prepared weapons of death
his arrows
into burning things he will make

7:15
there! he is in labor with wickedness
and he conceives mischief
and he brings forth falsehood

7:16
a pit he digs and he has dug it
and he has fallen
in the pit which he made

7:17
it returns his mischief on his own head
and on his scalp
his violence it comes down

7:18
I will give thanks to Yahweh according to his righteousness
and I will sing praise to
the name of Yahweh the Most High
