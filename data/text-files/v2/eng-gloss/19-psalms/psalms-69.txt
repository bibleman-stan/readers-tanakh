69:1
to the choirmaster on Shoshannim of David

69:2
save me O God
for they have come waters to the neck

69:3
I have sunk in mire of depth and there is not a foothold
I have come in depths of water
and a flood it has overflowed me

69:4
I have become weary by my calling out it has become parched my throat
they have failed eyes of my waiting for my God

69:5
they are many more than the hairs my head [those who] hate me without cause
they are numerous [those who] destroy me enemies my falsehood
that which not I stole
then I will return

69:6
O God
you know my folly and my guilt(s)
from you not they are hidden

69:7
not let them be ashamed in me [those who] wait for you Lord my Yahweh of hosts
not let them be humiliated in me [those who] seek you
O God of Israel

69:8
for on you I have borne reproach
it has covered ignominy my face

69:9
estranged I have become to my brothers and a foreigner
to the children my mother

69:10
for the zeal your house it has consumed me
and the reproaches of those who reproach you
they have fallen on me

69:11
and I wept with fasting my self
and it became reproach(es) to me

69:12
and I made clothing my sackcloth
and I became for them (into) a byword

69:13
they speak in me those who sit of the gate
and taunt songs of drinkers of strong drink

69:14
and I my prayer is to you O Yahweh a time of favor
O God in the greatness your covenant your loyalty
answer me
in the your faithfulness your salvation

69:15
deliver me from the mud and not let me sink
may I be delivered from [those who] hate me and from depths of water

69:16
not let it overflow me a flood of water and not let it swallow me up the deep
and not let it close over me the pit its mouth

69:17
answer me O Yahweh for is good loyalty of your covenant
according to the greatness your compassion(s)
turn to me

69:18
and do not hide your face from your servant for it is distress to me
hurry answer me

69:19
draw near to self my redeem it
on account of enemies my ransom me

69:20
you know my reproach and my shame and my ignominy
are before you all my foes

69:21
reproach it has broken my heart
and I have become sick
and I waited to show sympathy and there was not and for comforters
and not I found them

69:22
and they put in food my poison and for my thirst
they gave me to drink vinegar

69:23
may it become their table before them (into) a trap
and for allies (into) a snare

69:24
may they grow dim their eyes from seeing and their loins
continually make to shake

69:25
pour out on them your indignation
and the burning your anger
may it overtake them

69:26
may it be their encampment made desolate in their tents
not may anyone be dwelling

69:27
for you those whom you struck they have harassed
and concerning the pain of your fatally wounded ones they have recounted

69:28
put iniquity to their iniquity
and not let them come
in your righteousness

69:29
may they be wiped out from the scroll of life
and with righteous people
not may they be written down

69:30
and I am afflicted and am in pain
your salvation O God may it set me on high

69:31
I will praise the name of God with a song
and I will magnify him with thanksgiving

69:32
and it will be good to Yahweh more than an ox a young bull
having horns having hooves

69:33
they will see humble people they will rejoice
O you who seek (of) God
and may it live your heart

69:34
for is listening to needy people of Yahweh and his prisoners
not he despises

69:35
may they praise him heaven and earth the seas
and every thing which moves in them

69:36
for God he will save Zion
and he will rebuild the cities of Judah
and they will dwell there
and they will take possession of it

69:37
and the offspring his servants they will inherit it
