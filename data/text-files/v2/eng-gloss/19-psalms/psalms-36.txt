36:1
to the choirmaster of the servant of Yahweh of David

36:2
the utterance of transgression to wicked person is in the midst my heart
there is not fear of God
to before his eyes

36:3
for he flatters himself in his own eyes
to find his iniquity to hate it

36:4
the words his mouth are wickedness and deceit
he has ceased to act prudently by doing good

36:5
wickedness he plans on his bed
he takes his stand on a way not good evil
not he rejects

36:6
O Yahweh is in the heavens loyalty of your covenant
your faithfulness is to the clouds

36:7
your righteousness is like the mountains of God your judgments are the great deep
humankind and animals you deliver O Yahweh

36:8
how! precious covenant loyalty is your
O God
and the children of humankind in the shadow your wings
they take refuge

36:9
they take their fill from the fatness your house
and the river your delights you give them to drink

36:10
for is with you a fountain of life in your light we see light

36:11
prolong loyalty of your covenant to [those who] know you
and your righteousness to people upright of heart

36:12
not may it come to me the foot of pride and the hand of wicked people not may it make me wander

36:13
there they have fallen those who do (of) wickedness
they were pushed down
and not they were able to rise
