23:1
a psalm of David
Yahweh is my shepherd
not I lack

23:2
in pastures of fresh grass he makes me lie down at waters of rest(s) he leads me

23:3
my self he restores
he guides me in tracks of righteousness for the sake of his name

23:4
also though I will go in a valley of deep darkness not I will fear harm
for you are with me
your rod and your staff
they comfort me

23:5
you arrange before me a table
before my foes you have refreshed with oil my head my cup is saturation

23:6
surely good and covenant loyalty they will pursue me all the days my life
and I will return in the house of Yahweh for length of days
