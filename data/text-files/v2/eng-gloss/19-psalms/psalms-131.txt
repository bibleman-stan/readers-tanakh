131:1
the song of the ascents
of David
O Yahweh not it has been haughty my heart and not they have been uplifted my eyes
and not I have walked in things too great and in things too difficult for me

131:2
if not I have made still and I have made quiet
my self
like a weaned child on his mother
is like weaned child on me my soul

131:3
wait O Israel to Yahweh
from now
and until perpetuity
