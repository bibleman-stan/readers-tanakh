131:1
the song of the ascents of David
O Yahweh it has not been haughty my heart and they have not been uplifted my eyes
and I have not walked in things too great
and in things too difficult for me

131:2
if I have not made still and I have made quiet self my like a weaned child on his mother
is like weaned child on me my soul

131:3
wait O Israel to Yahweh from now
and until perpetuity
