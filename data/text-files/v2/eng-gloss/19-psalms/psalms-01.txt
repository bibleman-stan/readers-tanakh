1:1
blessed is the man

1:2
but rather is in the law of Yahweh
his delight
and in his law he meditates
by day and night

1:3
and he is
like a tree planted at streams
of water which its fruit it gives at its appropriate time
and its leafage not it withers
and all that he does it succeeds

1:4
not are so the wicked people
but rather like the chaff
which it drives it about wind

1:5
therefore not they will stand wicked people in the judgment
and sinners
in the congregation of righteous people

1:6
for is knowing Yahweh the way of righteous people
and the way of wicked people it will perish
