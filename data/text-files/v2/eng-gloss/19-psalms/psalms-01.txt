1:1
blessed is the man who he will not walks in the counsel of wicked people
and in the way of sinners he will not stands
and in the seat of mockers
he will not sits

1:2
but rather is in the law of Yahweh his delight
and in his law he meditates by day and night

1:3
and he is
like a tree planted at streams of water which its fruit it gives at its appropriate time
and its leafage it will not withers
and all that he does it succeeds

1:4
not are so the wicked people
but rather like the chaff
which it drives it about wind

1:5
therefore they will not stand wicked people in the judgment and sinners in the congregation of righteous people

1:6
for is knowing Yahweh the way of righteous people
and the way of wicked people it will perish
