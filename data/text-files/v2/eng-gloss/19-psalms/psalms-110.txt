110:1
of David
a psalm
the utterance of Yahweh to my lord
sit to my right hand
until I will make your enemies
a footstool for your feet

110:2
the staff of your strength
he will stretch out Yahweh from Zion
rule
in the midst of your enemies

110:3
your people will be freewill offerings on the day of your strength
in majesti of holiness from the womb of dawn
will belong to you
the dew of your youth

110:4
he has sworn an oath Yahweh and not he will repent
you are a priest for ever
on the manner of
Melchizedek

110:5
the Lord is at your right hand
he shatters on the day of his anger kings

110:6
he judges among the nations it is full corpses
he shatters chiefs
over the great earth

110:7
from a wadi on the way he drinks
therefore
he lifts up a head
