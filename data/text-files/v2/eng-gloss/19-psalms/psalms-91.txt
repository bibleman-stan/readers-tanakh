one who dwells in the shelter of the High Most in the shadow of the Almighty
he will lodge

91:2
I will say of Yahweh my refuge and my stronghold God whom I trust in him

91:3
for he will deliver you from the snare of a fowler from a plague of destruction

91:4
with his pinion he will cover you and under his wings you will take refuge is a shield and a wall of his faithfulness

91:5
not you will be afraid from the dread of night from an arrow
which it will fly by day

91:6
from a plague which in the gloom it will walk from destruction which it will devastate noon

91:7
it will fall from your side a thousand
and ten thousand from your right hand
to you
not it will draw near

91:8
only with your own eyes you will look
and the retribution of wicked people you will see

91:9
because you Yahweh my refuge the High Most
you have made place your dwelling

91:10
not it will happen to you harm and plague
not it will draw near in your tent

91:11
for his angels he will command to you to guard you in all your ways

91:12
on palms they will lift you up lest you should strike on stone of your foot

91:13
on a lion and a cobra you will tread you will trample a young lion and a serpent

91:14
because me he loves and I will deliver him
I will set him on high
because he knows my name

91:15
he will call out to me and I will answer him will be with him I in trouble
I will rescue him
and I will honor him
