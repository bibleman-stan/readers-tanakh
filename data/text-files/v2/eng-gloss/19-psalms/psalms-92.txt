92:1
a psalm a song
for the day of the sabbath

92:2
is good
to give thanks to Yahweh
and to sing praises to your name O Most High

92:3
to declare in the morning loyalty of your covenant
and your faithfulness in the nights

92:4
on a ten and on a lyre
on a melody with a harp

92:5
for you have made me rejoice O Yahweh by your deeds
in the works of your hands I sing for joy

92:6
how! they are great your works O Yahweh exceedingly they are deep your thoughts

92:7
a person stupid not he knows and a fool not he understands this

92:8
when sprout up wicked people like vegetation
and they flourished all those who do wickedness
they to be destroyed until perpetuity

92:9
and you are height
forever O Yahweh

92:10
for there! your enemies O Yahweh
for there! your enemies they will perish
they will be scattered
all those who do wickedness

92:11
and you have raised up like a wild ox my horn
I have been anointed with oil fresh

92:12
and it has looked my eye on my walls
in those who rise on me evil-doers they have heard my ears

92:13
the righteous like palm tree he will flourish
like a cedar in Lebanon he will grow

92:14
they are planted in the house of Yahweh
in the courts of our God our they will flourish

92:15
still they will bear fruit in old age
fat and fresh they will be

92:16
to declare that is upright Yahweh my rock and not
