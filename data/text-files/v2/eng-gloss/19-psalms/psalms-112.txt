112:1
praise Yahweh how blessed! is a person fearing Yahweh in his commandments
he delights exceedingly

112:2
mighty in the land it will be his offspring
the generation of upright people it will be blessed

112:3
wealth and riches are in his house and his righteousness is enduring forever

112:4
it rises in the light darkness for the upright people he is gracious and compassionate and righteous

112:5
is good a person who shows favor and who lends
who he maintains his affairs with justice

112:6
for forever not he will be shaken
a remembrance of perpetuity he will become a righteous person

112:7
from news bad not he will be afraid is steadfast his heart
trusting in Yahweh

112:8
is sustained his heart not he will be afraid
until that he will look on his foes

112:9
he scatters he gives to needy people
his righteousness is enduring forever his horn
it will be exalted in honor

112:10
a wicked person he will see and he will be angry
his teeth he will gnash and he will melt away
the desire of wicked people it will perish
