43:1
vindicate me O God and conduct my case from a nation not faithful
from a man of deceit and injustice you will deliver me

43:2
for you are the God of my refuge why have you rejected me
why mourning do I go about in the oppression of an enemy

43:3
send your light and your truth they may they lead me
may they bring me to the holy your mountain
and to place your dwelling

43:4
and I will go to the altar of God to the God of the joy of my rejoicing
and I will give thanks to you with a harp O my God

43:5
why are you bowed down my soul and why are you in turmoil on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him the salvation of my face and my God
