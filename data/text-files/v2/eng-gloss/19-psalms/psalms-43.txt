43:1
vindicate me O God and conduct my case
from a nation not faithful
from a man of deceit and injustice you will deliver me

43:2
for you are the my God my refuge why have you rejected me
why mourning do I go about
in the oppression of an enemy

43:3
send your light and your truth they may they lead me
may they bring me to the your holy mountain
and to place(s) your dwelling

43:4
and I will go to the altar of God
to the God of the joy my rejoicing
and I will give thanks to you with a harp
O my God

43:5
why are you bowed down my soul and why are you in turmoil on me
wait for God for again I will give thanks to him
the salvation(s) my face
and my God
