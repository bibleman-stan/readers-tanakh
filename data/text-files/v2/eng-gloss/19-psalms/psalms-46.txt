46:1
to the choirmaster of the sons of Korah
on alamoth a song

46:2
God for us is a refuge and strength
a help in troubles
he is found exceedingly

46:3
therefore not we will fear when changes the earth
and when shake mountains
in the heart of the seas

46:4
they will roar they will foam its waters
they will shake mountains at its swelling Selah

46:5
a river of its streams
they make glad the city of God
the holy place of the dwellings of the Most High

46:6
God is in its midst not it will be shaken
he will help it God
to the turning of the morning

46:7
they were in a tumult nations they shook kingdoms
he gave forth with his voice
it melted the earth

46:8
Yahweh of hosts is with us is a our refuge
the God of Jacob Selah

46:9
come see the works of Yahweh who he has appointed desolations on the earth

46:10
he is making cease wars to the end of the earth
bows he breaks and he cuts in two spears carts
he burns with fire

46:11
be quiet and know that I am God
I will be exalted among the nations
I will be exalted on the earth

46:12
Yahweh of hosts is with us is a our refuge
the God of Jacob Selah
