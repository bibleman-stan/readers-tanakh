71:1
in you O Yahweh I have taken refuge
may not I be ashamed for ever

71:2
in your righteousness
you will rescue me and you will deliver me
incline to me your ear
and save me

71:3
become of me a rock a dwelling place to go
continually
you have given command to save me
for are my rock and my stronghold you

71:4
O my God
deliver me from the hand of the wicked
from the hand of the wrongdoer and the ruthless

71:5
for you are my hope
O Lord Yahweh
my confidence since my youth

71:6
on you I have supported myself from the womb
from the inward parts of my mother you were [the one who] cut off me
is in you my praise continually

71:7
like a portent I have become for many people
and you
are refuge of my strength

71:8
it is filled my mouth your praise
all the day
your splendor

71:9
may not you cast away me to a time of old age
when fails my strength
may not you forsake me

71:10
for they have spoken my enemies of me
and those who watch of my life
they have consulted together

71:11
saying God he has forsaken him
pursue and seize him
for there not is a deliverer

71:12
O God may not you be far from me
O my God
to my help make haste !

71:13
may they be ashamed may they come to an end of the accusers of my self
may they cover themselves reproach and ignominy
those who seek
my harm

71:14
and I continually I will wait
and I will add
to all your praise

71:15
my mouth it will recount your righteousness
all the day your salvation
for not I know numbers

71:16
I will come
with the mighty deeds of the Lord Yahweh
I will bring to remembrance your righteousness to alone you

71:17
O God
you have taught me since my youth
and until now
I declare your wonders

71:18
and also old until age and grey hair O God may not you forsake me
until I will declare your strength to a generation
to every one who he will come
your might

71:19
and your righteousness O God
is to the height
who you have great done things
O God
who? is like you

71:20
who you have made see me troubles many
and calamities
you will return you will preserve alive me
and from the depths of the earth
you will return you will bring up me

71:21
may you increase my greatness
and may you turn may you comfort me

71:22
also I will praise you with an instrument of a lyre your faithfulness O my God
I will make music to you with a harp
holy O one of
Israel

71:23
they will sing for joy of my lips if I will make music to you
and my self
which you have redeemed

71:24
also my tongue
all the day it will utter your righteousness
for they are ashamed for they are abashed
those who seek my harm
