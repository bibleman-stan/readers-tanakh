31:1
to the choirmaster a psalm of David

31:2
in you O Yahweh I have taken refuge not let me be ashamed forever in righteousness of your deliver me

31:3
incline to me ear of your quickly deliver me
my become a rock of refuge a house of strongholds
to save me

31:4
for rock are my and my stronghold you
and for the sake of your name
you guide me and you lead me

31:5
you will bring me out
from the net which people have hidden for me for you place of refuge are my

31:6
in your hand I entrust my spirit you have redeemed me O Yahweh
God of faithfulness

31:7
I hate those who keep vanities of falsehood and I
to Yahweh I trust

31:8
I will be glad and I will rejoice in your covenant loyalty that you saw my affliction
you knew the troubles of my self

31:9
and not you delivered me up in the hand of an enemy
you made stand in roomy place my feet

31:10
show favor to me O Yahweh for it is distress to me
it has wasted away with grief of my eye being my and my belly

31:11
for they have wasted away in sorrow of my life and my years with groaning
it has failed by iniquity of my strength
and my bones they have wasted away

31:12
from all my foes I have become a reproach
and to neighbors my exceedingly and a dread to my acquaintances
those who see me in the street
they flee from me

31:13
I have been forgotten like a dead person from the heart
I have become like a vessel which is destroyed

31:14
for I have heard the whispering of many people terror is from around about
when they seated themselves together on me
to take my life they planned

31:15
and I on you I rely O Yahweh
I say God are my you

31:16
are in hand of your times my deliver me from the hand of my enemies and from those who pursue me

31:17
make shine your face on your servant
save me in your covenant loyalty

31:18
O Yahweh
not let me be ashamed for I have called out to you
may they be ashamed wicked people
may they be silent to Sheol

31:19
may they be dumb
lips of falsehood which speak on the righteous arrogant with pride and contempt

31:20
how! great goodness is your which you have stored up for those fearing you
you have done for those who take refuge in you before the children of humankind

31:21
you hide them in the hiding place your presence from the conspiracies of man you hide them in a shelter from strife of tongues

31:22
be blessed Yahweh for he has made wonderful loyalty of his covenant to me in a city of siege

31:23
and I said when I was making haste
I have been cut off from before your eyes nevertheless you heard the sound of my supplications
when I cried for help to you

31:24
love of Yahweh O all his faithful people faithful people is preserving Yahweh
and he is repaying on excess
one who does pride

31:25
be strong and let it show strength of your heart O all you who wait for Yahweh
