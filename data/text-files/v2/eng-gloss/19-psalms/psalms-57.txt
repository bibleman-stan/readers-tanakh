57:1
to the choirmaster al- tashcheth of David a miktam
when fled he from before Saul in the cave

57:2
show favor to me O God show favor to me
for in you it takes refuge my self
and in the shadow of your wings I take refuge
until it will pass by destruction

57:3
I call out to God Most High to God who avenges on me

57:4
he will send from heaven and he will save me
he will reproach the one who tramples on me Selah
God will send loyalty of his covenant and his faithfulness

57:5
my self is among lions I lie with those who devour the children of humankind
their teeth are a spear and arrows and their tongue
is a sword sharp

57:6
be exalted above the heavens O God be over all the earth your glory

57:7
a net they prepared for my feet it bowed down my self
they dug before me a pit
they have fallen in the midst of it Selah

57:8
is steadfast my heart O God is steadfast my heart I will sing and I will sing praises

57:9
awake my honor
awake O lyre and harp I will waken the dawn

57:10
I will give thanks to you among the peoples Lord my
I will sing praises to you not nations

57:11
for is great to the heavens loyalty of your covenant
and to the clouds of your faithfulness

57:12
be exalted above the heavens O God be over all the earth your glory
