57:1
to the choirmaster al- tashcheth of David a miktam
when fled he from before Saul
in the cave

57:2
show favor to me O God show favor to me
for in you it takes refuge my self
and in the shadow of your wings I take refuge
until it will pass by destruction

57:3
I call out to God Most High
to God
who avenges on me

57:4
he will send from heaven and he will save me
he will reproach one who crushes me Selah
he will send God
covenant of his loyalty and his faithfulness

57:5
my self in among lions I lie with those that devour
the children of humankind
their teeth are a spear and arrows
and their tongue
is a sword sharp

57:6
be exalted ! above the heavens O God
be over all the earth your glory

57:7
a net they prepared for my feet it bent down my self
they dug before me a pit
they have fallen in the midst of it Selah

57:8
is steadfast my heart O God is steadfast my heart
I will sing
and I will sing praises

57:9
awake ! O my honor
awake ! O lyre and harp
I will waken the dawn

57:10
I will give thanks to you among the peoples O Lord
I will sing praises to you
not nations

57:11
for is great to the heavens covenant of your loyalty
and to the clouds your faithfulness

57:12
be exalted ! above the heavens O God
be over all the earth your glory
