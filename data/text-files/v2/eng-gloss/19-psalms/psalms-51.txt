51:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

51:2
when came to him Nathan the prophet
when he had gone
into Bathsheba

51:3
show favor to me O God according to your covenant loyalty
according to the greatness your compassion(s)
wipe out my transgressions

51:4

51:5
for my transgressions I know
and my sin is before me continually

51:6
to you alone I have sinned and the evil in your eyes
I have done
so that you may be just when speak you
you will be pure when judge you

51:7
here! in iniquity I was brought forth
and in sin
she conceived me my mother

51:8
here! faithfulness you desire in the inner parts
and in a place kept secret
wisdom you cause me to know

51:9
you will cleanse me from sin with hyssop so that I may be pure
you will wash me
and more than snow I will become white

51:10
you will make me hear joy and gladness
let them rejoice
the bones which you have crushed

51:11
hide your face from my sins
and all iniquities my wipe out

51:12
a heart pure create for me O God
and a spirit steadfast
renew in my inner being

51:13
do not cast me from before face you
and the your holy spirit
do not take from me

51:14
restore to me the joy your salvation
and a spirit willing you will sustain me

51:15
I will teach transgressors your ways
and sinners
to you they will return

51:16
deliver me from blood(s) O God the my God my salvation
it will shout for joy of my tongue
your righteousness

51:17
Lord my lips you will open
and my mouth
it will declare your praise

51:18
for not you desire sacrifice and I will give it
burnt offering
not you are pleased with

51:19
the sacrifices of God are a spirit broken
a heart broken and crushed
O God
not you despise

51:20
do good to in your favor Zion
you will build
the walls of Jerusalem

51:21
then you will delight in sacrifices of righteousness burnt offering and whole offering
then people will offer up on your altar young bulls
