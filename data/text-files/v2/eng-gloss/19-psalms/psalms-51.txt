51:1
to the choirmaster
a psalm of David

51:2
when came to him Nathan the prophet
just when he had gone
into Bathsheba

51:3
show favor to me O God according to covenant of your loyalty
according to the greatness of your compassion
wipe out my transgressions

51:4
increase wash me from my iniquity
and from my sin cleanse me

51:5
for my transgressions I know
and my sin is before me continually

51:6
to you to alone you I have sinned and the evil in your view
I have done
so that you may be just when speaking you
you will be pure when judging you

51:7
here! in iniquity I was brought forth
and in sin
she conceived me my mother

51:8
here! faithfulness you desire in the covered places
and in a place kept secret
wisdom you cause to know me

51:9
you will cleanse from sin me with hyssop so I may be pure
you will wash me
and more than snow I will become white

51:10
you will make hear me joy and gladness
let them rejoice
the bones which you have crushed

51:11
hide your face from my sins
and all my iniquities wipe out

51:12
a pure heart create for me O God
and a spirit steadfast
renew in my inner being

51:13
may not you cast me from before you
and the spirit of your holiness
may not you take from me

51:14
restore ! to me the joy of your salvation
and a spirit willing you will sustain me

51:15
I will teach transgressors your ways
and sinners
to you they will return

51:16
deliver me from blood O God the God of my salvation
it will shout for joy of my tongue
your righteousness

51:17
O Lord my lips you open will
and my mouth
it will declare your praise

51:18
for not you desire sacrifice and I will give it
burnt offering
not you are pleased with

51:19
the sacrifices of God are a broken spirit
a broken heart and crushed
O God
not you despise

51:20
good do to ! in your favor obj. Zion
you will build
the walls of Jerusalem

51:21
then you will delight in sacrifices of righteousness burnt offering and whole offering
then people will offer up on your young altar bulls
