15:1
a psalm of David O Yahweh who will he sojourn in your tent
who will he dwell on the holy your mountain

15:2
one who walks blameless and one who does righteousness
and one who speaks truth in his heart

15:3
not he takes slander on his tongue
not he does to his neighbor evil and reproach
not he lifts up on his kinsman

15:4
is despised in his eyes a rejected person
and those fearing Yahweh he honors
he swears to do harm
and not he changes

15:5
his money not he gives for interest and a bribe on the innocent
not he takes one who does these things
not he will be shaken forever
