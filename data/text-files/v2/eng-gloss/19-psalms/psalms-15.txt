15:1
a psalm of David O Yahweh who will he sojourn in your tent
who will he dwell on the holy your mountain

15:2
one who walks blameless and one who does righteousness
and one who speaks truth in his heart

15:3
he will not takes slander on his tongue
he does not to his neighbor evil and reproach
he will not lifts up on his kinsman

15:4
is despised in his eyes a rejected person
and those fearing Yahweh he honors
he swears to do harm
and he will not changes

15:5
his money he will not gives for interest and a bribe on the innocent
he will not takes one who does these things
he will not be shaken forever
