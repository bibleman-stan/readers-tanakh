21:1
to the choirmaster a psalm of David

21:2
O Yahweh in your strength he rejoices the king
and in your deliverance
how!

21:3
the desire of his heart you have given to him and the request of his lips
you have not withheld Selah

21:4
for you meet him blessings of good things
you set to his head a crown of pure gold

21:5
life he asked from you gave it to him length of days
forever and ever

21:6
is great his honor by your victory
splendor and majesty
you place on him

21:7
for you give him blessings forever
you make him rejoice with gladness with your presence

21:8
for the king is trusting in Yahweh
and in the covenant loyalty of the High Most
he will not be shaken

21:9
it will find your hand all enemies your hand your right
it will find those who hate you

21:10
you will make them like a furnace of fire to the time of your presence
Yahweh in his anger he will swallow them up
and it will consume them fire

21:11
their descendants from the earth you will destroy and their offspring from the children of humankind

21:12
though they have extended on you harm
they have planned a scheme
they will not succeed

21:13
for you will make them a shoulder on your bowstrings
you will prepare on their faces

21:14
rise up O Yahweh in your strength we will sing and we will sing praises might your
