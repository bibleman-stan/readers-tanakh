121:1
a song of the ascents
I lift up my eyes to the mountains from where?
will it come my help

121:2
my help is from with Yahweh
who made (of) heaven and earth

121:3
not may he permit to slip your foot
not may he slumber
your protector

121:4
there! not he will slumber and not he will sleep
the protector of Israel

121:5
Yahweh is your protector
Yahweh shade is your
at the hand your right your side

121:6
by day
the sun not it will strike you
and the moon in the night

121:7
Yahweh
he will protect you from all harm
he will protect your life

121:8
Yahweh
he will protect your going out and your coming from now and until perpetuity
