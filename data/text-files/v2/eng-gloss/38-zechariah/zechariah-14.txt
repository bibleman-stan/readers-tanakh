14:1
there! a day is coming of Yahweh
and it will be divided your plunder in your midst

14:2
and I will gather all the nations to Jerusalem for battle
and it will be captured the city and they will be plundered the houses
and the women



14:3
and he will go forth Yahweh
and he will fight against the nations those like the day fought he in a day of battle

14:4
and they will stand his feet on that day on the mountain of Olives which is on the face of Jerusalem from the east
and it will be split open the mountain of Olives from middle of its eastward and westward a valley
great very and it will depart half of the mountain northward
and half of it southward

14:5
and you will flee the valley my mountains for it will reach the valley of mountains to Azal
and you will flee just as you fled from before the earthquake in the days of Uzziah the king of Judah and he will come Yahweh my God
all holy ones [will be] with you

14:6
and it will be on that day
not it will be light splendid ones


14:7
and it will be one day it will be known to Yahweh not day
and not night and it will be to the time of evening it will be light

14:8
and it will be on that day they will go out waters living from Jerusalem
of them half to the sea eastern
and half of them to the sea western in the summer
and in the winter
it will be

14:9
and he will become Yahweh king over all the earth
on that day he will be Yahweh one and his name will be one

14:10
it will change all the land like the Arabah from Geba to Rimmon the south of Jerusalem
and it will rise and it will remain in its place (to) from the gate of Benjamin to the place of the gate first to the gate of the corners
and the tower of Hananel to the winepresses of the king

14:11
and people will dwell in it
and total destruction not it will be again
and it will remain Jerusalem to security

14:12
and this it will be the plague which he will strike Yahweh all the peoples
which they have gone to war on Jerusalem
he will cause to rot its flesh and it is standing on its feet
and its eyes they will rot in their sockets
and its tongue it will rot in their mouth

14:13
and it will be on that day
it will be a panic of Yahweh great among them
and they will take hold of each the his hand his neighbor
and it will go up his hand on the his hand his neighbor

14:14
and also Judah it will fight at Jerusalem
and it will be gathered the wealth of all the nations all around gold
and silver
and garments to abundance very great

14:15
and thus it will be the plague of the horse the mule the camel and the donkey
and all the livestock which it will be in the camps those like the plague this

14:16
and it will be every [one who] remains of all the nations which came on Jerusalem
and they will go up as often as a year in a year to bow down to the king Yahweh of hosts
and to celebrate a festival
the festival of Tabernacles

14:17
and it will be one who not he will go up from with the clans of the earth to Jerusalem
to bow down to the king Yahweh of hosts
and not on them it will be rain

14:18
and if the clan of Egypt not it will go up
and not it is going
and not on them it will be the plague which he will strike Yahweh on the nations
which not they will go up to celebrate a festival the festival of Tabernacles

14:19
this it will be the punishment for sin of Egypt
and the punishment for sin of all the nations which not they will go up to celebrate a festival
the festival of Tabernacles

14:20
on that day it will be on the bells of the horses holiness to Yahweh
and it will be the pots in the house of Yahweh [will be] like the bowls before the altar

14:21
and it will be every pot in Jerusalem
and in Judah a holy thing to Yahweh of hosts
and they will come all [those who] sacrifice
and they will take some of them
and they will boil in them
and not he will be a merchant again in the house of Yahweh of hosts on that day
