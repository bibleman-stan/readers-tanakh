1:1
and the word of Yahweh came to Jonah son of Amittai, saying

1:2
arise go to Nineveh the great city
and call out on it because it has come up their wickedness before me

1:3
and Jonah arose to flee to Tarshish from before Yahweh
and he went down to Joppa
and he found a ship which was about to go Tarshish
and he gave its fare
and he went down in it to go with them to Tarshish from before Yahweh

1:4
and Yahweh he hurled a great wind to the sea
and it was a great storm on the sea
and the ship it thought to be broken

1:5
and the mariners were afraid
and they cried for help each to his gods
and they hurled the vessels which were in the ship into the sea to lighten
from upon them and Jonah he had gone down into the innermost parts of the ship
and he had lain down
and he had fallen asleep

1:6
and he drew near to him the chief of the sailors
and he said to him what is to you sleeping
arise call to your god
perhaps he will give thought the god to us and not we will perish

1:7
and they said each to his neighbor come and let us cast lots
so that we may know by which of whom this evil belongs to us
and they cast lots
and the lot fell on Jonah

1:8
and they said to him tell us please
because of whom this evil belongs to us
what occupation is your and from where do you come
what land is your and where from this people are you

1:9
and he said to them I am a Hebrew
and Yahweh the God of the heavens I am fearing who he made the sea and the dry land

1:10
and the men feared great fear
and they said to him what this have you done
for they knew the men that from before Yahweh he was fleeing
because he had told to them

1:11
and they said to him what will we do to you
so that it will be quiet the sea from upon us for the sea was going and storming

1:12
and he said to them pick me up and hurl me into the sea
and it will be quiet the sea from upon you
for I am knowing that by which of me
the great storm this is on you

1:13
and the men rowed to bring back to the dry land
and not they were able for the sea was going and storming on them

1:14
and they called out to Yahweh
and they said please! O Yahweh please do not let us perish by the life of this man
and do not put on us blood innocent for you O Yahweh just as you pleased
you have done

1:15
and they picked up Jonah
and they hurled him into the sea
and the sea stopped from its storming

1:16
and the men feared great fear of Yahweh
and they offered a sacrifice to Yahweh
and they vowed
