4:1
and it was displeasing to Jonah
displeasure great and it burned to him

4:2
and he prayed to Yahweh
and he said
we beg you, O Yahweh not was this my word until was I on land of my own
therefore I did the first time to flee to Tarshish
for I knew that you are a God gracious and compassionate
long of anger(s) and great of covenant loyalty
and who relents on evil

4:3
and therefore O Yahweh
take please my life from me for is good my death more than my life

4:4
and Yahweh said
rightly (do well) does it burn to you

4:5
and Jonah went out from the city
and he sat from east of the city
and he made for himself there a shelter
and he sat under it in the shade
until that he will see what will it be in the city

4:6
and Yahweh God appointed a plant
and it went up over Jonah to be shade over his head
to deliver him from his distress and he rejoiced Jonah on the plant a joy great

4:7
and God appointed a worm when came up the dawn to the next day
and it attacked the plant
and it withered

4:8
and it was just as arose the sun and he appointed God a wind an east wind hot
and it struck the sun on the head of Jonah
and he became faint
and he asked his life to die
and he said
is good my death more than my life

4:9
and God said to Jonah
rightly (do well) does it burn to you on the plant
and he said
rightly (do well) it burns to me to death

4:10
and Yahweh said
you had compassion on the plant which not you had toiled in it and not you had made it grow
which a son of a night it was
and a son of a night it perished

4:11
and I not will I have compassion on Nineveh the great city
which there are in it many more than two plus ten ten thousand persons who not he distinguishes between his hand right and his left
and livestock
much
