2:1
and he appointed Yahweh a great fish
to swallow Jonah
and he was Jonah in the inward parts of the fish
three days and three nights

2:2
and he prayed Jonah
to Yahweh his God
from the inward parts of the fish

2:3
and he said
I called from my distress to Yahweh and he answered me
from the belly of Sheol I cried for help you heard my voice

2:4
and you have thrown me the deep in the heart of the seas
and a river it surrounds me
all your breakers and your waves over me they have passed

2:5
and I said
I have been driven away from before your eyes
nevertheless I will repeat to look
to your holy temple

2:6
they have encompassed me waters to the neck
the deep it surrounds me
rushes is wrapped round to my head

2:7
to extremities of mountains I went down
the earth its bars were behind me forever
and you brought up from the pit my life O Yahweh my God

2:8
when fainted away on me my life
O Yahweh, I remembered
and it came to you my prayer
to your holy temple

2:9
those who pay regard vanities of nothingness
their loyalty they abandon

2:10
and I
with a voice of thanksgiving I will sacrifice to you
that which I have vowed I will fulfill
rescue belongs to Yahweh

2:11
and he spoke Yahweh to the fish
and it vomited up Jonah to the dry land
