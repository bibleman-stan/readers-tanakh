7:1
and the king went and Haman to drink with Esther the queen

7:2
and the king said to Esther also on the day second at the feast of (the) wine
what petition is your O Esther the queen and it will be given to you
and what request is your up to (the) half of the kingdom and it will be done

7:3
and Esther answered the queen and she said
if I have found favor in your eyes O king and if is on the king good let it be given to me my life for my petition
and my people for my request

7:4
for we have been sold I and my people to annihilate
to kill and to destroy
and if to male slaves and to female slaves we had been sold I kept quiet
because not the distress is equal for the injury of the king

7:5
and the king said Ahasuerus
and he said to Esther the queen
who he is this one and where this is he
whom it has filled him his heart
to do thus

7:6
and Esther said
a man a foe and an enemy Haman evil this
and Haman he was terrified from before the king and the queen

7:7
and the king he rose up in his rage from the feast of (the) wine into the garden of the palace
and Haman he remained to seek on his life from Esther the queen
for he saw
that it was determined against him harm from with the king

7:8
and the king he returned from the garden of the palace to the house of the feast of (the) wine and Haman was falling
on the couch which Esther was on it and he said the king
also to violate the queen with me in the house the word it came out from the mouth of the king and the face of Haman people covered

7:9
and he said Harbona one of the eunuchs before the king also there! the gallows which he has made Haman for Mordecai who he spoke good on the king is standing at the house of Haman
high
fifty cubits
and the king said
hang him on it

7:10
and they hanged Haman on the gallows which he had prepared for Mordecai
and the rage of the king it subsided
