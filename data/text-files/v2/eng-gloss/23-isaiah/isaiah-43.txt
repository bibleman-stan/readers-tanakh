43:1
and now thus he says Yahweh who created you O Jacob
and who formed you O Israel do not be afraid for I have redeemed you I have called you by your name belong to me you

43:2
when you will pass in the waters will be with you I
and in the rivers
they will not overflow you
when you will walk in fire you will not be scorched
and flame it will not burn you

43:3
for I am Yahweh your God
the holy one of Israel your deliverer
I have given your ransom Egypt Cush and Seba in place of you

43:4
because you are precious in my eyes you are honored and I love you
and I will give humankind in place of you
and peoples in place of your life

43:5
do not fear for am with you I from the east I will bring your offspring
and from the west I will gather you

43:6
I will say to the north deliver up
and to the south do not withhold
bring my sons from a distance
and my daughters from the end of the earth

43:7
every one who is called by my name
and whom for my glory I created him
whom I formed him also I made him

43:8
bring out a people blind and eyes there are and deaf people
and ears belong to them

43:9
all the nations they have assembled together and they gather peoples
who among them will he declare this
and former things will they proclaim to us let them appoint their witnesses so that they may be justified
and let them hear
and let them say truth

43:10
you witnesses are my the utterance of Yahweh
and my servant whom I have chosen
so that you may know and you may believe me and you may perceive that I am he
before me it was not formed a god
and after me it will not be

43:11
I am Yahweh
and there is not except me a deliverer

43:12
I have declared and I have saved and I have proclaimed
and there was not among you a strange god and you witnesses are my the utterance of Yahweh and I am God

43:13
also from a day I am he and there is not from my hand
a deliverer I act
and who will he revoke it

43:14
thus says Yahweh your redeemer the holy one of Israel
for your sake I have sent to Babylon and I will bring down in boats of them all
and the Chaldeans in the ships of their shout of their joy

43:15
I am Yahweh
your holy one the one who created Israel your king

43:16
thus says Yahweh who made in the sea
a way and in waters mighty a path

43:17
who sent out chariotry and horses
an army and a strong one
together they lay down they will not rose they were extinguished like wick they were quenched

43:18
do not remember former things
and ancient things do not consider

43:19
here I am about to do a new thing now it is springing up do you not know it
also I will make in the wilderness a way in a desolate place rivers

43:20
it honors me the animals of the field jackals
and daughters of an ostrich
because I have put in the wilderness water rivers in a desolate place
to give drink to my people one my chosen

43:21
a people which I formed for myself my praise they will recount

43:22
and not me you have called on O Jacob
but you have grown weary with me O Israel

43:23
you have not brought to me the sheep of your burnt your offerings
and your sacrifices you have not honored me I have not burdened you with offerings
and I have not made you weary with frankincense

43:24
you have not bought for me for money calamus
and the fat of your sacrifices you have not satisfied me
nevertheless you have burdened me with your sins
you have made me weary with your iniquities

43:25
I am he who is wiping out your transgressions for my own sake
and your sins
I will not remember

43:26
cause me to remember let us enter into judgment
together recount you
so that you may be justified

43:27
your ancestor first he sinned and your spokesmen they transgressed against me

43:28
and I will profane the officials of holiness and I will give to total destruction Jacob
and Israel to reviling words
