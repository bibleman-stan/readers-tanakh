25:1
O Yahweh are my God you
I will exalt you I will give thanks to your name for you have done wonders
plans from distant time faithfulness

25:2
for you have made from a city into heap a town fortified into a ruin
a fortress of strangers from a city for ever
not it will be rebuilt

25:3
therefore they will honor you a strong people
a town of nations ruthless they will fear you

25:4
for you have been a refuge for the poor person a refuge for the needy person when it was distress to him
a shelter from rainstorm a shade from heat
for the breath of ruthless people is like a rainstorm of a wall

25:5
like heat in a dry land
the uproar of strangers
you will subdue heat in the shade of a cloud
the song of ruthless people he will quieten

25:6
and will make Yahweh of hosts for all the peoples on this mountain
a feast of fat things a feast of old wine
fat things full of marrow old wine refined

25:7
and he will swallow up on this mountain of the surface of the covering which covers over all the peoples
and the woven stuff which is woven over all the nations

25:8
he will swallow up death to perpetuity
and will wipe away the Lord Yahweh tears
from on every face and the reproach of his people he will remove from on all the earth
for Yahweh he has spoken

25:9
and someone will say in that day
here! is our this God we have waited for him
and he will save us this is Yahweh we have waited for him let us be glad and let us rejoice in his salvation

25:10
for it will rest of the hand of Yahweh on this mountain
and will be trampled down Moab in its place as is trampled down a heap of straw in a dung hill

25:11
and it will spread out its hands in its midst
just as he spreads them out swimmer to swim
and he will its low bring haughtiness with the tricks of its hands

25:12
and the fortification of the refuge of your walls he will low lay he will bring low it he will make it touch the ground to the dust
