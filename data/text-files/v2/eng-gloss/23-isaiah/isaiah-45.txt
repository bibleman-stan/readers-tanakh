45:1
thus says Yahweh to his anointed to Cyrus whom I have taken hold on his right hand to subdue before him nations
and the loins of kings I will loosen to open before him doors
and gates
not they will be shut

45:2
I before you I will go
and the mountainous land(s)



45:3
and I will give to you treasures of darkness
and hidden treasures of secret places
so that you may know that I am Yahweh who calls by your name the God of Israel

45:4
for the sake of my servant Jacob and Israel
one my chosen and I summoned you by your name
I will give you a title and not you know me

45:5
I am Yahweh and there is not yet
besides me there is not a god I will gird you and not you know me

45:6
so that people may know from the rising of the sun
and from its setting place
that nothing is apart from me
I am Yahweh
and there is not yet

45:7
I am forming light and I am creating darkness I am bringing about well-being and I am creating calamity
I am Yahweh
who does all these things

45:8
drip down O heaven above and clouds let them flow righteousness
let it open up the earth and let them bear fruit salvation and righteousness let it cause to sprout together I Yahweh I have created it

45:9
woe to! one who contends with the [one who] formed him
a potsherd
with potsherds of the ground
does it say clay to the [one who] forms it what are you making and your work there belong not hands to him

45:10
woe to! one who says to a father what are you fathering
and to a woman what are you in labor with

45:11
thus says Yahweh the holy one of Israel and the [one who] formed it
the coming things ask me on my children and on the work my hands
you command me

45:12
I made the earth and humankind on it I created
I my hands they stretched out the heavens
and all their host I commanded

45:13
I have stirred him up in righteousness
and all his ways I will make smooth
he will build my city and my exiles he will let go not for price
and not for a bribe he says Yahweh of hosts

45:14
thus says Yahweh the produce of Egypt and the trading profit of Cush and the Sabeans people of stature
to you they will pass over and to you they will belong
behind you they will walk in fetters
they will pass over
and to you they will bow down to you they will pray
surely is among you God and there is not yet there not are gods

45:15
truly you are a God who hides himself O God of Israel deliverer

45:16
they will be ashamed and also they will be put to shame of them all
together they will walk in ignominy craftsmen of images

45:17
Israel it has been saved by Yahweh
a salvation of everlastingness(es) not you will be ashamed
and not you will be put to shame until everlastingness(es) of perpetuity

45:18
for thus he says Yahweh who created the heavens he is (the) God who formed the earth and who made it he established it
not a wasteland he created it
to dwell in he formed it I am Yahweh and there is not yet

45:19
not in secrecy I spoke in a place of a land of darkness
not I said to the offspring of Jacob emptiness seek me
I Yahweh am speaking righteousness I am declaring uprightness(es)

45:20
gather together and come draw near together O fugitives of the nations
not they know [those who] carry the wood their idol and those who pray to a god
which not it will save

45:21
declare and bring near
also let them take counsel together
who did he proclaim this from antiquity from then did he declare it not was it I Yahweh and there is not again a god except me
a God righteous and a deliverer there is not besides me

45:22
turn to me and be saved O all the ends of the earth for I am God and there is not yet

45:23
by myself I swear it goes forth from my mouth righteousness a word
and not it will return
that to me it will bow down every knee
it will swear every tongue

45:24
only are in Yahweh of me everyone will say righteousness(es) and strength
to him he will go and they will be ashamed all [those who are] angry with him

45:25
by Yahweh they will be justified and they will boast all the offspring of Israel
