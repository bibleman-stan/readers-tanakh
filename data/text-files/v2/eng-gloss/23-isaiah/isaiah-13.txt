13:1
the oracle of Babylon which Isaiah saw the son of Amoz

13:2
on a mountain swept bare lift up a standard lift up a voice to them
wave a hand so that they may go the doors of noble people

13:3
I have commanded to my consecrated ones
also I have summoned my warriors to my anger the exultant ones my pride

13:4
the sound of a tumult is on the mountains
the likeness of a numerous people of the sound of an uproar of kingdoms of nations which are gathering
Yahweh of hosts is mustering
an army of battle

13:5
are coming from a land of distance from the end of the heavens
Yahweh and the weapons of his indignation to ruin all the land

13:6
wail for is near the day of Yahweh like devastation
from the Almighty it will come

13:7
therefore this all hands they will drop
and every heart of a person it will melt

13:8
and they will be terrified pangs and labor-pains they will seize them like woman who gives birth they will be in anguish
each to his neighbor they will look in astonishment will be faces of flames their faces

13:9
there! the day of Yahweh is coming a cruel day and wrath
and burning of anger to make the land a waste and its sinners
he will destroy from it

13:10
for the stars of the heavens and their constellations
they will not flash forth
their light it will be dark the sun when it goes forth and the moon
it will not make shine its light

13:11
and I will visit on the world evil
and on wicked people of their iniquity
and I will put an end to the pride of arrogant people
and the haughtiness of ruthless people I will bring low

13:12
I will make precious humankind more than pure gold and humankind more than gold of Ophir

13:13
therefore heaven I will make tremble and it will shake the earth from its place in the wrath of Yahweh of hosts
and in the day of the burning of his anger

13:14
and it will be like a gazelle hunted
and like sheep and there will not be one who gathers
everyone to his people own they will turn
and everyone to land of his own they will flee

13:15
every one who is found he will be pierced through
and every one who is captured he will fall by the sword

13:16
and their children they will be dashed in pieces to their eyes
they will be plundered their houses
and their wives

13:17
here I am about to rouse on them Media
who silver they will not value
and gold they will not delight in it

13:18
and bows
youths they will dash in pieces
and the fruit of the womb they will not have compassion on children it will not look with pity their eye

13:19
and it will be Babylon the beauty of kingdoms the splendor of the pride of the Chaldeans like the overthrow of God Sodom and Gomorrah

13:20
it will not remain to perpetuity and it will not settle until a generation and a generation
and he will not pitch tent there an Arab
and shepherds they will not make lie down there

13:21
and they will lie down there desert-dwellers and they will be full houses of their jackals
and they will dwell there daughters of an ostrich
and goats they will leap about there

13:22
and it will sing hyenas in its palaces and jackals in the palaces of delight
and is near to go its time and its days
they will not be prolonged
