55:1
alas! O every thirsty one come to the waters and O one who there belongs not to him money
come buy grain and eat
and come buy grain with not money
and with not price
wine and milk

55:2
why do you weigh out money for not bread and your toil for not to satiety
listen carefully to me and eat good things
so that it may take delight in fatness your appetite

55:3
incline your ear and come to me listen
so that it may live your self
and I will make to you a covenant of perpetuity the covenant loyalti of David reliable

55:4
here! a witness of peoples I made him a leader and one who commands peoples

55:5
there! a nation which you will not know you will call
and a nation which they have not known you to you they will run on account of Yahweh your God
and to the holy one of Israel
for he has glorified you

55:6
seek Yahweh when he lets himself be found call on him when is he near

55:7
let him forsake a wicked person his way and a person of wickedness his thoughts
and let him return to Yahweh so that he may have compassion on him
and to God our for he will increase to forgive

55:8
for not thoughts my thoughts are your and not ways are your my ways
declares Yahweh

55:9
for they are high the heavens more than the earth so they are high my ways more than your ways
and my thoughts more than your thoughts

55:10
for just as it comes down the rain and the snow from the heavens and to it will not returns
but if it has given water to the earth
and it has caused it to bring forth
and it has made it sprout
and it has given seed to the sower
and bread to the eater

55:11
thus it is my word which it goes forth from my mouth it will not return to me empty
but if it has done that which I desired
and it has prospered that which I sent it

55:12
for in joy you will go out and in peace
you will be led
the mountains and the hills they will break forth before you a shout of joy
and all the trees of the field they will clap a palm

55:13
in place of the thornbush it will grow up a cypress
