33:1
woe to! the destroyer and you not have been destroyed and the traitor
and not people have dealt treacherously with him
when finishing you destroying you will be destroyed when completing you to deal treacherously people will deal treacherously with you

33:2
O Yahweh show favor to us
for you we have waited be their strength to the mornings also our salvation in a time of distress

33:3
from the sound of tumult they flee peoples from your arising they scatter nations

33:4
and it will be gathered your plunder gathering of locusts like the rushing of locusts someone will be rushing on it

33:5
is exalted Yahweh
for he is dwelling a high place he will fill Zion justice
and righteousness

33:6
and will be the steadiness of your times wealth of deliverance
wisdom and knowledge the fear of Yahweh
it will be his treasure

33:7
there! their valiant one they have cried out the toward outside messengers of peace bitter
they weep !

33:8
they have been made desolate highways he has ceased one who passes by of a path
someone broken has a covenant someone has rejected cities
not anyone has valued humankind

33:9
it has dried up it has languished the land it has displayed shame Lebanon
it has withered it has become Sharon like desert plain
and is shaking off Bashan and Carmel

33:10
now I will arise he says Yahweh
now I will exalt myself
now I will lift myself up

33:11
you will conceive dry grass
you will give birth to stubble your breath is a fire
which it will consume you

33:12
and they will be peoples burnings of lime thornbushes cut down
with fire they will be kindled

33:13
hear O distant ones that which I have done
and know O near ones my strength

33:14
they are in dread in Zion sinners it has seized trembling godless people
who? will he sojourn of us a fire consuming who? will he sojourn of us
hearths of perpetuity

33:15
one who walks righteousness and one who speaks uprightness one who rejects unjust gain of extortion one who shakes out his hands from taking hold on bribe
one who shuts his ear from hearing blood
and one who shuts his eyes from looking on evil

33:16
he high places he will dwell will be strongholds of cliffs
his refuge his food will be given his water will be reliable

33:17
a king in his beauty they will see your eyes they will see a land of distances

33:18
your heart it will meditate on terror where? is one who counted where? is one who weighed
where? is one who counted the towers

33:19
a people barbarous
not you will see a people too unintelligible of lip for hearing
stammering of tongue there not is understanding

33:20
see Zion the town of appointed our feast
your eyes they will see Jerusalem a habitation undisturbed a tent which not anyone will pack up which not anyone will pull out tent of its pegs to perpetuity
and all its ropes not they will be torn apart

33:21
that except there will be majestic Yahweh for us a place of rivers canals
broad of both hands not it will go on it a ship of rowing
and a ship majestic not it will pass along it

33:22
for Yahweh is our judge
Yahweh is our commander
Yahweh is our king
he will deliver us
