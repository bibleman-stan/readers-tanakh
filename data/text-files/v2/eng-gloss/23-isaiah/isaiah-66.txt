66:1
thus says Yahweh the heavens throne are my
and the earth is the footstool my my feet
where this is the house which you will build for me
and where this is the place my rest

66:2
and all these things my hand it made
and they were all these things the utterance of Yahweh
and to this one I will look
to an afflicted person and a person stricken of spirit
and a person trembling on my word

66:3
one who slaughters ox one who strikes down (of) a person one who sacrifices lamb one who breaks the neck of a dog
one who offers up (of) a grain offering the blood of a pig one who offers as a memorial frankincense
one who blesses wickedness
also they they have chosen (in) their own ways
and (in) their detestable things
their self it has delighted

66:4
also I will choose their punishments and their fears I will bring to them
because I called and there was not one who answered I spoke and not they listened
and they did the evil in my eyes
and (in) that which not I desired they chose

66:5
hear the word of Yahweh O people trembling to his word
they have said your relatives who hate you who exclude you for the sake of my name let him be glorified Yahweh
so that we may look on your joy
and they they will be ashamed

66:6
the sound of an uproar from the city a sound
from the temple the sound of Yahweh repaying recompense to his enemies

before she was in labor she gave birth
before it came labor-pains to her
and she delivered a male

her children

I do I bring to the birth and not I cause to bring forth he says Yahweh
or am I the [one who] causes to bring forth and do I restrain he says your god

66:10
rejoice with Jerusalem and be glad in it
O all [you who] love it rejoice with it joy O all [you who] mourn on it

66:11
so that you may suck and you will be satisfied from the breast its her consolations
so that you may suck and you will take delight from the nipple its her abundance

for thus he says Yahweh here I am about to extend to it like a river peace
and like a torrent overflowing the abundance of nations and you will suck
on a hip you will be carried and on knees
you will be fondled

66:13
like a person whom his mother she comforts him
so I will comfort you and in Jerusalem
you will be comforted

66:14
and you will see and it will exult your heart
and your bones

and it will be made known the hand of Yahweh with his servants
and he will be indignant
his enemies

for there! Yahweh in fire he will come and [will be] like the storm-wind
his chariots to bring back with rage his anger
