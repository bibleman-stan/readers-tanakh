62:1
for the sake of Zion not I will be silent
and for the sake of Jerusalem
not I will remain quiet
until it will go forth like brightness its righteousness and its salvation
like a torch which it burns

62:2
and they will see nations of your righteousness
and all kings of your glory
and it will be called to you a name new which the mouth of Yahweh
it will designate it

62:3
and you will be a crown of beauty in the hand of Yahweh

62:4
not it will be said of you again abandoned
and of your land not it will be said again desolation
but to you it will be called Hephzibah
and to land of your married for he delights Yahweh in you
and your land it will be married

62:5
for he marries a young man a young woman they will marry you your sons
and the joy of a bridegroom on a bride
he will rejoice on you your god

62:6
on your walls O Jerusalem I have appointed watchmen all the day and all the night continually
not they will be silent O you who cause to remember Yahweh let not rest belong to you

62:7
and do not give rest to him until he will establish
and until he will make Jerusalem praise on the earth

62:8
he swears an oath Yahweh by his right hand and by the arm his strength
if I will give grain of your again food to your enemies
and if they will drink sons of foreignness your wine new which you have labored for it

62:9
but [those who] gather it they will eat it
and they will praise Yahweh
and [those who] gather it they will drink it in the courts my holiness

62:10
pass pass in the gates
make clear
the way of the people cast up cast up the highway clear it from stones
raise a standard over the peoples

62:11
there! Yahweh he has proclaimed to the end of the earth say to the daughter of Zion
there! your salvation is about to come
there! his reward is with him
and his wages is before him

62:12
and people will call them the people of (the) holiness those redeemed of Yahweh
and to you it will be called sought out
a city which not it has been forsaken
