64:1
as kindles fire brushwood water it boils fire to make known your name
to your foes from before you nations they will quake

64:2
when did you awesome deeds which we were not waiting for you came down from before you
mountains they shook

64:3
and from long ago not people have heard not people have given ear
an eye it has not seen a God besides you
who he acts for one who waits for him

64:4
you meet one who rejoices and one who does righteousness in your ways
they remember you
there! you were angry and we sinned
in them long duration and will we be saved?

64:5
and we have become like unclean person of us all
and are like a garment of menstruation
all our righteousness and we have withered like leafage of us all
and iniquities our like the wind they carry us off

64:6
and there is not one who calls on your name one who rouses himself to take hold on you
for you have hidden your face from us and you have melted us in the hand of our iniquity

64:7
and now O Yahweh father are our you
we are the clay and you are the one who formed us and are the work of your hand of us all

64:8
do not be angry O Yahweh up to muchness
and do not forever remember iniquity here! look please people are your of us all

64:9
the holy your cities
they have been a wilderness of Zion a wilderness it has been Jerusalem a desolation

64:10
the house of our holiness and our splendor where they praised you our ancestors
it has become a burning of fire
and all things our desirable it has become a ruin

64:11
on these things will you restrain yourself
O Yahweh will you be silent and will you afflict us up to muchness
