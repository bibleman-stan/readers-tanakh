26:1
on that day it will be sung the song this in the land of Judah
a city of strength belongs to us salvation he sets walls and a rampart

26:2
open the gates
so that it may enter a nation righteous which keeps faithfulness(es)

26:3
an inclination firm you will keep peace because in you he is trusting

26:4
trust in Yahweh until perpetuity for is (in) Yahweh a rock of everlastingness(es)

26:5
for he will lay low the inhabitants of a high place a town set on high
he will bring it low he will bring it low to the ground he will make it touch to the dust

26:6
it will trample it a foot of the feet of the afflicted the footsteps of poor people

26:7
the way of the righteous is level paths O upright one the track of the righteous
you make level

26:8
also the way your judgments O Yahweh
we have waited for you is for your name and for your memorial the desire of self

26:9
my self I have desired you in the night
also my spirit in my inner being I will seek you earnestly
for when your judgments are to the earth righteousness they learn the inhabitants of the world

26:10
he is shown favor of the wicked not he learns righteousness
in a land of straightforwardness(es) he acts unjustly
and not he sees the majesty of Yahweh

26:11
O Yahweh it is raised your hand not they behold
they will behold and they will be ashamed the zeal of a people also the fire your foes it will consume them

26:12
O Yahweh you will establish peace for us
for also all our works you have done for us

26:13
Yahweh our God they have ruled over us lords
except you only in you we bring to remembrance your name

26:14
dead ones not they will live shades not they will rise
therefore you have visited and you have destroyed them
and you have destroyed all remembrance of them

26:15
you have added to the nation O Yahweh
you have added to the nation
you have gained glory you have extended all the boundaries of the land

26:16
O Yahweh in distress people sought you
they poured out a whisper your chastening was to them

26:17
like a pregnant woman who she brings near to give birth she is in labor she cries out in her labor-pains
so we were from before you O Yahweh

26:18
we were pregnant we were in labor like we gave birth to wind
deliverance(s) not we accomplish the earth and not they fall the inhabitants of the world

26:19
they will live your dead ones my corpse
they will rise
awake and shout for joy O you who dwell of dust for will be the dew of lights your dew
and the earth the shades it will make fall

26:20
come my people go in your rooms and shut


26:21
for there! Yahweh is about to go forth from his place to visit the iniquity of the inhabitants of the earth
