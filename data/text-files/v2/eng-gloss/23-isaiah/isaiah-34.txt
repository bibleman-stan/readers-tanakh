draw near O nations to hear and O peoples
pay attention
let it hear the earth and what fills it
the world
and all its produce(s)

34:2
for wrath belongs to Yahweh towards all the nations
and rage towards every their army
he will totally destroy them
he will deliver them up to slaughter

34:3
and their slain ones they will be thrown out
and their corpses it will go up their stench
and they will melt away mountains from their blood

34:4
and they will rot away all the host of the heavens
and they will be rolled up like scroll
the heavens
and all their host it will wither
as withers leafage from a vine and like withered fruit from a fig tree

34:5
when it has drunk its fill in the heavens
my sword
there! on Edom it will come down and on the my people total my destruction for judgment

34:6
a sword belongs to Yahweh it is full blood it has fattened itself from fat
from the blood of lambs and goats from the fat of the kidneys of rams
for a sacrifice belongs to Yahweh in Bozrah
and a slaughter great
in the land of Edom

34:7
and they will go down wild oxen with them
and young bulls with mighty bulls
and it will drink its fill their land from blood
and their dust
from fat it will be fattened

34:8
for a day of vengeance belongs to Yahweh
a year of retribution(s)
for the cause of Zion

34:9
and they will be changed its torrents into pitch
and its dust into sulfur and it will become land of its pitch burning

34:10
night and by day not it will be extinguished forever
it will go up its smoke
from generation to generation it will be desolate
to perpetuity of perpetuities there will not be one who passes in it

34:11
and they will take possession of it desert owl and hedgehog
and great owl and raven
they will dwell in it
and he will stretch out over it a measuring-line of formlessness
and stones of emptiness

34:12
its nobles and there will not be there
that which a kingship people will call
and all its princes they will be nothing

34:13
and it will grow up fortresses its thorns
nettles and thornbushes [will be] on its fortifications
and it will be a habitation of jackals
grass
for daughters of an ostrich

34:14
and they will meet desert-dwellers with hyenas
and goat
to its companion it will call
surely there it will rest of the night-creature and it will find for itself a resting place

34:15
there it will make a nest arrow snakes and it laid eggs and it will hatch
and it will gather a brood in its shadow
surely there they will be gathered red kites
each its companion

34:16
seek from upon the scroll of Yahweh and read
one of them not it will be missing
each its companion not they will miss
for my mouth it it has commanded
and his spirit
it it will gather them
