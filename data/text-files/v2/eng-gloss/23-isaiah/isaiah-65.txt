65:1
I let myself be consulted by those who they had not asked
I let myself be found by those who they had not sought me
I said here I am here am to a nation which it had not been called by my name

65:2
I spread out my hands all the day to a people rebellious who walk the way not good
after thoughts their own

65:3
the people who provoke to anger me on my face continually who sacrifice in gardens
and who make smoke on the bricks

65:4
those who sit among the graves and in secret places
they spend the night those who eat the flesh of the pig

65:5
those who say draw near to yourself do not draw near to me for I am set apart from you
these people are smoke in my nose
a fire which burns all the day

65:6
here! it is written before me I will not be silent but rather I will repay
and I will repay on their bosom

65:7
your iniquities and the iniquities of your ancestors your together
Yahweh says who they made smoke on the mountains
and on the hills
they taunted me and I will measure their wages first

65:8
thus says Yahweh just as it is found the new wine in the cluster of grapes
and someone says do not destroy it for blessing is in it
so I will act for the sake of my servants to not to destroy everyone

65:9
and I will bring out from Jacob offspring and from Judah one who will possess my mountains
and they will take possession of it ones my chosen
and my servants they will dwell there

65:10
and it will become Sharon a pasture of sheep
and the valley of Achor a resting place of cattle
for my people who they have sought me

65:11
and you who forsake Yahweh forgetting
the mountain of my holiness
who arrange for Gad a table
and who fill for Meni mixed wine

65:12
and I will appoint you to the sword
and all of you to the slaughter you will bow down because I called and you will not answered
I spoke and you will not listened
and you did the evil in my eyes
and that which I will not desired you chose

65:13
therefore thus he says Lord the Yahweh here! my servants they will eat and you will be hungry
here! my servants they will drink
and you will be thirsty
here! my servants they will rejoice
and you will be ashamed

65:14
here! my servants they will shout for joy from goodness of heart
and you will cry out from pain of heart
and from brokenness of spirit you will wail

65:15
and you will leave your name to a curse to my chosen ones
and he will put you to death Lord the Yahweh
and his servants he will call a name another

65:16
that the one who blesses himself in the land he will bless himself by the God of surely
and the one who swears an oath in the land he will swear an oath by the God of surely
because they will be forgotten the troubles former
and they will be hidden from my eyes

65:17
for here I am about to create heavens new
and an new earth
and they will not be remembered the former things
and they will not come up on a heart

65:18
then rather rejoice and be glad until perpetuity that which I am about to create
for here I am about to create Jerusalem gladness
and people of its joy

65:19
and I will be glad in Jerusalem
and I will rejoice in my people
and it will not be heard in it again a sound of weeping and a sound of a cry of distress

65:20
it will not be from there again a sucking child of days and an old person who he will not complete his days
for the young man a son of one hundred years he will die and the one who misses
a son of one hundred years he will be cursed

65:21
and they will build houses and they will dwell in them and they will plant vineyards and they will eat their fruit

65:22
they will not build and another he will dwell they will not plant
and another he will eat
for will be like the days of tree the days of my people and the work of their hands
they will use to the full ones my chosen

65:23
they will not labor to emptiness and they will not father children for sudden terror
for will be the offspring of those blessed of Yahweh they and their descendants
will be with them

65:24
and it will be before they will call
and I will answer
still they will be speaking and I will hear

65:25
wolf and lamb they will graze as one and the lion like the ox it will eat straw and a snake
will be dust its food they will not do harm
and they will not destroy in all the mountain of my holiness
