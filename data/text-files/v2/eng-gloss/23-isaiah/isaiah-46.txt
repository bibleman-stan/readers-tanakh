46:1
he has bowed down Bel is stooping low Nebo
they belong their idols to the animals
and to the cattle your burdens are carried a load for a weary animal

46:2
they have stooped low they have bowed down together
not they have been able to rescue a burden
and their self in captivity it has gone

46:3
listen to me O house of Jacob
and all the remnant of the house of Israel
[who have been] carried from the belly [who have been] borne from the womb

46:4
and until old age I am he and until gray hair I will carry
I made and I will bear
and I will carry
and I will rescue

46:5
to whom will you liken me and will you make me equal and will you compare me so that we may be alike

46:6
[those who] pour out gold from a bag and silver
on scales they weigh out
they hire a metalsmith and he makes it a god they prostrate themselves also they bow down

46:7
they lift it up on a shoulder they carry it and they set it down in its place and it stands from its place
not it departs
also he cries out to it and not it answers from his trouble not it delivers him

46:8
remember this
and pluck up courage
bring it back O rebels on heart

46:9
remember former things from long ago
that I am God and there is not yet
God and there not is like me

46:10
who declares from the beginning the end
and from antiquity things which not they have been done
who says my purpose it will be fulfilled
and all my desire I will accomplish

46:11
who calls from the east a bird of prey from a land of distance
a man

46:12
listen to me O people mighty of heart O people distant from righteousness

46:13
I have brought near my righteousness not it is far away and my salvation
not it will delay
and I will give in Zion salvation to Israel my splendor
