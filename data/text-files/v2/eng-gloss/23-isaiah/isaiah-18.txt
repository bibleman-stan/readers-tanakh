18:1
woe to! a land of whirring of wings which is from the other side of the rivers of Cush

18:2
the [one which is] sending on the sea envoys and in vessels of papyrus on the surface of the waters
go O messengers swift to a nation tall and smooth skinned
to a people feared from it and beyond
a nation of strength of strength and trampling
which they divide rivers
its land

18:3
O all the inhabitants of the world
and those who dwell of earth just as lifts up a standard mountains you will see it
and just as sounds a trumpet you will hear it

18:4
for thus he said Yahweh to me



18:5
for before harvest just as is finished buds and unripe grapes ripening
it will become blossom
and he will cut off the shoots with (the) pruning knives
and the tendrils
he will remove he will cut away

18:6
they will be abandoned alike to birds of prey of the mountains and to the animals
of the earth and it will pass the summer on it the birds of prey
and every (the) animal of the earth on it it will pass the winter

18:7
at that time it will be brought a gift to Yahweh of hosts a people tall and smooth skinned
and from a people feared from it and beyond
a nation of strength of strength and trampling which they divide rivers of its land
to the place of the name of Yahweh of hosts
the mountain of Zion
