58:1
call out with a throat do not refrain like trumpet raise your voice
and declare to my people their transgression
and to the house of Jacob their sins

58:2
and me day day they seek
and the knowledge of my ways they desire
like a nation which righteousness it has done
and the judgment of its his God not it has forsaken
they ask me judgments of righteousness
the approach of God they desire

58:3
why have we fasted and not you have seen have we humbled our self and not you know
there! on the day of your fasting you find pleasure
and all your laborers you drive hard

58:4
there! for strife
and contention you fast
and to strike with a fist of wickedness not you fast like this day to make heard in the heights your voice

58:5
like this is it the fast which I choose it a day to humble anyone his self
to bow down like a bulrush his head and sackcloth and ashes he spreads as a bed this do you call a fast
and a day of acceptance to Yahweh

58:6
not is this the fast which I choose it to loosen the bonds of wickedness
to unfasten the bindings of the yoke-bar
and to let go those who are crushed free
and every yoke-bar you will tear apart

58:7
not is it to break for the hungry person your bread
and poor people homeless people you will bring a house when you will see the naked
and you will cover him
and from your own flesh
not you will hide yourself

58:8
then it will break forth like the dawn of your light and your healing
quickly it will spring up
and it will go before you your righteousness the glory of Yahweh it will be your rearguard

58:9
then you will call and Yahweh he will answer
you will cry for help
and he will say here I am
if you will remove from among you the yoke-bar the stretching out of a finger and the speaking of wickedness

58:10
and you will offer to the hungry person your self
and a person afflicted you will satisfy
and it will rise in the darkness of your light and your gloom will be like the noon

58:11
and he will lead you Yahweh continually
and he will satisfy in arid regions your appetite
and your bones he will strengthen
and you will be like a garden watered
and like a spring of waters which not they will fail its waters

58:12
and they will rebuild some of you the ruins of antiquity the foundations of a generation and a generation
you will raise up
and it will be called to you one who walls up a breach one who restores paths
to dwell in

58:13
if you will turn back from the sabbath your foot to do your pleasures on the day of my holiness
and you will call the sabbath a delight the holy day of Yahweh honorable
and you will honor it from doing your ways own from finding your pleasure and to speak a word

58:14
then you will take delight on Yahweh
and I will make you ride on the high places of the land and I will feed you the inheritance of Jacob your ancestor for the mouth of Yahweh it has spoken
