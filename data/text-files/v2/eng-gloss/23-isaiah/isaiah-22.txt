22:1
the oracle of the valley of vision
what is to you then that you have gone up of you all to the roofs

22:2
noises full O city turbulent O town exultant
your slain ones not are slain of a sword and not they are dead of battle

22:3
all your rulers they have fled together
from a bow they have been captured
all those found in you they were captured together from a distance they had fled

22:4
therefore I said look away from me I will show bitterness with weeping
do not hasten to comfort me on the devastation of the daughter my people

22:5
for a day of panic and trampling
and confusion belongs to the Lord Yahweh of hosts in the valley of vision breaking down a wall
and crying out to the mountain

22:6
and Elam it took up a quiver with chariotry of a man horsemen
and Kir it laid bare a shield

22:7
and it was the choicest your valleys they were full chariotry and the horsemen powerfully (take a stand)
they took a stand to the gate

22:8
and he uncovered the covering of Judah
and you looked in that day to the weaponry of the house of the forest

22:9
and the breaches of the city of David you saw
that they were many and you gathered the waters of the pool lower

22:10
and the houses of Jerusalem
you counted and you pulled down the houses
to fortify the city wall

22:11
and a reservoir you made between the two walls for the water
of the pool old and not you looked to the [one who] made it and the [one who] planned it from distant time
not you considered

22:12
and Lord called the Yahweh of hosts in that day for weeping and for wailing and for baldness
and for girding of sackcloth

22:13
and there! joy and gladness they are killing cattle and they are slaughtering sheep
they are eating meat
and they are drinking wine
we will eat and we will drink
for tomorrow we will die

22:14
and he has revealed himself in my ears Yahweh of hosts
if it will be atoned for the iniquity this for you until you will die
says the Lord Yahweh of hosts

22:15
thus he says Lord the Yahweh of hosts
come go to the steward this to Shebna who is over the house

22:16
what belongs to you here
and who belongs to you here
that you have dug out for yourself here a tomb he is digging out of a high place his tomb
he is cutting in the rock a dwelling for himself

22:17
there! Yahweh is about to hurl you away hurling O man
and [he is] about to grasp you firmly (grasp)

22:18
tightly (wind up) he will wind you up a winding a ball to a land broad of both sides
there you will die and [will be] (to) there the chariots your splendor O disgrace of the house your master(s)

22:19
and I will drive you from your office
and from your position he will tear you down

22:20
and it will be in that day
and I will summon my servant Eliakim the son of Hilkiah

22:21
and I will clothe him your tunic and your sash I will make firm on him and your authority I will put in his hand
and he will become (into) a father of the inhabitants of Jerusalem
and of the house of Judah

22:22
and I will put the key of the house of David on his shoulder
and he will open and there will not be one who shuts
and he will shut and there will not be one who opens

22:23
and I will drive him a tent peg in a place firm
and he will become (into) a seat of honor for the his house his father

22:24
and they will hang on him all the honor of the his house his father the descendants and the offshoot(s) all the vessels of the small from the vessels of the bowls and unto all the vessels of the jars

22:25
on that day of the utterance of Yahweh of hosts it will be removed the tent peg [which had been] driven
in a place firm
and it will be cut down and it will fall and it will be cut off the burden which was on it
for Yahweh he has spoken
