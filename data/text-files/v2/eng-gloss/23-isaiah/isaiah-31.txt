31:1
woe to! those who go down Egypt for help on horses
they depend
and they have relied on chariotry because it is many
and on horsemen because they are numerous exceedingly and not they look on the holy one of Israel and Yahweh not they seek

31:2
and also he is wise and he has brought calamity and his words
not he has turned aside
and he will rise up on the house of evil-doers
and on the help of those who do wickedness

31:3
and Egypt is humankind and not God
and their horses are flesh and not spirit
and Yahweh he will stretch out his hand and he will stumble the one who helps and he will fall the one who is helped and together
of them all they will come to an end

31:4
for thus he has said Yahweh to me just as it growls the lion and the young lion over its prey that it is summoned on it a multitude of shepherds
from their voice not it is dismayed and from their tumult
not it cringes
so he will come down Yahweh of hosts
to go to war on the mountain of Zion and on its hill

31:5
like birds flying so he will defend Yahweh of hosts over Jerusalem
he will defend it and he will rescue it he will pass over it and he will deliver it

31:6
return to the one whom people have made deep rebellion O people of Israel

31:7
for in that day
they will reject everyone the idols of his silver and the idols of his gold which they have made for you hands of your sin

31:8
and it will fall Assyria by a sword not a human
and a sword not a human it will consume it
and it will flee for itself from before a sword and its young men
forced labor they will become

31:9
and its rock from terror it will pass away
and they will be dismayed from a standard its commanders the utterance of Yahweh who fire belongs to him in Zion and an oven belongs to him in Jerusalem
