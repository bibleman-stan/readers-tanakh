54:1
shout for joy O barren woman
who not she has given birth break forth a shout of joy and cry aloud O one who not she has been in labor
for are many the children of a desolate woman more than the children of a married woman
he says Yahweh

54:2
enlarge the place of your tent and the tent curtains of your dwellings may people stretch out may not you refrain
long make tent of your cords and tent of your pegs strong make

54:3
for right and left you will spread out
and your offspring nations he will take possession of
and cities desolated they will cause to be inhabited

54:4
may not you be afraid for not you will be put to shame and may not you be ashamed
for not you will display shame
for the shame of your youth you will forget
and the reproach of your widowhood not you will remember again

54:5
that your husband is your maker is Yahweh of hosts
his name
and is your redeemer the holy one of Israel
the God of all the earth he is called

54:6
for like a wife abandoned and grieved of spirit he will call you Yahweh
and a wife of youth if she is rejected
he your says God

54:7
for a small moment I abandoned you and in great compassion I will gather you

54:8
in a flood of wrath I hid my face a moment from you
and in covenant loyalty of perpetuity I will have compassion on you
he says your redeemer Yahweh

54:9
for is the water of this Noah to me
which I swore from passing the waters of Noah again over the earth
so I swear from being toward angry you
and from rebuking you

54:10
that the mountains they will be removed
and the hills they will shake
and covenant of my loyalty from with you not it will be removed
and the covenant of my peace not it will shake
he says one who has compassion on you Yahweh

54:11
O afflicted one it is storm tossed
not it has been comforted
here! I am about to set in antimony your stones
and I will refound you with sapphires

54:12
and I will make ruby your battlements
and your gates
stones of beryl
and all your border stones of delight

54:13
and all your children will be taught of Yahweh
and will be great
the peace of your children

54:14
in righteousness you will be established be distant from oppression for not you will be afraid
and from terror for not it will draw near to you

54:15
there! certainly anyone will quarrel not from me whoever is quarreling with you
on you he will fall

54:16
here! I created a craftsman who blows on a fire of charcoal
and who produces a weapon for its work
and I created a destroyer to ruin

54:17
any weapon which it will be formed on you not it will succeed
and every tongue which it will arise with you to judgment you will condemn as guilty
this is the inheritance of the servants of Yahweh and their righteousness from with me
the utterance of Yahweh
