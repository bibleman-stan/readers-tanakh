54:1
shout for joy O barren woman
who she has not given birth break forth a shout of joy and cry aloud O one who she has not been in labor
for are many the children of a desolate woman more than the children of a married woman
Yahweh says

54:2
enlarge the place of your tent and the tent curtains your dwellings may people stretch out do not refrain
make long your tent-cords and your tent pegs make strong

54:3
for right and left you will spread out
and offspring of your nations he will dispossess
and cities desolated they will cause to be inhabited

54:4
do not be afraid for you will not be put to shame and do not be ashamed
for you will not display shame
for the shame of your youth you will forget
and the reproach of your widowhood you will not remember again

54:5
for is your husband your maker is Yahweh of hosts his name
and is your redeemer the holy one of Israel
the God of all the earth he is called

54:6
for like a wife who has been abandoned and who is grieved of spirit he will call you Yahweh
and a wife of youth when she is rejected
he your says God

54:7
for a small moment I abandoned you
and in compassion great I will gather you

54:8
in a flood of wrath I hid my face a moment from you
and in covenant loyalty of perpetuity I will have compassion on you
he says your redeemer Yahweh

54:9
for is the waters of Noah this to me
which I swore from passing the waters of Noah again over the earth
so I swear from being angry towards you
and from rebuking you

54:10
for the mountains they will be removed
and the hills they will shake
and my covenant loyalty from with you it will not be removed
and the covenant of my peace it will not shake
he says one who has compassion on you Yahweh

54:11
O afflicted one which it is storm-tossed
which it has not been comforted
behold, I am about to set in antimony your stones
and I will refound you with sapphires

54:12
and I will make ruby your battlements
and your gates
stones of beryl
and all border of your stones of delight

54:13
and all your children will be disciples of Yahweh and will be great
the peace of your children

54:14
in righteousness you will be established be distant from extortion for you will not be afraid
and from terror for it will not draw near to you

54:15
there! actually anyone will stir up trouble not from me whoever is stirring up trouble with you
on you he will fall

54:16
behold, I

54:17
any weapon which it will be formed on you it will not succeed
and every tongue which it will arise with you to judgment you will condemn as guilty
this is the inheritance of the servants of Yahweh and their righteousness from with me the utterance of Yahweh
