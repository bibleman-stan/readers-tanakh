11:1
if was a youth Israel
and I loved him and from Egypt I summoned my son

11:2
they summoned them
so they went from before them to the Baals they sacrificed
and to the idols they made smoke !

11:3
and I taught to walk Ephraim he took them on his arms
and not they knew that I had healed them

11:4
with cords of humankind I drew them with ropes of love
and I was to them like those who lift a yoke on their jaws
and I incline to him I feed him

11:5
not he will return to the land of Egypt and Assyria
it will be his king for they have refused to repent

11:6
and it will whirl a sword in his cities
and it will put an end to his bars
and it will devour them from their schemes

11:7
and my people is hung up to my apostasy
and to a height they call on him together
not he will exalt them

11:8
how? will I give up you O Ephraim will I deliver up? you O Israel
how? will I make you like Admah
will I make? you like Zeboiim
it has been changed on me my heart
together they have grown warm my compassions

11:9
not I will carry out the burning of my anger not I will repeat
to destroy Ephraim
for am God I
and not a human in your midst the holy one
and not I will come in rage

11:10
after Yahweh they will walk like a lion he will roar
if he will roar
and they may tremble children from the west

11:11
they will tremble like a bird from Egypt
and like a dove
from the land of Assyria
and I will cause to dwell them at their houses the utterance of Yahweh
