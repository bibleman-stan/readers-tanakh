9:1
may not you rejoice O Israel to rejoicing like the peoples
for you have prostituted yourself from with your God
you have loved the hire of a prostitute on all threshing floors of grain

9:2
threshing floor and wine-press not it will feed them and new wine
it will fail it

9:3
not they will remain in the land of Yahweh
and will return Ephraim Egypt and in Assyria
unclean food they will eat

9:4
not they will pour out to Yahweh wine and not they will be pleasing to him
their sacrifices will be like bread of mourning to them
all those who eat it they will make themselves unclean for their bread will be for their self
not it will go the house of Yahweh

9:5
what? will you do to the day of an appointed feast
and to the day of the festival of Yahweh

9:6
for there! they have gone from the devastation Egypt it will gather them
Memphis it will bury them treasure of their silver nettles it will take possession of them thorns will be in their tents

9:7
they have come the days of punishment they have come the days of retribution
let them know Israel
is a fool the prophet is mad the person of the spirit on the greatness of your iniquity
and great animosity

9:8
was a watchman Ephraim with my God
a prophet a trap of a fowler is on all his ways animosity is in the house of his God

9:9
they have made deep they have dealt corruptly like the days of Gibeah
he will remember their iniquity
he will punish their sins

9:10
like grapes in the wilderness I found Israel like early figs on a fig tree at its beginning
I saw your ancestors
they came Baal Peor and they devoted themselves to shame
and they were detestable things as loved they

9:11
Ephraim like bird it will fly away their glory from child bearing
and from womb
and from conception

9:12
that except they will bring up their children
and I will bereave them from everyone for also woe! to them when depart I from them

9:13
Ephraim just as I have seen to Tyre
is planted in pastureland and Ephraim to bring out to one who kills his children

9:14
give to them O Yahweh what? will you give to them a womb which miscarry and breasts which dry up

9:15
all their wickedness is in Gilgal for there I hated them on the wickedness of their deeds
from my house I will drive out them
not I will repeat to love them all their leaders are rebellious

9:16
he has been struck Ephraim their root it has dried up fruit not they will produce !
also if they masc will give birth !
and I will put to death the precious things of masc their womb

9:17
he will reject them my God for not they have listened to him
so they may be wanderers among the nations
