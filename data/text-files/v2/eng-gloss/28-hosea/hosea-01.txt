1:1
the word of Yahweh which came to Hosea the son of Beeri
in the days of Uzziah Jotham Ahaz Hezekiah the kings of Judah
and in the days of Jeroboam the son of Joash the king of Israel

1:2
the beginning when Yahweh spoke by Hosea
and Yahweh said to Hosea
go take for yourself a wife of prostitution and children of prostitution
for continually it prostitutes itself the land from after Yahweh

1:3
and he went and he took Gomer
the daughter of Diblaim and she conceived and she bore to him a son

1:4
and Yahweh said to him
call his name Jezreel
for yet a little and I will visit the blood of Jezreel on the house of Jehu
and I will put an end to the kingdom of the house of Israel

1:5
and it will be on that day
and I will break the bow of Israel in the valley of Jezreel

1:6
and she conceived again
and she bore a daughter
and he said to him
call her name Lo-Ruhamah Lo-Ruhamah
for not I will repeat again I will have compassion on the house of Israel
that certainly I will forgive them

1:7
and the house of Judah I will have compassion on
and I will save them by Yahweh God their and not I will save them by bow and by sword
and by battle by horses and by horsemen

1:8
and she weaned Lo-Ruhamah Lo-Ruhamah
and she conceived
and she bore a son

1:9
and he said
call his name Lo-Ammi Lo-Ammi for you not people are my and I
not I will belong to you
