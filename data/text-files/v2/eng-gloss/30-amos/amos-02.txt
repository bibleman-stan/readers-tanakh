2:1
thus says Yahweh on three (the) transgressions of Moab
and on four not I will revoke it because burned he the bones of the king of Edom into lime

2:2
and I will send forth a fire on Moab and it will consume
the fortresses of (the) Kerioth
and it will die in uproar Moab with a shout of alarm with the sound of a trumpet

2:3
and I will cut off a judge from its midst
and all its officials I will kill with him he says Yahweh

2:4
thus says Yahweh on three (the) transgressions of Judah
and on four not I will revoke it
because they have rejected the law of Yahweh and his statutes not they have kept
and they have misled them their lies which they walked their ancestors after them

2:5
and I will send forth a fire on Judah and it will consume
the fortresses of Jerusalem

2:6
thus says Yahweh on three (the) transgressions of Israel
and on four not I will revoke it
because sold they for silver the righteous and the needy because of a pair of sandals

2:7
[those who] trample on the dust of the earth on the head of poor people
and the way of humble people
they turn aside
and a man and his father they go into the young woman so as to profane
the name my holiness

2:8
and on garments taken as pledges they stretch out beside every altar and the wine of fines they drink
the house their god

2:9
and I destroyed the Amorites from before them who was like the height of cedars his height
and is strong he like oaks
and I destroyed his fruit above
and his roots beneath

2:10
and I brought up you from the land of Egypt
and I led you in the wilderness forty years to take possession of
the land of the Amorites

2:11
and I raised up some of your sons to prophets
and some of your young men to Nazirites
also not is this O people of Israel
declares Yahweh

2:12
and you made drink the Nazirites wine
and to the prophets you commanded to not you must prophesy

2:13
behold, I am about to make totter under you
just as it totters the cart full (for itself cut grain

2:14
and it will be lost a place of escape from a swift person
and a strong person not he will make firm his power
and a mighty person not he will save his life

2:15
and the one who handles (of) the bow not he will stand
and a person swift on his feet
not he will save
and the rider of the horse
not he will save his life

2:16
and the person strong his heart among the warriors
