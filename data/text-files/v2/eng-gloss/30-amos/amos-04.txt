4:1
hear this word O cows of Bashan who are on the mountain of Samaria
who oppress poor people who crush
needy people who say to their masters
bring so that we may drink

4:2
he has sworn Lord the Yahweh by his holiness
surely here! days are coming on you and someone will take away you with barbs
and your posterity with hooks of fishing

4:3
and breaches you will go out each before herself
and you will throw to Harmon the utterance of Yahweh

4:4
come Bethel Bethel and transgress Gilgal increase
to transgress
and bring to the morning of your sacrifices to three days of your tithes

4:5
and make smoke from leaven a thank-offering
and proclaim freewill offerings make them heard
for thus you love O people of Israel the utterance Lord of the Yahweh

4:6
and also I gave to you cleanness of teeth in all your cities and lack of bread
in all your places
and you will not returned to me the utterance of Yahweh

4:7
and also I withheld from you the rain in yet three months were to the harvest
and I sent rain on a city one
and on a city one
I will not sent rain
a portion of ground one it was rained on
and a portion of ground which it will not sent rain on it dried up

4:8
and they staggered two three cities to a city one
to drink water
and they were not satisfied
and you will not returned to me the utterance of Yahweh

4:9
I struck you with blight
and with mildew
to make many your gardens and your vineyards and your fig trees and your olive trees it ate the locust
and you will not returned to me the utterance of Yahweh

4:10
I sent among you pestilence in the manner of Egypt
I killed by the sword of your men young with the capture of your horses
and I caused to go up the stench of your camp in your nose
and you will not returned to me the utterance of Yahweh

4:11
I overthrew among you like the overthrow of God Sodom and Gomorrah
and you were like a piece of firewood plucked out from the burning
and you will not returned to me the utterance of Yahweh

4:12
therefore thus I will do to you O Israel
consequence that this I will do to you be prepared to your meet God O Israel

4:13
for here! one who formed mountains and one who created wind
and one who tells to humankind what thought is his
one who makes dawn darkness
and one who treads on the high places of the earth is Yahweh God of hosts his name
