9:1
I saw the Lord standing on the altar and he said strike the capital so they may shake the thresholds
and break off them on the head of all of them and their posterity
by the sword I will kill not he will flee of them one who flees
and not he will escape of them an escapee

9:2
if they will dig in Sheol from there my hand it will take them
and if they will go up the heavens from there I will bring down them

9:3
and if they will hide themselves on the top of Carmel
from there I will search for them and I will take them
and if they will hide themselves from before my eyes at the bottom of the sea
from there I will command the snake
and it will bite them

9:4
and if they will go in captivity before their enemies
from there I will command the sword
and it will kill them
and I will set my eye on them for evil and not for good

9:5
and the Lord Yahweh of hosts who touches the earth and it melted
and they mourn all the inhabitants in it
and it rises like the River all of it
and it sinks like the River of Egypt

9:6
who builds in the heavens his stairs and his vault on the earth he has founds it
who summons the waters of the sea and he poured out them on the surface of the earth is Yahweh his name

9:7
¿ not like the sons of the Cushites are you to me O people of Israel
the utterance of Yahweh
¿ not Israel did I bring up from the land of Egypt
and the Philistines from Caphtor and Aram from Kir

9:8
here! the eyes of the Lord Yahweh are on the kingdom sinful
and I will destroy it from on the surface of the ground
nevertheless for not certainly I will destroy the house of Jacob the utterance of Yahweh

9:9
that here! I am about to command and I will shake among all the nations the house of Israel
just as it is shaken in sieve
and not it will fall a pebble the ground

9:10
by the sword they will die
all the sinners of my people who say not it will bring near and it may meet through us the calamity

9:11
on that day I will raise up the booth of David falling
and I will wall up their breaches and his ruins I will raise up and I will rebuild it like the days of antiquity

9:12
so that they may dispossess the remnant of Edom and all the nations which it is called my name
on them the utterance of Yahweh
who is about to this do

9:13
here! days are coming the utterance of Yahweh
and will approach the plowman the reaper
and the treader of grapes the sower of the seed
and will drip the mountains sweet wine
and all the hills they will flow with it

9:14
and I will turn back the captivity of my people Israel
and they will rebuild cities desolated and they will dwell in them
and they will plant vineyards
and they will drink their wine
and they will make gardens
and they will eat their fruit

9:15
and I will plant them on their own land
and not they will be plucked up again from on their land which I have given to them
