6:1
woe to! the people at ease in Zion
and [those who] trust in the mountain of Samaria
the dignitaries of the first of the nations and they will come to them
the house of Israel

6:2
pass over Calneh and see and go from there Hamath-Rabbah and go down Gath of the Philistines [are they] good more than the kingdoms these or great territory is their more than your territory

6:3
[those who] push away
(to) a day of calamity
and you have brought near a seat of violence

6:4
[those who] lie on beds of ivory
and people sprawled out on their couches
and those who eat lambs from the flock
and calves from the midst of the stall

6:5
[those who] improvise on the mouth of the lyre like David
they have devised for themselves instruments of song

6:6
[those who] drink in bowls of wine
and the best of oils
they smear
and not they were sickened on the ruin of Joseph

6:7
therefore now they will go into exile at the head of those who go into exile
and it will come to an end of the revelry of sprawled out people

6:8
he has sworn Lord the Yahweh by his self the utterance of Yahweh God of hosts
I am loathing the pride of Jacob and his fortresses I hate
and I will deliver up the city and what fills it

6:9
and it shall be, if ten men in a house one and they will die

6:10
and he will lift him up one his loved
and the [one who] burns him to bring out bones from the house
and he will say to the one who is in the innermost parts of the house still [is anyone] with you and he will say no
and he will say hush!
because not to bring to remembrance (in) the name of Yahweh

6:11
for here! Yahweh is commanding and someone will strike the great house fragments
and the house small rubble(s)

6:12
do they run on the crags horses or does anyone plow with oxen
but you have turned into poison justice and the fruit of righteousness into wormwood

6:13
the joyful people
to Lo Debar who say not by our own strength have we taken for ourselves Karnaim

6:14
for here I am about to raise up on you O house of Israel the utterance of Yahweh the God of hosts
a nation and they will oppress you from Lebo Hamath to the wadi of Arabah
