5:1
hear this word which I am lifting up on you a lamentation
O house of Israel

5:2
she has fallen not she will repeat to rise the virgin of Israel
she has been abandoned on land of her own there is not [one who] her raises up

5:3
for thus he says Lord the Yahweh
the city which goes out a thousand it will have left a hundred
and which goes out a hundred it will have left ten of the house of Israel

5:4
for thus he says Yahweh to the house of Israel seek me
and live

5:5
and do not seek Bethel and Gilgal not you must go
and Beer Sheba not you must pass over
for Gilgal certainly (go into exile) it will go into exile
and Bethel it will become trouble

5:6
seek Yahweh
and live
lest he should rush like fire the house of Joseph
and it will consume and there is not one who extinguishes for Bethel

5:7
O you who turn into wormwood justice and righteousness
to the ground they thrust down

5:8
the one who made (of) Pleiades and Orion and the one who turns into morning deep darkness
and day night he makes dark
the [one who] summons (to) the waters of the sea and he poured them out on the surface of the earth is Yahweh his name

5:9
the [one who] causes to flash devastation on the strong
and devastation
on a fortress it will come

5:10
they hate in the gate one who reproves and one who speaks blameless
they abhor

5:11
therefore because trample you on the poor
and tribute of grain you take from him houses of hewn stone you have built
and not you will dwell in them
vineyards of desire you have planted
and not you will drink their wine

5:12
for I know many your transgressions
and numerous
your sins
O foes of the righteous O you who take (of) a ransom and needy people
in the gate they turn aside

5:13
therefore [one who] has insight at the time that
he will be silent
for is a time evil
it

5:14
seek good and not evil
so that you may live
and it will be right Yahweh God of hosts [will be] with you
just as you have said

5:15
hate evil and love good and set up in the gate
justice perhaps he will show favor to Yahweh God of hosts the remnant of Joseph

5:16
therefore thus he says Yahweh God of hosts Lord the
[will be] in all the open places wailing
and in all the streets people will say alas! alas!
and people will call a farmer to mourning
and wailing to those who know (of) mourning songs

5:17
and [will be] in all the vineyards wailing for I will pass in your midst
he says Yahweh

5:18
woe to! [those who] desire
the day of Yahweh
why this to you the day of Yahweh
it will be darkness and not light

5:19
just as he flees someone from before the lion
and it meets him
the bear
and he goes the house and he leans his hand on the wall
and it bites him the snake

5:20
not be darkness will the day of Yahweh
and not light and gloomy
and not brightness [will belong] to it

5:21
I hate I reject your feasts
and not I delight in your assemblies

5:22
even if you will offer up to me burnt offerings and your grain offerings not I will accept them
and the peace offering your fatlings
not I will pay attention to

5:23
remove from with me the noise your songs
and the music your lyres
not I will listen to

5:24
and let it roll on like water justice
and righteousness like a wadi ever-flowing

5:25
sacrifices and grain offerings did you bring near to me in the wilderness forty years O house of Israel

5:26
and you will lift up Sikkuth your king
and Kiyyun your images the star your gods which you made
for yourselves

5:27
and I will send into exile you from beyond Damascus
he says is Yahweh God of hosts his name
