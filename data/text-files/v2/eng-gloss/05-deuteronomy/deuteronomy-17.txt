17:1
not you will sacrifice to Yahweh your God an ox and a sheep which it will be in it a blemish
any thing bad for is abomination of Yahweh your God it

17:2
if he will be found in your midst in one your gates which Yahweh your God is about to give to you a man or a woman who he will do the evil in the eyes of Yahweh your God
by transgressing his covenant

17:3
and he has gone and he has served gods other
and he has bowed down to them
and to the sun or to the moon or to all the host of the heavens which not I have commanded

17:4
and it will be told to you
and you will hear
and you will investigate thoroughly and there! truth is certain the matter
it has been done the abomination this in Israel

17:5
and you will bring out that man or that woman who they have done the thing evil this to your gates the man or the woman
and you will stone them with stones and they will die

17:6
on the mouth of two witnesses or three witnesses
he will be put to death the one about to die
not he will be put to death on the mouth of a witness one

17:7
the hand of the witnesses it will be on him at the first to put him to death
and the hand of all the people at the last
and you will remove the evil from your midst

17:8
if it will be too difficult for you a matter for judgment between blood and blood between dispute and dispute
and between wound and wound
matters of cases at law in your gates
and you will arise and you will go up to the place which Yahweh your God will choose it

17:9
and you will go to the priests the Levites
and to the judge who he will be in the days those
and you will enquire and they will tell to you the word of the verdict

17:10
and you will do on the mouth of the word which they will tell to you from the place that which Yahweh will choose
and you will take care to do according to all that they will instruct you

17:11
on the mouth of the instruction which they will instruct you
and on the verdict which they will say to you will do
not you will turn aside from the word which they will tell to you right hand and left hand

17:12
and the person who he will act with presumption to not to listen to the priest who stands
to serve there Yahweh your God
or to the judge
and he will die the person that and you will remove the evil from Israel

17:13
and all the people they will hear and they will fear and not they will act presumptuously again

17:14
when you will go into the land which Yahweh your God is about to give to you
and you will take possession of it and you will dwell in it
and you will say let me appoint over myself a king like all the nations which are around me

17:15
indeed you will appoint over yourself a king whom Yahweh your God will choose him
from the midst of your countrymen you will appoint over yourself a king
not you will be able to set over yourself a foreign man who not countryman is your he

17:16
only not he will increase for himself horses
and not he will make return the people to Egypt so as to increase horses
and Yahweh he has said to you
not you will repeat to return on the way this again

17:17
and not he will increase for himself wives and not it will turn aside his heart
and silver and gold not he will increase for himself exceedingly

17:18
and it will be just as he sits on the throne of his kingdom
and he will write for himself copy of the law this on a scroll
from before the priests the Levites

17:19
and it will be with him and he will read it all the days of his life
so that he may learn to fear of Yahweh his God
to keep all the words of the law this
and the statutes these to do them

17:20
to not to be lifted up his heart more than his countrymen
and to not to turn aside from the commandment right hand and left hand
so that he may prolong days over his kingdom he
and his descendants in the midst of Israel
