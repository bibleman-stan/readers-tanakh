21:1
if it will be found one slain in the land which Yahweh your God is about to give to you
to take possession of it
lying in the open country not it is known who did he strike him down

21:2
and they will go out your elders
and your judges and they will measure to the cities which are around the one slain

21:3
and the city will be near to the one slain
and the elders will take of that city a heifer of the herd which not it has been worked by it
which not it has dragged by a yoke

21:4
and the elders will bring down of that city the heifer to a wadi ever-flowing
which not it is tilled in it
and not it is sown
and they will break the neck of there the heifer in the wadi

21:5
and they will draw near the priests the sons of Levi
for them he has chosen Yahweh your God
to serve him
and to bless
in the name of Yahweh
and on their mouth it will be every dispute and every wound

21:6
and all the elders of that city near to the one slain
they will wash their hands over the broken heifer necked in the wadi

21:7
and they will answer and they will say
our hands not

21:8
make atonement for your people Israel which you ransomed O Yahweh
and do not put blood innocent in the midst of your people Israel
and it will be atoned for them the blood

21:9
and you will remove the blood innocent from your midst
because you will do the right in the eyes of Yahweh

21:10
when you will go out to war on your enemies
and Yahweh your God will give him in your hand
and you will take captive his captives

21:11
and you will see among the captives a beautiful woman of form
and you will love of her
and you will take her for yourself to a wife

21:12
and you will bring her into the middle of your house
and she will shave her head
and she will attend to her nails

21:13
and she will remove the clothing of her captivity from upon her and she will dwell in your house
and she will weep for her father and her mother a month of days
and after thus you will go into her and you will be her husband
and she will become for you a wife

21:14
and it shall be, if you delight in her and you will let her go for her self
and certainly not you will her sell for money not you will treat as a slave her because that you have her humiliated

21:15
if they will belong to a man two wives of the one is loved and the one is hated
and they will bear to him sons of the loved wife
and the hated wife and he will belong the son the firstborn to the hated wife

21:16
and it will be on the day he causes to inherit his sons that which it will belong to him
not he will be able to treat as firstborn of the son the loved wife on the face of the son of the hated wife of the firstborn

21:17
but the firstborn the son of the hated wife he will acknowledge by giving to him a mouth of two in all that it will be found to him
for he is the beginning of his generative his power belongs to him the claim of the birthright

21:18
if he will belong to anyone a son stubborn and rebellious
not he is listening to the voice of his father
and to the voice of his mother and they will discipline him
and not he will listen to them

21:19
and they will seize him his father and his mother
and they will bring out him to the elders of his city
and to the gate of his place

21:20
and they will say to the elders of his city his our son this is stubborn
and rebellious not he is listening to our voice he is a glutton and a drunkard

21:21
and they will stone him all the men of his city with stones and he will die and you will remove the evil
from your midst and all Israel they will hear and they will fear

21:22
and it will be in anyone a sin of a sentence of death
and he will be put to death and you will hang him on a tree

21:23
not it will remain overnight his corpse on the tree but certainly you will bury him on that day
for is a curse of God one who is hanged
and not you will make unclean your land which Yahweh your God
is about to give to you an inheritance
