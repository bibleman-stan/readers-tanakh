and they will draw near to the place of judgment
and they will judge them
and they will righteous declare the righteous one
and they will condemn as guilty the guilty one

and it will be if is a son of striking the guilty one
and will make lie down him the judge and someone will strike him before him according to the sufficiency of his wickedness by number
