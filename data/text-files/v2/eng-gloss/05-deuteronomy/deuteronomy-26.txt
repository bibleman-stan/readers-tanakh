26:1
and it will be when you will go into the land which Yahweh your god
is about to give to you an inheritance
and you will take possession of it
and you will dwell in it

26:2
and you will take some of the first of all the fruit of the ground which you will bring in from your land which Yahweh your god is about to give to you
and you will put it in the basket
and you will go to the place where he will choose Yahweh your god
to cause to dwell his name there

26:3
and you will go to the priest who he will be
in the days those
and you will say to him I declare this day to Yahweh your god
that I have come into the land which he swore Yahweh to our ancestors
to give to us

26:4
and he will take the priest the basket from your hand
and he will set it down before the altar of Yahweh your god

26:5
and you will answer and you will say before Yahweh your god was an Aramean wandering my ancestor
and he went down to Egypt
and he sojourned there men of fewness
and he became there (into) a nation great
mighty and numerous

26:6
and they did harm to us the Egyptians
and they afflicted us
and they put on us labor hard

26:7
and we cried out to Yahweh the God our ancestors
and Yahweh heard our voice
and he saw our affliction and our toil and our oppression

26:8
and he brought us out Yahweh from Egypt by a hand strong
and by an arm outstretched
and by terror great
and by signs
and by wonders

26:9
and he brought us
to this place and he gave to us this land a land flowing of milk
and honey

26:10
and therefore here! I have brought the first of the fruit of the ground
which you have given to me O Yahweh
and you will set it down before Yahweh your god
and you will bow down before Yahweh your god

26:11
and you will rejoice in all the good which he has given to you Yahweh your god and to your household
you and the Levite
and the sojourner who is in your midst

26:12
when you will finish to tithe all the tithe your produce in the year third
the year of the tithe
and you will give it to the Levite to the sojourner to the fatherless one
and to the widow and they will eat in your gates
and they will be satisfied

26:13
and you will say before Yahweh your god I have removed the holy thing from the house and also I have given it to the Levite
and to the sojourner to the fatherless one
and to the widow according to all your commandment which you have commanded me not I have transgressed any of your commandments
and not I have forgotten them

26:14
not I have eaten in mourning any of it
and not I have removed any of it unclean
and not I have given any of it to the dead
I have listened to the voice of Yahweh my God
I have done according to all that you have commanded me

26:15
look down from the your holy habitation from the heavens and bless your people Israel
and the land which you have given to us
just as you swore to our ancestors a land flowing of milk
and honey

26:16
this day of Yahweh your god is commanding you to observe the statutes these
and the judgments
and you will take care and you will observe them with all your heart and with all being your

26:17
Yahweh you have declared this day to become for you God
and to walk in his ways
and to keep his statutes
and his commandments
and his judgments
and to listen to his voice

26:18
and Yahweh he has declared you this day to become for him (into) a people of possession
just as he spoke to you and to keep all his commandments

26:19
and to set you most high above all the nations which he has made for praise
and for a name
and for honor
and you to be a people holy to Yahweh your god
just as he spoke
