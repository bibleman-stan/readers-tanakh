10:1
at the time that he said Yahweh to me cut out for yourself two tablets of stone(s) like the former tablets
and come up to me to the mountain
and you will make for yourself an ark of wood

10:2
and I will write on the tablets the words
which they were on the tablets former which you broke
and you will put them in the ark

10:3
and I made an ark of wood(s) of acacia
and I cut out two tablets of stone(s) like the former tablets
and I went up to the mountain
and the two the tablets were in my hand

10:4
and he wrote on the tablets like the writing former the ten the words
which he had spoken Yahweh to you on the mountain from the middle of the fire
on the day of the assembly and he gave them Yahweh to me

10:5
and I turned and I came down from the mountain
and I put the tablets in the ark which I had made
and they have been there just as he commanded me Yahweh

10:6
and the people of Israel they set out from Beeroth Bene-Yaaqan Beeroth Bene-Yaaqan Beeroth Bene-Yaaqan Moserah
there he died Aaron
and he was buried there
and he served as priest of Eleazar his son in place of him

10:7
from there they set out to Gudgodah
and from Gudgodah Jotbathah a land of torrents of water

10:8
at the time that he set apart Yahweh the tribe of the Levites
to carry
the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to stand before Yahweh to serve him
and to bless in his name until this day

10:9
therefore not it belongs to Levi a portion and an inheritance with his brothers
Yahweh he inheritance is his
just as he spoke Yahweh your God to him

10:10
and I remained on the mountain like the days former forty days
and forty nights
and Yahweh listened to me also at the time that
not he was willing Yahweh to destroy you

10:11
and Yahweh said to me
arise go to journeying
before the people so that they may go and so that they may take possession of the land which I swore to their ancestors
to give to them

10:12
and now O Israel what is Yahweh your God
asking from with you
but only to fear of Yahweh your God to walk in all his ways
and to love him
and to serve Yahweh your God with all your heart
and with all being your

10:13
to keep the commandments of Yahweh
and his statutes which I am commanding you
this day for good to you

10:14
there! belong to Yahweh your God
the heavens
and the heavens of the heavens
the earth and all that is in it

10:15
only your ancestors he loved Yahweh
to love them
and he chose their offspring after them you from all the peoples as this day

10:16
and you will circumcise the foreskin your heart
and your neck
not you will stiffen again

10:17
for Yahweh your God
he is the God of the gods
and the lord(s) of the lords
the great God mighty and awesome who not he lifts up face
and not he takes a bribe

10:18
he is bringing about judgment of a fatherless one
and a widow
and he is loving a sojourner to give to him food and clothing

10:19
and you will love of the sojourner for sojourners you were in the land of Egypt

10:20
Yahweh your God you will fear
him you will serve
and to him you will cleave and in his name
you will swear oaths

10:21
he praise is your
and he God is your
who he has done with you the great things
and the awesome deeds these which they have seen your eyes own

seventy persons they went down your ancestors
to Egypt and now he has made you Yahweh your God like the stars of the heavens for multitude
