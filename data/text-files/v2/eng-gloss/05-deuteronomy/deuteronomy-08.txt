8:1
all the commandment which I am commanding you this day you will take care to observe
so that you may live and you will increase
and you will go and you will take possession of the land which he swore Yahweh to your ancestors

8:2
and you will remember all the way which he led you Yahweh your god this forty years
in the wilderness
so as to humble you to put you to the test to know that which was in your heart will you keep?


8:3
and he humbled you and he allowed you to hunger
and he caused you to eat the manna which not you had known and not they had known your ancestors
so as to make you know that not on the bread it alone he lives humankind
but on every utterance of the mouth of Yahweh he lives humankind

8:4
your clothing not it wore out from upon you and your foot not it swelled this
forty years

8:5
and you will know with your heart
that just as he disciplines everyone his child Yahweh your god is disciplining you

8:6
and you will keep the commandments of Yahweh your god by walking in his ways
and by fearing him

8:7
for Yahweh your god is about to bring you into a land good
a land of torrents of water
springs and deeps which go forth in the valley and on the mountain

8:8
a land of wheat and barley
and vine and fig tree
and pomegranate
a land of olive tree of oil
and honey

8:9
a land which not in scarcity you will eat in it food
not you will lack anything in it
a land which its stones are iron and from its hills
you will dig copper

8:10
and you will eat
and you will be satisfied and you will bless Yahweh your god on the land good which he has given to you

8:11
take heed to yourself
lest you should forget Yahweh your god to not to keep his commandments
and his judgments and his statutes which I am commanding you
this day

8:12
lest you should eat
and you will be satisfied and houses good you will build
and you will dwell

8:13
and your herd and your flock they will increase and silver and gold it will increase to you
and all that belongs to you it will increase

8:14
and it will be lifted up your heart
and you will forget Yahweh your god
who brought out you from the land of Egypt from a house of slaves

8:15
who led you in the wilderness great and awesome snake fiery and scorpion
and thirsty ground where there was not water who brought forth for you water from the rock of flint

8:16
who caused you to eat manna in the wilderness which not they had known your ancestors
so as to humble you
and so as to put you to the test to do good to you in your end

8:17
and you will say in your heart
my strength and the might my hand
it has acquired for me the wealth this

8:18
and you will remember Yahweh your god
that he is the one who gives to you strength
to acquire wealth
so as to carry out his covenant which he swore to your ancestors as this day

8:19
and it shall be, if you will forget Yahweh your god
and you will walk after gods other
and you will serve them
and you will bow down to them
I testify against you this day that certainly (perish)
you will perish

8:20
like the nations which Yahweh is about to destroy from before you so
you will perish
consequence not you will listen to the voice of Yahweh your god
