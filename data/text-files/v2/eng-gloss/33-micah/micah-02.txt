2:1
woe to! those who plan wickedness and those who evil do on their beds
at the light the morning they do it for there belongs to the power of their hand

2:2
and they desire fields and they seize them
and houses and they take them
and they oppress a man and his household and a man
and his inheritance

2:3
therefore thus he says Yahweh here I am planning on this clan
calamity which not you will remove from there your necks
and not you will walk haughtily for will be a time evil it

2:4
on that day someone will lift up on you a saying and he will wail a wailing it is finished he will say utterly we are devastated
the portion of my people he exchanges
how! he removes it for me
to an apostate our fields he apportions

2:5
therefore not he will belong to you one who casts a measuring-line by lot in the assembly of Yahweh

2:6
may not you prophesy they prophesy ! not they will prophesy of these things not it will be turned back ignominies

2:7
¿ is it said O house of Jacob ¿ is short it the spirit of Yahweh
or? are these his deeds ¿ not my words do they good do with the upright person walking

2:8
and yesterday my people to an enemy he raises up from in front of a garment
a mantle you strip off ! from those who pass by security those who return of war

2:9
the women of my people you drive out ! from the house of her delights
from with her children you take away my glory for ever

2:10
arise and go for this not is resting place
because it has become unclean it will destroy
and the destruction will be grievous

2:11
if anyone walking of wind and falsehood he told a lie
I will prophesy for you of wine
and of strong drink and will be the prophesier of this people

2:12
surely I will gather O Jacob all of you surely I will assemble the remnant of Israel
together I will make it like sheep a sheepfold
like a flock in the midst of its pasture they will be distraught from humankind

2:13
he will go up the one who breaks through before them they will break through and they have passed through
the gate and they have gone out by it
and he has passed on their king before them and Yahweh will be at their head
