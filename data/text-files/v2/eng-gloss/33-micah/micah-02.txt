2:1
woe to! those who plan (of) wickedness and those who do (of) evil on their beds
at the light the morning they do it because (there) it is to the power their hand

2:2
and they desire fields and they seize them
and houses and they take them
and they oppress a man and his household and a man and his inheritance

2:3
therefore thus he says Yahweh here I am planning on the clan this
calamity which not you will remove from there your necks
and not you will walk haughtily for will be a time evil it

2:4
on that day someone will lift up on you a saying and he will wail a wailing it is finished he will say utterly (devastate) we are devastated the portion my people he changes
how! he removes it for me
to an apostate our fields he apportions

2:5
therefore not he will belong to you one who casts a measuring-line by lot in the assembly of Yahweh

2:6
do not prophesy they prophesy not they will prophesy of these things not it will be turned back ignominies

2:7
[is it] said O house of Jacob is it short the spirit of Yahweh
or are these his deeds not my words do they do good with the upright person walking

2:8
and yesterday my people to an enemy he raises up from in front of a garment
a mantle you strip off from those who pass by security those who turn back of war

2:9
the women my people you drive out from the house her delights
from with her children you take away glory of my forever

2:10
arise and go for not this is resting place
because of uncleanness it will ruin
and the ruin will be grievous

2:11
if anyone who walks wind and falsehood he told a lie
I will prophesy for you of wine
and of strong drink and he will be the prophesier of this people

2:12
surely (gather) I will gather O Jacob of you all surely (assemble) I will assemble the remnant of Israel
together I will make it like sheep of a sheepfold
like a flock in the midst of its pasture they will be distraught from humankind

2:13
he will go up the [one who] breaks through before them they will break through and they have passed through
the gate and they have gone out by it
and he has passed on their king before them and Yahweh [will be] at their head
