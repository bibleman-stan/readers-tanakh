6:1
hear please
that which Yahweh is saying arise conduct a case with the mountains
and let them hear the hills
your voice

6:2
hear O mountains of the case of Yahweh
and O enduring ones the foundations of the earth
for a case at law belongs to Yahweh with his people and with Israel
he will argue

6:3
my my people what have I done to you and what have I made you weary
testify against me

6:4
for I brought you up from the land of Egypt and from a house of slaves
I ransomed you
and I sent before you Moses
Aaron and Miriam

6:5
my my people remember please what did he plan Balak the king of Moab
and what did he answer him Balaam the son of Beor from Shittim to Gilgal so as to know
the righteousness(es) of Yahweh

6:6
with what will I come to meet Yahweh
will I bow myself down to the God of height will I come to meet him with burnt offerings with calves sons of a year

6:7
will he take pleasure Yahweh in thousands of rams in tens of thousands
of torrents of oil
will I give firstborn of my my transgression
the fruit my my belly
the sin my my self

6:8
he has told to you O humankind what is good
and what is Yahweh requiring from you surely indeed to do justice and the love of covenant loyalty and to be modest to walk
with your god

6:9
the voice of Yahweh to the city it calls out
and success
it will see your name
hear O tribe
and whoever he appointed it

6:10
still are there house of the wicked
treasures
of wickedness
and an ephah of scantness
accursed

6:11
will I be clean with balances of wickedness and with a bag
of weights of deceit

6:12
which its rich people they are full violence and its inhabitants
they speak falsehood and their tongue
is deceit in their mouth

6:13
and also I
I will make sick by striking you
I will make desolate you on your sins

6:14
you you will eat and not you will be satisfied
and your emptiness [will be] in your midst
and you will carry away and not you will bring to safety
and that which you will bring to safety to the sword I will give

6:15
you you will sow
and not you will reap
you you will tread olives and not you will anoint yourself oil
and new wine
and not you will drink wine

6:16
and it has been kept the statutes of Omri and every (the) deed of the house of Ahab
and you have walked in their schemes
so as I to make you into a horror and its inhabitants into hissing
and the reproach my my people you will bear
