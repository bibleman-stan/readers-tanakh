5:1
and you O Bethlehem Ephrathah are insignificant to be among the families of Judah
from you for me someone will go out to be ruler over Israel
and his origins are from antiquity from days of antiquity

5:2
therefore he will deliver them up until a time when one who is about to give birth she has given birth and the rest of his brothers
they will return with the people of Israel

5:3
and he will stand and he will shepherd by the strength of Yahweh by the majesty of the name of Yahweh his God
and they will remain
for now he will be great to the ends of the earth

5:4
and he will be this one peace Assyria when it will come in our land
and it will tread on our fortresses
and we will raise up on him seven shepherds
and eight princes of man

5:5
and they will shepherd of the land of Assyria with the sword
and the land of Nimrod at its entrances
and he will deliver us from Assyria when it will come in our land
and it will tread in our territory

5:6
and it will be the remnant of Jacob in the midst of peoples many
like dew from with Yahweh like showers on vegetation which it will not waits for anyone
and it will not waits for the children of humankind

5:7
and it will be the remnant of Jacob among the nations in the midst of peoples many
like a lion among the animals of the forest like a young lion
among flocks of sheep which if it has passed through and it tramples and it tears
and there is not a deliverer

5:8
may it be lifted up your hand above your foes
and all your enemies may they be cut off

5:9
and it will be in that day of the utterance of Yahweh
and I will cut off your horses from your midst and I will destroy your chariots

5:10
and I will cut off the cities of your land
and I will tear down all your fortresses

5:11
and I will cut off sorceries from your hand
and soothsayers they will not belong to you

5:12
and I will cut off your images
and your sacred pillars from your midst
and you will not bow down again
to the work of your hands

5:13
and I will pluck up your poles Asherah from your midst
and I will destroy your cities

5:14
and I will do in anger
and in rage vengeance
with the nations which they have not obeyed
