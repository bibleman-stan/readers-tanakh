1:1
the vision of Obadiah
thus he says Lord the Yahweh of Edom a report we have heard from with Yahweh and an envoy among the nations he has been sent
arise and let us arise on it for battle

1:2
here! small I will make you among the nations will be despised you exceedingly

1:3
the presumption your heart it has deceived you
O you who dwell in clefts of rock the height his dwelling he is saying in his heart
who will he bring me down the ground

1:4
if you will make high like eagle and if between the stars will be set your nest
from there I will bring you down the utterance of Yahweh

1:5
if thieves they came to you if destroyers of night
how! you will be destroyed
not will they steal their sufficiency if grape gatherers they came to you not
will they leave gleanings

1:6
how! they will be plundered Esau
they will be searched out treasures its hidden

1:7
to the border they will send you away all the men your covenant
they will deceive you they will prevail to you the men your peace
your food they will set a trap under you there is not understanding in him

1:8
not on that day
declares Yahweh


1:9
and they will be dismayed your warriors O Teman
so that he may be cut off everyone from the mountain of Esau from slaughter

1:10
from the violence your brother Jacob it will cover you shame
and you will be cut off forever

1:11
on the day stood you from before on the day took captive strangers
his wealth
and foreigners they came


1:12
and do not look on the day your brother on the day his misfortune
and do not rejoice to the people of Judah on the day perish they
and do not make big your mouth on the day of trouble

1:13
do not go in the gate my people on the day their disaster do not look also you on his calamity
on the day his disaster
and do not stretch out on his wealth
on the day his disaster

1:14
and do not stand at the parting of ways to cut off his fugitives
and do not deliver up his survivors on the day of trouble

1:15
for is near the day of Yahweh on all the nations
just as you have done it will be done to you
your recompense it will return on your own head

1:16
for just as you have drunk on the mountain my holiness
they will drink all the nations continually
and they will drink and they will swallow
and they will be
like those who not they had been

1:17
and on the mountain of Zion it will be deliverance and it will be a holy place
and they will take possession of the house Jacob their possessions

1:18
and it will be the house of Jacob a fire
and the house of Joseph will be a flame
and the house of Esau [will become] (into) stubble
and they will burn them and they will consume them
and not he will belong a survivor to the house of Esau
for Yahweh he has spoken

1:19
and they will take possession of the Negev the mountain of Esau and the Shephelah Philistines
and they will take possession of the region of Ephraim
and the region of Samaria and Benjamin Gilead

1:20
and the exiles of the fortress this of the people of Israel that which is of the Canaanites to Zarephath
and the exiles of Jerusalem who are in Sepharad
they will take possession of the cities of the Negev

1:21
and they will go up deliverers on the mountain of Zion to rule of the mountain of Esau
and it will belong to Yahweh the kingship
