3:1
and Naomi said to her mother-in-law
my daughter not will I seek for you a resting place that it will go well for you

3:2
and therefore not is Boaz our relative whom you were with servants of his female
there! he will be winnowing the threshing floor of the barley this night

3:3
and you will wash and you will anoint yourself and you will put

3:4
and let it be when he lies down and you will take note of the place where he will lie down there
and you will go and you will uncover his feet

3:5
and she said to her all that you are saying I will do

3:6
and she went down
the threshing floor and she did according to all that she had commanded her mother-in-law

3:7
and Boaz ate and he drank and it was good his heart
and he went to lie down
at the end of the heap and she came in secrecy
and she uncovered his feet
and she lay down

3:8
and it was in the middle of the night
and he trembled the man
and he twisted and there! a woman was lying down his feet

3:9
and he said who are you
and she said I am Ruth your maidservant
and you will spread out your skirt over your maidservant for are a kinsman-redeemer you

3:10
and he said be blessed you by Yahweh my daughter
you have made good loyalty of your latter more than former to not to go after the young men whether poor and or rich

3:11
and therefore my daughter do not fear
all that you are saying I will do for you
for is knowing all the gate of my people that are a woman of ability you

3:12
and therefore though truly that

3:13
stay this night and it will be in the morning if he will redeem you good let him redeem
and if he will not be willing to redeem you and I will redeem you I
by the life of Yahweh lie down until the morning

3:14
and she lay down

3:15
and he said give the shawl which is on you and take hold on it
and she took hold on it
and he measured out six barley
and he put it on her
and the city came

3:16
and she came to her mother-in-law
and she said who are you my daughter
and she told to her all that he had done for her the man

3:17
and she said the six the barley
these he gave to me
for he said do not go empty to your mother-in-law

3:18
and she said sit down my daughter
until that you will know how?
will it fall out a matter
for he will not be at peace the man until when he has finished the matter this day
