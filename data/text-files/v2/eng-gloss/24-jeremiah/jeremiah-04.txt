4:1
if you will return O Israel the utterance of Yahweh to me you will return
and if you will remove detestable your things from before me and not you will waver

4:2
and you will swear by the life of Yahweh in faithfulness in justice
and in righteousness
and they will bless themselves by him nations
and in him they will boast

4:3
for thus he says Yahweh to the man of Judah and to Jerusalem
plow of you unplowed ground
and may not you sow to thorns

4:4
circumcise yourselves to Yahweh and remove the foreskins of your heart O man of Judah
and the inhabitants of Jerusalem lest it should go forth like fire my rage
and it will burn and there not will be one who extinguishes because of the wickedness of your deeds

4:5
declare in Judah and in Jerusalem make a proclamation and say
blow a trumpet in the land
call out fill and say
gather yourselves so let us go to the cities of fortification

4:6
lift up a standard toward Zion take refuge may not you stand still
for calamity I am about to bring from the north
and great destruction

4:7
it has gone up a lion from its thicket and a destroyer of nations he has set out
he has gone out from his place to make your land into a waste
your cities they will fall in ruins from not inhabitant

4:8
on this gird yourself sackcloth mourn and wail
for not it has turned back the burning of the anger of Yahweh from us

4:9
and it will be on that day of the utterance of Yahweh it will perish the heart of the king
and the heart of the officials and will be appalled the priests
and the prophets they will be astounded

4:10
and I said alas! O Lord Yahweh truly certainly you have deceived the people this
and Jerusalem saying
peace it will belong to you
and it will reach a sword to the throat

4:11
at the time that it will be said to the people this
and to Jerusalem
a wind scorching bare heights in the wilderness
will be the direction of the daughter of my people not to winnow
and not to cleanse

4:12
a wind too full for these things it will come for me
now also I will speak judgments with them

4:13
there! like clouds he is going up
and like the storm-wind his chariots they are swift more than eagles his horses
woe! to us
for we are devastated

4:14
wash from your evil heart O Jerusalem so that you may be saved
until when? will it remain in your inner being the thoughts of your wickedness

4:15
for a voice is declaring from Dan
and is proclaiming trouble from the hill country of Ephraim

4:16
make known to the nations here! proclaim to Jerusalem
besiegers are coming from the land of distance
and they have given forth on the cities of Judah their voice

4:17
like those who guard a field
they are on it from round about for me it has been rebellious against the utterance of Yahweh

4:18
your conduct and your deeds
they have brought about these things to you
this is your wickedness for it is bitter
for it has reached to your heart

4:19
inward my parts inward my parts I am waiting the walls of my heart is in turmoil to me my heart
not I will keep silent
for the sound of a trumpet you have heard O my self the shout of battle

4:20
destruction to destruction it has been proclaimed for it has been devastated
all the land suddenly they have been devastated my tents a moment tent of my curtains

4:21
until when? will I see a standard will I hear the sound of a trumpet

4:22
for is a fool my people me not they know
are children fools they
and not are having understanding they are skillful they to evil do
and to good do
not they know

4:23
I looked at the earth
and there! it was formlessness and emptiness
and to the heavens
and there not was their light

4:24
I looked at the mountains and there! they were quaking
and all the hills they shook to and fro

4:25
I looked and there! there not was humankind
and every bird of the heavens they had fled

4:26
I looked and there! the garden-land was wilderness
and all its cities they had been pulled down from before Yahweh from before the burning of his anger

4:27
for thus he says Yahweh
a desolation it will be all the earth and complete destruction
not I will make

4:28
on this it will mourn the earth and will be dark the heavens
above on for I have spoken I have resolved
and not I have repented
and not I will turn back from it

4:29
from the sound of a horseman and a shooter of a bow is fleeing every city people will go in the thickets
and on the rocks people will go up every city is abandoned and there not is dwelling in them anyone

4:30
and you O devastated one what? are you doing that you are dressing scarlet that you ornament yourself ornaments of gold that you open tear with eye-paint your eyes
for vanity you beautify yourself they have rejected you lovers
your life they seek

4:31
for a sound like a sick woman I have heard distress like a woman bearing a first child
the sound of the daughter of Zion she is gasping for breath
she is spreading out her hands woe! please to me for it is weary my self to murderers
