6:1
flee for refuge O people of Benjamin from the midst of Jerusalem
and in Tekoa blow a trumpet and at Beth Hakkerem
raise a signal for calamity it has looked down from the north
and great destruction

6:2
the beautiful one and the dainty one I resemble the daughter of Zion

6:3
to it they will come shepherds
and their flocks they will pitch at it tents all around they will graze everyone his hand

6:4
consecrate on it battle arise
so we may go up at noon
woe! to us for it has turned the day for they are stretched out the shadows of evening

6:5
arise so we may go up in the night and let us destroy its fortresses

6:6
for thus he says Yahweh of hosts cut down wood
and pour out on Jerusalem a mound
that city it will be visited all of it is oppression in its midst

6:7
as keeps cool a well its water so it has kept its cool evil
violence and havoc it is heard in it are on my face continually sickness and wounds

6:8
let yourself be instructed O Jerusalem
lest it should be alienated my self from you lest I should make you a desolation
a land which not it is inhabited

6:9
thus he says Yahweh of hosts
thoroughly people will glean like vine the remnant of Israel
return your hand like a grape gatherer over branches

6:10
to whom? will I speak and will I warn? and they may listen
there! is uncircumcised their ear
and not they are able to pay attention
there! the word of Yahweh it has become for them a reproach not they delight in it

6:11
and the rage of Yahweh I am full I am weary to hold it in
pour it out on children in the street
and on the gathering of young men alike
for also a husband with a wife they will be caught the old with the full of days

6:12
and they will be turned over their houses to others fields and wives alike
for I will stretch out my hand on the inhabitants of the land the utterance of Yahweh

6:13
that from their little and unto their great
all of it is gaining unjust gain
and from prophet
and unto priest
all of him
is doing falsehood

6:14
and they have healed the fracture of my people on a trifling thing saying
peace and there not is peace

6:15
are they ashamed? that an abomination
they have done
neither at all not they are ashamed nor to exhibit shame not they know
therefore they will fall among those who fall at the time when I have visited them they will stumble
he says Yahweh

6:16
thus he says Yahweh stand at the roads and look and ask of paths of ancient time where? this is the way of the good
and walk in it
and find rest for your self and they said not we will walk

6:17
and I raised up over you watchmen
pay attention
to the sound of a trumpet
and they said not we will pay attention

6:18
therefore listen O nations
and know O congregation that which is in them

6:19
hear O earth here! I am about to bring calamity to this people
the fruit of their own schemes for on my words not they have paid attention and my law
and they have rejected it

6:20
why? this to me frankincense which from Sheba it comes
and the good cane from a land of distance burnt your offerings not are for acceptance
and your sacrifices not they are pleasing to me

6:21
therefore thus he says Yahweh
here I am about to give to this people stumbling blocks
and they will stumble over them fathers
and sons alike a neighbor and his friend and they will perish

6:22
thus he says Yahweh there! a people is about to come
from a land of the north
and a great nation
it will be roused from the remotest parts of the earth

6:23
bow and javelin they take hold of is cruel it and not they have mercy
their sound is like the sea which it roars and on horses
they ride
arranged like a man for battle on you O daughter of Zion

6:24
we heard the report of it
they hung limp our hands distress it took hold of us anguish like woman giving birth

6:25
may not you go out the field
and on the road mau not you walk
for a sword belongs to the enemy terror is from round about

6:26
O daughter of my people gird yourself sackcloth and roll in ashes
the mourning of an only child observe for yourself wailing of
bitterness for suddenly it will come the destroyer on us

6:27
an assayer I have made you among my people fortification so you may observe
and you will test their conduct

6:28
all of them are those who turn aside of rebels
those who go about of slander
bronze and iron all of them are destroyers they

6:29
it puffs the bellows from a fire it is complete lead
for vanity someone has refined steadfastly
and evil people
not they have been separated

6:30
silver rejected people called them for he has rejected Yahweh them
