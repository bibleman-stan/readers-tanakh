47:1
that which it came the word of Yahweh to Jeremiah the prophet concerning the Philistines
before he attacked Pharaoh Gaza

47:2
thus says Yahweh here! waters are rising from the north and they will become a torrent overflowing
and they will overflow a land and what fills it a city and those who dwell in it
and they will cry out everyone
and he will wail every inhabitant of the land

47:3
from the sound of the stamping of the hooves of its mighty horses from the rattling of its chariotry the din of its his wheels
they have not turned back fathers to children from slackness of hands

47:4
on the day which is about to come to devastate all the Philistines to cut off to Tyre
and to Sidon
every survivor who helps for is about to devastate Yahweh the Philistines
the remnant of the coastland of Caphtor

47:5
it will come baldness to Gaza
it will be silent Ashkelon
O remnant of their valley until when will you cut yourself

47:6
alas! O sword of Yahweh until when?
you will not be inactive gather yourself not sheath your rest
and be still

47:7
how will you be inactive
and Yahweh he has commanded it against Ashkelon
and against the coast of the sea
there he has appointed it
