19:1
thus he said Yahweh
go and you will buy a bottle of a potter of earthenware
and some of the elders of the people and some of the elders of the priests

19:2
and you will go out to the valley of Ben Hinnom which is at the entrance of the gate of the potsherd
and you will proclaim there the words
which I will speak to you

19:3
and you will say hear the word of Yahweh O kings of Judah and the inhabitants of Jerusalem
thus he says Yahweh of hosts the God of Israel here I am about to bring calamity on this place
which every one who hears of it they will tingle his ears

19:4
because that they have forsaken me
and they have treated as foreign the place this
and they have made smoke in it to gods other which not they knew them they and their ancestors
and the kings of Judah and they have this filled place the blood of innocent ones

19:5
and they have built the high places of Baal to burn their children in the fire
burnt offerings to Baal which not I commanded
and not I spoke and not it came up on my heart

19:6
therefore here! days are coming the utterance of Yahweh
and not it will be called to this place again Topheth
and the valley of Ben Hinnom that
except the valley of slaughter

19:7
and I will make void the plan of Judah and Jerusalem in this place
and I will make fall them by the sword before their enemies
and by the hand of those who seek their life
and I will give their corpses to food
to the birds of the heavens and to the animals of the earth

19:8
and I will this make city into a waste and into a hissing
every one who passes by at it he will be appalled and he may hiss on all its wounds

19:9
and I will make eat them the flesh of their sons and the flesh of their daughters
and everyone the flesh of his neighbor they will eat in the siege
and in the distress which they will press them their enemies
and those who seek their life

19:10
and you will break the bottle to the eyes of the men who go with you

19:11
and you will say to them thus he says Yahweh of hosts thus I will this break people and this city
just as someone breaks the vessel of the potter which not it will be able to be repaired again
and in Topheth people will bury from not room to bury

19:12
thus I will do to this place the utterance of Yahweh and to its inhabitants
and to this make city like Topheth

19:13
and will be the houses of Jerusalem and the houses of the kings of Judah
like the place of Topheth the unclean things
to all the houses which people made smoke on their roofs to all the host of the heavens
and they poured out drink offerings to gods other

19:14
and came Jeremiah from Topheth where he had sent him Yahweh there
to prophesy and he stood in the court of the house of Yahweh
and he said to all the people

19:15
thus he says Yahweh of hosts the God of Israel
here I am about to bring to the city this
and on all its cities
all the calamity which I have spoken
on it for they have stiffened their neck to not to hear my words
