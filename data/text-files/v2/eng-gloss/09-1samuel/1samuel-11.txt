11:1
and he went up Nahash the Ammonite
and he encamped on Jabesh Gilead
and they said all the men of Jabesh to Nahash
make to us a covenant and we will serve you

11:2
and he said to them Nahash the Ammonite
by this I will make to you by gouging out your every eye of the right side
and I will make it a reproach on all Israel

11:3
and they said to him
the elders of Jabesh refrain to us seven days
so that we may send messengers in all the territory of Israel
and if there is not one who delivers us
and we will come out to you

11:4
and the messengers came Gibeah of Saul
and they spoke the words
in the ears of the people and they lifted up all the people their voice
and they wept

11:5
and there! Saul was coming after the cattle from the field
and Saul said what is to the people
that they are weeping
and they recounted to him the words of the men of Jabesh

11:6
and it rushed the spirit of God on Saul

11:7
and he took a pair of oxen and he cut it in pieces
and he sent them in all the territory of Israel by the hand of the messengers
to one who not he is coming out after Saul and after Samuel
thus it will be done to his oxen
and it fell the dread of Yahweh on the people
and they came out like a man one

11:8
and he mustered them at Bezek
and they were the people of Israel three hundred(s) thousand and the man of Judah was thirty thousand

11:9
and they said to the messengers [who had] come
thus you will say to the man of Jabesh Gilead
tomorrow it will belong to you deliverance

11:10
and the men of Jabesh said tomorrow
will we go out to you and you will do to us according to all the good in your eyes

11:11
and it was from the next day and he made Saul the people three companies
and they went in the midst of the camp in the watch of the morning
and they defeated Ammon
until the heat of the day and it was [those who] remained and they were scattered
and not they remained among them two together

11:12
and the people said to Samuel who is the one who said
Saul will he reign over us deliver up the men
so that we may put them to death

11:13
and Saul said
not he will be put to death anyone on this day
for this day he has done Yahweh deliverance in Israel

11:14
and Samuel said to the people
come and let us go Gilgal
and let us renew there the kingship

11:15
and they went all the people Gilgal
and they made king there Saul before Yahweh in Gilgal
and they sacrificed there sacrifices peace offerings
before Yahweh
and he rejoiced there Saul and all the men of Israel up to muchness
