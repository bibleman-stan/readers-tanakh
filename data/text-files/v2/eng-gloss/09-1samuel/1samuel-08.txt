Samuel and he made sons of his judges for Israel

8:2
and it was the his name his son the firstborn Joel
and the his name second his was Abijah judges in Beer Sheba

8:3
and not they walked his sons



8:4
and they gathered together all the elders of Israel
and they came to Samuel to Ramah

8:5
and they said to him here! you have become old and your sons
not they have walked in your ways
now appoint for us a king to judge us like all the nations

8:6
and it was displeasing the matter in the eyes of Samuel
when they said give to us a king to judge us
and Samuel prayed to Yahweh

8:7
and Yahweh said to Samuel
listen to the voice of the people to all that they will say
to you for not you they have rejected
but me they have rejected from reigning over them

8:8
according to all the deeds which they have done from the day I brought up them from Egypt
and until this day
and they have forsaken me
and they have served
gods other so they are doing also to you

8:9
and now listen to their voice
nevertheless indeed certainly (warn) you will warn them
and you will tell to them the rights of the king who he will reign over them

8:10
and Samuel spoke all the words of Yahweh to the people [who were] asking from with him a king

8:11
and he said this it will be the rights of the king who he will reign
over you your sons he will take and he will appoint them for himself in his chariots
and among his horsemen
and they will run before his chariot

8:12
and to appoint them for himself commanders of thousands and commanders of fifties
and to plow his plowing
and to harvest of his harvest
and to make the weapons his warfare
and the equipment(s) his chariotry

8:13
and your daughters
he will take to perfumers
and to cooks
and to bakers

8:14
and your fields and your vineyards and your olive groves good
he will take and he will give them to his servants

8:15
and your seed(s) and your vineyards he will take a tithe
and he will give them to his officials and to his servants

8:16
and servants of your male and servants of your female and your men young good and your donkeys
he will take and he will use them for his work

8:17
your flocks he will take a tithe and you
you will become his servants

8:18
and you will cry out in that day because of your king whom you have chosen for yourselves
and not he will answer Yahweh you in that day

8:19
and they refused the people to listen
to the voice of Samuel and they said no but indeed a king he will be over us

8:20
and we will be also we like all the nations and he will judge us our king and he will go out before us and he will fight
our battles

8:21
and Samuel heard all the words of the people and he spoke them
in the ears of Yahweh

8:22
and Yahweh said to Samuel listen to their voice
and you will make king for them a king and he said Samuel to the people of Israel go
