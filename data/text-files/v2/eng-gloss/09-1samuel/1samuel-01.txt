if really (look) you will look on the affliction your maidservant and you will remember me and not you will forget your maidservant
and you will give to maidservant your offspring of men

and her voice

will you be drunk remove your wine from with you

no my lord am a woman hard of spirit I

do not give your maidservant before a daughter of worthlessness for from the greatness my complaint
