27:1
and said David to his heart
now I will be swept away a day one by the hand of Saul
not belongs to good me for really I will escape to the land of the Philistines and he will desist from me Saul to seek me again in all the territory of Israel
and I will be delivered from his hand

27:2
and arose David
and he passed over he
and six hundred man who were with him to Achish the son of Maoch the king of Gath

27:3
and David dwelt with Achish in Gath he and men of his each one
and his household
David and the two his wives
Ahinoam the Jezreelite woman
and Abigail the wife of Nabal the Carmelite woman

27:4
and it was told to Saul that he had fled David Gath
and not he repeated again to seek him

27:5
and said David to Achish if please I have found favor in your view let people give to me a place in one of the cities of the field
so I may dwell there
and why? will he dwell your servant in the city of royalty with you

27:6
and he gave to him Achish on that day of Ziklag
therefore it has belonged Ziklag to the kings of Judah until this day

27:7
and was the number of the days which he dwelt David in the region of the Philistines days
and four months

27:8
and went up David and his men
and they made raids against the Geshurites and the Gizrites
and the Amalekites
for they were dwelling the land which is from antiquity
coming you toward Shur
and to the land of Egypt

27:9
and struck David the land
and not he let live a man and a woman
and he took sheep and cattle and donkeys and camels and clothes
and he returned
and he went to Achish

27:10
and said Achish against where did you make a raid
this day and said David on the Negev of Judah and on the Negev of the Jerahmeelites
and against the Negev of
the Kenites

27:11
and a man and a woman not he let live David to bring Gath saying
lest they should tell on us saying thus he has done David
and is thus his practice all the days which he has dwelt in the region of the Philistines

27:12
and trusted Achish in David saying certainly he has become odious among his people among Israel
and he will become of me a servant of perpetuity
