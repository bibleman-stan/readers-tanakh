2:1
O my son if you will receive my words
and my commandments
you will treasure with you

2:2
to make attend to wisdom your ear
you will incline your heart
to understanding

2:3
that except to discernment you will call
to understanding
you will give your voice

2:4
if you will seek it like silver
and like treasures you will search for it

2:5
then
you will discern the fear of Yahweh
and the knowledge of God you will find

2:6
for Yahweh he gives wisdom
from his mouth
knowledge and understanding

2:7
he stores up for upright people success
a shield
to those who walk of integrity

2:8
to guard the paths of justice
and the way of faithful his people he will preserve

2:9
then
you will understand righteousness and justice
and uprightness
every course of good

2:10
for it will come wisdom in your heart
and knowledge
to your self it will be pleasant

2:11
discretion it will watch over you
understanding it will preserve you

2:12
to deliver you from a evil way
from anyone
who speaks perverse things

2:13
those who leave paths of uprightness
to walk
in the ways of darkness

2:14
the ones joyful to evil do
they exult
in perverse things of evil

2:15
who their paths are twisted
and who are crooked
in their tracks

2:16
to deliver you from a woman strange
from a foreign woman
who her words she makes smooth

2:17
who leaves of the close friend of her youth
and the covenant of her God she forgets

2:18
for she sank down to death her house
and to the shades
her tracks

2:19
all those who go to her not they return !
and not they reach
the paths of life

2:20
so that
you may walk in the way of good people
and the paths of righteous people you will keep

2:21
for upright people they will dwell the land
and blameless people
they will remain in it

2:22
and wicked people from the land they will be cut off
and treacherous people
they will be torn away from it
