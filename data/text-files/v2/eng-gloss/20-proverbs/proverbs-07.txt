7:1
my son my keep my words and my commandments you will treasure with you

7:2
keep my commandments and live and instruction my like the pupil of your eyes

7:3
bind them on your fingers
write them on the tablet of your heart

7:4
say to wisdom sister are my you and a relative to the discernment you will call

7:5
to keep you from a strange woman from a foreign woman
words who her she makes smooth

7:6
for at the window of my house
through my window-lattice I looked down

7:7
and I saw among the naive people I observed among the sons a youth lacking of heart

7:8
he was passing by in the street beside her corner
and the direction of her house he strode

7:9
at twilight in the evening of the day in the middle of the night and darkness

7:10
and there! a woman to meet him
the garment of a prostitute
and guarded of heart

7:11
is boisterous she and rebellious in her house
not they stay her feet

7:12
a time in the street
a time in the open places
and beside every corner she lies in wait

7:13
and she takes hold on him and she kisses him
she has made firm her face
and she said to him

7:14
sacrifices of peace offerings are on me this day
I have paid my vows

7:15
therefore so I have come out to meet you to seek eagerly your face
and I have found you

7:16
coverings I have spread couch my colored fabrics linen of Egypt

7:17
I have sprinkled bed my myrrh aloes and cinnamon

7:18
come let us take our fill of love until the morning let us delight ourselves with love

7:19
for not the husband is in his house he has gone on a journey from a distance

7:20
the bag of money he has taken in his hand
to the day of the full moon he will come his house

7:21
she turns him with the greatness of her persuasiveness
with the seductiveness of her lips she impels him

7:22
he is walking behind her suddenly
like an ox which to the slaughter it goes
and like an anklet to the chastening of a fool

7:23
until it will pierce an arrow his liver
as hurries a bird into a trap
and not he knows that is for his life it

7:24
and therefore O children listen to me
and be attentive to the words of my mouth

7:25
not let it turn aside to ways of her your heart not let it go astray in her pathways

7:26
for many slain ones she has made fall and are numerous all her killed men

7:27
is the ways of Sheol her house they are going down to the chambers of death
