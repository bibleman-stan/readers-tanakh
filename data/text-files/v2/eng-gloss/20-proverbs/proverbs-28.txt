28:2
by the transgression of a land are many its princes
and by a person who has understanding who knows
the right it will last long

28:3
a poor man and one who oppresses poor people rain which prostrates
and there is not food

28:4
those who forsake the law they praise the wicked
and those who keep the law they engage in strife with them

28:5
people of evil not they understand justice
and those who seek Yahweh they understand everything

28:6
is good one who is poor who walks in his integrity more than a person perverse of two ways
and he is rich

28:7
one who keeps the law is a son who has understanding
and one who associates with gluttons
he shames his father

28:8
one who increases his wealth by interest

28:9
one who turns aside his ear from hearing the law also his prayer is an abomination

28:10
one who leads astray upright people in a way evil
in his own pit he will fall and blameless people they will inherit good

28:11
is wise in his own eyes a rich person
and a poor person who has understanding he examines him thoroughly

28:12
when rejoice righteous people great glory and when arise wicked people
he is searched for everyone

28:13
one who conceals his transgressions not he will prosper
and one who confesses and one who forsakes them he will be shown mercy

28:14
how blessed! is a person who fears continually and one who hardens his heart
he will fall in calamity

28:15
is a lion roaring and a bear rushing a wicked ruler over a poor people

28:16
a ruler lacking of understanding and great of extortion

28:17
a person oppressed by the blood of a person to the pit he will flee not let people support him

28:18
one who walks blameless he will be delivered and one who is crooked of two ways
he will fall at one

28:19
one who tills his land he will be satisfied food
and one who pursues worthless things he will be satisfied poverty

28:20
a person of faithfulness will be great of blessings
and one who hastens to gain riches
not he will go unpunished

28:21
to pay regard to faces not is good and on a piece of bread he will transgress a man

28:22
is hastening to wealth
a person evil of eye
and not he knows that poverty it will come to him

28:23
one who reproves a person after favor he will find more than one who makes smooth the tongue

28:24
one who robs his father and his mother
and one who says there is not a transgression
is a companion he
of a person of destruction

28:25
a person arrogant of self he stirs up strife
and one who relies on Yahweh he will be made fat

28:26
one who trusts in his own heart he is a fool
and one who walks in wisdom
he will be delivered

28:27
one who gives to the poor there is not lack
and one who hides his eyes is great of curses
