5:1
my son to my wisdom be attentive to my understanding
incline your ear

5:2
to preserve discretion and knowledge
your lips they will observe

5:3
for honey they drip the lips of a strange woman
and is smooth more than oil her mouth

5:4
and her end is bitter like wormwood sharp like a sword of mouths

5:5
her feet are going down death Sheol
her steps they will attain

5:6
the path of life lest she should observe
they wander her tracks
not she knows

5:7
and therefore O children listen to me
and do not depart from the words my mouth

5:8
make far away from with her your way
and do not draw near to the entrance her house

5:9
lest you should give to others your vigor
and your years to a cruel person

5:10
lest they should be satisfied strangers your strength
and your hard labors be in the house of a foreigner

5:11
and you will groan at your end
when wastes away your flesh
and your body

5:12
and you will say
how! I hated discipline and rebuke
it spurned my heart

5:13
and not I listened to the my voice my teachers and to [those who] taught me
not I inclined my ear

5:14
like a little I was in every calamity among the assembly and the congregation

5:15
drink water from your own cistern and streams from the midst your own your well

5:16
will they overflow springs your outside to the in the open places streams of water

5:17
let them belong to you alone
and not to strangers with you

5:18
may it be your spring blessed
and rejoice from the wife your youth(s)

5:19
a doe of loves and a mountain goat of grace
her breasts may they satisfy you at every time by love of her
may you be intoxicated continually

5:20
and why will you go astray my son with a strange woman
and will you embrace the bosom of a foreign woman

5:21
for are in front of the eyes of Yahweh the ways of a person
and all his tracks he is observing

5:22
his iniquities they will seize him the wicked person
and by the cords his sin he will be held

5:23
he will die in there is not discipline
and in the greatness his foolishness he will go astray
