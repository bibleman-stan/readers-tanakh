3:1
my son my instruction do not forget and my commandments let it keep your heart

3:2
for length of days and years of life and peace
they will add to you

3:3
loyalty and faithfulness not may they leave you
bind them on your neck
write them on the tablet of your heart

3:4
and find favor and insight good in the eyes of God and humankind

3:5
trust to Yahweh with all your heart
and to understanding your own do not depend

3:6
in all your ways acknowledge him
and he will make straight your paths

3:7
do not be wise in your own eyes fear of Yahweh
and turn aside from evil

3:8
healing may it be to your navel and refreshment to your bones

3:9
honor of Yahweh from your wealth and from the first of all your produce

3:10
so that they may be filled barns your plenty and new wine
your wine-vats they will burst open

3:11
the discipline of Yahweh my son do not reject
and do not loath his rebuke

3:12
for the one whom he loves Yahweh he reproves and like a father
a son whom he is pleased with

3:13
how blessed! is a person who he finds wisdom and a person
who he obtains understanding

3:14
for is good profit her trading more than the trading profit of silver and more than gold her gain

3:15
is precious she

3:16
length of days is in her right hand are in her left hand
wealth and honor

3:17
her ways are ways of kindness
and all her pathways are well-being

3:18
is a tree of life she to those who take hold on her
and those who hold fast to her is called blessed

3:19
Yahweh by wisdom he founded the earth
he established the heavens by understanding

3:20
by his knowledge the depths they were split open and clouds they dripped dew

3:21
my son not let them depart from your eyes keep sound wisdom and discretion

3:22
and they will be life for your self and grace for your neck

3:23
then you will walk to security your way
and your foot
you will not strike

3:24
if you will lie down you will not fear
and you will lie down
and it will be pleasing your sleep

3:25
do not fear from terror suddenly
and from the devastation of wicked people
when it will come

3:26
for Yahweh he will be your confidence
and he will keep your foot from capture

3:27
do not withhold good from its owners
when belongs to the power

3:28
do not say

3:29
do not devise on neighbor your harm
and he is dwelling to security with you

3:30
do not

3:31
do not be jealous of a person of violence
and do not choose all his ways

3:32
for is abomination of Yahweh one who is crooked
and is with upright people of his intimacy

3:33
the curse of Yahweh is on the house of the wicked
and the habitation of righteous people he blesses

3:34
though to the mockers he mocks

3:35
honor wise people they will inherit and fools is lifting up shame
