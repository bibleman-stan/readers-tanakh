3:1
O my son my instruction may not you forget
and my commandments
let it keep your heart

3:2
for length of days and years of life
and peace
they will add to you

3:3
loyalty and faithfulness
may not they leave you
bind them on your necks
write them
on the tablet of your heart

3:4
and find favor and good insight
in the eyes of God and humankind

3:5
trust to Yahweh with all your heart
and to your own understanding
may not you depend

3:6
in all your ways acknowledge him
and he
he will make straight your paths

3:7
may not you be wise in your own eyes
fear of Yahweh
and turn aside from evil

3:8
healing may it be to your navel
and refreshment
to your bones

3:9
honor of Yahweh from your wealth
and from the first of
all your produce

3:10
so they may be filled your storehouses plenty
and new wine
wine your vats they will open burst

3:11
the discipline of Yahweh O my son may not you reject
and may not you loath
his rebuke

3:12
for the one whom he loves Yahweh he reproves
and like a father
a son whom he is pleased with

3:13
how blessed! is a person who he finds wisdom
and a person
who he obtains understanding

3:14
for is her good profit more than the profit of silver
and more than gold
her gain

3:15
is precious she more than jewels
and all your pleasures
not they compare with her

3:16
length of days is in her right hand
are in her left hand
wealth and honor

3:17
her ways are ways of kindness
and all her pathways are well-being

3:18
is a tree of life she to those who take hold on her
and those who hold fast to her are called blessed

3:19
Yahweh
by wisdom he founded the earth
he established the heavens
by understanding

3:20
by his knowledge the depths they were open split
and clouds
they dripped dew

3:21
O my son may not they depart from your eyes
keep sound wisdom
and discretion

3:22
so they may be life for your self
and grace
for your neck

3:23
then you will walk to security your way
and your foot
not it will strike

3:24
if you will lie down not you will fear
and you will lie down
and it will be your pleasing sleep

3:25
may not you be afraid from terror suddenly
and from the devastation of wicked people
that it will come

3:26
for Yahweh he will be your confidence
and he will keep your foot from capture

3:27
may not you good withhold from its owners
when belongs to the power of your hand to do it

3:28
may not you say to your neighbor go and come back and tomorrow I will give it
and there is with you

3:29
may not you devise on your neighbor harm
and he is dwelling to security with you

3:30
may not you contend with anyone without cause
if not he has dealt out to you evil

3:31
may not you be jealous of a person of violence
and may not you choose
all his ways

3:32
for is the abomination of Yahweh a crooked person
and is with upright people of his intimacy

3:33
the curse of Yahweh is on the house of the wicked
and the habitation of righteous people he blesses

3:34
though mockers he mocks
and to humble people
he gives favor

3:35
honor wise people they will inherit
and fools
is lifting up shame
