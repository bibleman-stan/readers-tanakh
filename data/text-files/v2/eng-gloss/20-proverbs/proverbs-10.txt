10:1
the proverbs
of Solomon
a son wise he makes glad a father
and a son a fool
is the grief his mother his

10:2
not they profit treasures of wickedness
and righteousness
it delivers from death

10:3
not he allows to hunger Yahweh the appetite of the righteous
and the desire of wicked people he rejects

10:4
is poor
one who works a palm of slackness
and the hand of diligent people it gains riches

10:5
one who gathers in the summer is a son who acts prudently
one who sleeps at the harvest
is a son who acts shamefully

10:6
blessings belong to the head of the righteous
and the mouth of wicked people
it conceals violence

10:7
the memorial of the righteous is for a blessing
and the name of wicked people it will rot

10:8
a person wise of heart he accepts commandments
and a fool of lips
he will be ruined

10:9
one who walks in integrity he walks security
and one who makes crooked ways his
he will become known

10:10
one who winks an eye he gives sorrow
and a fool of lips
he will be ruined

10:11
is a fountain of life the mouth of a righteous person
and the mouth of wicked people
it conceals violence

10:12
hatred it stirs up contentions
and over all transgressions
it covers love

10:13
on the lips of one who has understanding it is found wisdom
and a rod
is for the back of one lacking of heart

10:14
wise people they store up knowledge
and the mouth of a fool
is ruin near

10:15
the wealth of a rich person is the town his his strength
is the ruin of poor people poverty their

10:16
the wages of the righteous is (to) life
the yield of the wicked is (to) sin

10:17
is a path to life one who keeps discipline
and one who neglects rebuke is leading astray

10:18
one who conceals hatred is lips of falsehood
and one who spreads a bad report
he is a fool

10:19
in a multitude of words not it ceases transgression
and one who restrains lips his is acting prudently

10:20
is silver chosen the tongue of the righteous
the heart of wicked people is like a little

10:21
the lips of the righteous they feed many people
and fools
by lack of heart they die

10:22
the blessing of Yahweh it it makes rich
and not he adds hard labor with it

10:23
is like laughter for a fool to do wickedness
and wisdom
for a person of understanding

10:24
the fear of a wicked person it it will come to him
and the desire of righteous people someone will give

10:25
just as passes by a storm-wind and not the wicked are
and a righteous person
is a foundation of perpetuity

10:26
like vinegar to the teeth
and like smoke to the eyes
is so the sluggard
to [those who] send him

10:27
the fear of Yahweh it adds days
and the years of wicked people they will be short

10:28
the hope of righteous people is joy
and the hope of wicked people it is lost

10:29
is a refuge for integrity the way of Yahweh
and ruin
for those who do (of) wickedness

10:30
the righteous forever not he will be shaken
and wicked people
not they will dwell in the land

10:31
the mouth of a righteous person it will bear fruit wisdom
and a tongue of perversiti(es)
it will be cut off
