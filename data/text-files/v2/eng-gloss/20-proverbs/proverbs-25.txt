25:1
also these are the proverbs of Solomon
which they copied
the men of Hezekiah the king of Judah

25:2
is the glory of God to conceal a matter
and is the glory of kings
to search out a matter

25:3
heaven for height and earth for depth
and the heart of kings
there is not searching

25:4
remove dross(es) from silver
and it came out for the metalsmith a vessel

25:5
remove the wicked before a king
and it will be established in righteousness of his throne

25:6
do not claim honor before a king
and in the great place of people
do not stand

25:7
for is good to say to you
come up
here more than setting you low before a noble person
that which they have seen your eyes

25:8
do not go forth to contest a lawsuit quickly
lest what will you do at its end
when puts to shame you your neighbor

25:9
case your own conduct with your neighbor
and the secret of another person do not reveal

25:10
lest he should bring you shame one who hears
and your bad report
not it will turn back

25:11
apples of gold in carvings of silver a word
spoken on times of its proper

25:12
an earring of gold and an ornament of gold
one who reproves wise
on an ear listening

25:13
is like the cold of snow on a day of harvest
an envoy faithful to [those who] sent him
and the life his masters he will restore

25:14
clouds and wind and rain there is not
a person who boasts in a gift of falsehood

25:15
by length of anger(s) he will be persuaded a ruler
and a tongue soft it will break a bone

25:16
honey you have found eat your sufficiency
lest you should be surfeited with it
and you will vomit it up

25:17
make precious your foot from the house your neighbor
lest he should be surfeited with you
and he will hate you

25:18
a war-club and a sword and an arrow sharpened
a person who testifies against his neighbor
a witness of falsehood

25:19
a tooth broken and a foot slipped
the confidence of one who acts treacherously
in a day of trouble

25:20
one who removes a garment on a day of coldness vinegar on soda
and one who sings (with the songs
to a heart sad

25:21
if is hungry [one who] hates you give him to eat food and if he is thirsty
give him to drink water

25:22
for burning coals
you will be snatching up on his head and Yahweh
he will repay to you

25:23
a wind of the north it brings forth rain
and faces indignant
a tongue of secrecy

25:24
is good
to dwell on the corner of a roof
more than a woman

25:25
water cool on a person weary
and a report good
from a land of distance

25:26
a spring fouled and a fountain ruined a righteous person
who sways before a wicked person

25:27
to eat honey much not is good
and the searching their own their honor is an honor

25:28
a city broken into there is not a wall a person
whom there is not control for his spirit
