31:1
the words of Lemuel the king
an oracle
which she instructed him his mother

31:2
what? O my son and what? O son of my womb
and what?
O son of my vows

31:3
may not you give to women of your strength
and your ways
to wiping out kings

31:4
may not it be for kings O Lemuel
may not it be for kings to drink wine
and for rulers
where? strong drink

31:5
lest he should drink and he may forget what is decreed
and he may change
the cause of all the sons of affliction

31:6
strong give drink to one who is perishing
and wine
to people bitter of soul

31:7
he will drink and he may forget his poverty
and his trouble
not he will remember again

31:8
your open mouth for the dumb
to the cause of
all the sons of passing away

31:9
your open mouth judge righteousness
and plead the cause of
the poor and the needy

31:10
a wife of ability who? will he find
and is far more than jewels her price

31:11
it trusts in her the heart of her husband
and gain
not he lacks

31:12
she feeds good him and evil not
all
the days of her life

31:13
she seeks wool and flax
and she worked
in the delight of her hands

31:14
she is like the ships of a trader
from a distance
she brings her food

31:15
and she rose when still night
and she gave food to her household
and an allotted portion
to female her servants

31:16
she considers a field and she took it
from the fruit of her hands
she plants a vineyard

31:17
she girds with strength of her loins
and she strengthened
her arms

31:18
she perceives that is her good profit
not it is extinguished in the night her lamp

31:19
her hands she stretches out on the distaff
and her palms
they grasp the spindle

31:20
her palm she spreads out to poor person
and her hands
she stretches out to the needy person

31:21
not she is afraid for her household from snow
for all her household
is clothed scarlet

31:22
coverings she makes for herself
is fine linen and purple her clothing

31:23
is known in the gates her husband
when sits he
with the elders of the land

31:24
linen garments she makes and she sold
and a belt
she supplies to the merchants

31:25
is strength and honor of her clothing
and she laughed
to a day future

31:26
her mouth she opens with wisdom
and instruction of loyalty
is on her tongue

31:27
she is watching over the ways of her household
and the bread of laziness
not she eats

31:28
they rise up her sons and they called her blessed
her husband
and he her praised

31:29
many daughters they have done ability
and you
you have gone up above all of them

31:30
is deception grace and is vanity beauty
a woman fearing Yahweh
she will be praised

31:31
give to her some of the fruit of her hands
and let them her praise in the gates her work
