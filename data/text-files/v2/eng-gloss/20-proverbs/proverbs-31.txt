31:1
the words of Lemuel the king
an oracle
which she instructed him his mother

31:2
what my son and what O son of my womb
and what?
O son of my vows

31:3
do not give to women of your strength
and your ways
to wiping out kings

31:4
not is for kings O Lemuel
not for kings to drink wine
and for rulers

31:5
lest he should drink and he will forget that which is decreed
and he will change
the cause of all the sons of affliction

31:6
give strong drink to one who is perishing
and wine
to people bitter of soul

31:7
he will drink and he will forget his poverty
and his trouble
not he will remember again

31:8
open your mouth for the dumb
to the cause
of all the sons of passing away

31:9
open mouth of your judge righteousness
and plead the cause of
the poor and the needy

31:10
a wife of ability who will he find
and is far more than corals her price

31:11
it trusts in her the heart her husband
and gain
not he lacks

31:12
she does to him good and not evil
all
the days her life

31:13
she seeks wool and flax(es)
and she worked
in the delight her hands

31:14
she is like the ships of a trader
from a distance
she brings her food

31:15
and she rose when still night
and she gave food to her household
and an allotted portion
to her female servants

31:16
she considers a field and she took it
from the fruit her hands

31:17
she girds with strength of her loins
and she strengthened
her arms

31:18
she tastes that is good profit her trading
not it is extinguished

31:19
her hands she stretches out on the distaff
and her palms
they grasp the spindle

31:20
her palm she spreads out to poor person
and her hands
she stretches out to the needy person

31:21
not she is afraid for her household from snow
for all her household
is clothed scarlet(s)

31:22
coverings she makes for herself
is fine linen and purple her clothing

31:23
is known in the gates her husband
when sits he
with the elders of the land

31:24
linen garments she makes and she sold
and a belt
she supplies to the merchants

31:25
is strength and honor of her clothing
and she laughed
to a day future

31:26
her mouth she opens with wisdom
and instruction of loyalty
is on her tongue

31:27
she is watching over the goings her household
and the bread of laziness
not she eats

31:28
they rise up her sons and they called her blessed
her husband
and he her praised

31:29
many daughters they have done ability
and you
you have gone up above of them all

31:30
is deception grace and is vanity beauty
a woman fearing (of) Yahweh
she will be praised

31:31
give to her some of the fruit her hands
and let them her praise in the gates her works
