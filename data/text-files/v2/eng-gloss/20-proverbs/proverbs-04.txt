4:1
listen to O children of the correction of a father
and be attentive to know understanding

4:2
for teaching good I give to you my instruction
do not neglect

4:3
when a son I was to my father a tender and only child before my mother

4:4
and he taught me
and he said to me let it hold fast to words of my your heart
keep my commandments and live

4:5
acquire wisdom acquire understanding
do not forget and do not turn aside from the words of my mouth

4:6
do not her abandon and she will keep you love of her and she will guard you

4:7
the beginning of wisdom acquire wisdom
and by all your acquisition
acquire understanding

4:8
her esteem and she will exalt you
she will honor you
if you will her embrace

4:9
she will put to your head a wreath of grace
a crown of beauty she will give you

4:10
listen my son and receive my words
so that they may increase for you
years of life

4:11
the way of wisdom I taught you I led you in tracks of uprightness

4:12
when walk you not it will be restricted your step
and if you will run
not you will stumble

4:13
take hold on discipline do not let go
her keep for she life is your

4:14
in the path of wicked people do not go
and do not advance in the way of evil people

4:15
avoid it do not pass along on it
turn aside from upon it and pass on

4:16
for not they will sleep if not they do harm
and it is taken away their sleep
if not

4:17
for they eat the bread of wickedness
and the wine of violence they drink

4:18
and the path of righteous people is like a light of brightness
which goes and which shines until the established part of the day

4:19
the way of wicked people is like darkness
not they know on what are they stumbling

4:20
my son to my words be attentive to my utterances
incline your ear

4:21
do not let them depart from your eyes keep them in the midst of your heart

4:22
for are life they to those who find them and to all flesh his healing

4:23
more than all guarding guard your heart for are from it the sources of life

4:24
remove from yourself crookedness of mouth and deviousness of lips
put far away from yourself

4:25
your eyes to the front let them look
and your eyelids let them look straight before you

4:26
make level the track of your foot and all your ways let them be steadfast

4:27
do not turn aside right and left turn aside your foot from evil
