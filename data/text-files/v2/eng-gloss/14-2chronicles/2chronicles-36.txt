36:1
and took the people of the land Jehoahaz the son of Josiah
and they made king him in place of his father in Jerusalem

36:2
was a son of three and twenty years of Jehoahaz when became king he
and three months he reigned in Jerusalem

36:3
and removed him the king of Egypt in Jerusalem
and he imposed a fine on the land one hundred talents of silver
and a talent of gold

36:4
and made king of the king of Egypt Eliakim his brother over Judah and Jerusalem
and he changed his name Jehoiakim
and Jehoahaz his brother he took Necho
and he brought him toward Egypt

36:5
was a son of twenty and five years of Jehoiakim when became king he
and one plus ten years he reigned in Jerusalem
and did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh his God

36:6
on him he came up Nebuchadnezzar
the king of Babylon and he bound him with bronze fetters to take him toward Babylon

36:7
and some of the articles of the house of Yahweh he brought Nebuchadnezzar
to Babylon and he put them in his palace in Babylon

36:8
and the rest of the matters of Jehoiakim and his abominations which he did and what was found on him
there they are written on the scroll of the kings of Israel
and Judah
and became king of Jehoiachin his son in place of him

36:9
was a son of eight years Jehoiachin when became king he
and three months and ten days he reigned in Jerusalem
and did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh

36:10
and to the return of the year he sent the king Nebuchadnezzar
and brought him toward Babylon with the articles of preciousness of the house of Yahweh
and he made king of Zedekiah his relative over Judah and Jerusalem

36:11
was a son of twenty and one years
Zedekiah when became king he
and one plus ten years he reigned in Jerusalem

36:12
and did the evil in the eyes of Yahweh his God
not he humbled himself from before Jeremiah the prophet from the mouth of Yahweh

36:13
and also against the king Nebuchadnezzar he rebelled who he had made swear an oath him by God
and he made stiff his neck
and he made hard his heart from turning back to Yahweh the God of Israel

36:14
also all the leaders of the priests and the people they increased to act unfaithfully unfaithfulness according to all the abominations of the nations
and they made unclean the house of Yahweh which he had consecrated in Jerusalem

36:15
and sent Yahweh the God of their ancestors to them by the hand of his messengers
rising early and sending for he had compassion on his people and on dwelling his place

36:16
and they were deriding the messengers of God
and despising his words
and mocking at his prophets until came up the anger of Yahweh on his people until there not was healing

36:17
and he brought up on them the king of the Chaldeans
and he killed young their men with the sword in the house of their sanctuary
and not he had compassion on young man
and young woman
the old and the decrepit
everyone he gave in his hand

36:18
and all the articles of the house of great God and small
and the treasures of the house of Yahweh
and the treasures of the king and his officials
everything he brought Babylon

36:19
and they burned the house of God
and they pulled down the wall of Jerusalem and all its palaces they burned with fire
and all the articles of its preciousness
to destroy

36:20
and took into exile the remainder from the sword to Babylon
and they became for him
and for sons of his slaves until reigned the kingdom of Persia

36:21
to fulfill the word of Yahweh by the mouth of Jeremiah until it has accepted the land its sabbaths
all the days of the desolation of it rested to complete seventy years

36:22
and in year one of Cyrus the king of Persia
to fulfill the word of Yahweh by the mouth of Jeremiah
he stirred up Yahweh the spirit of Cyrus the king of Persia and he caused to pass a proclamation in all his kingdom and also in writing saying

36:23
thus he says Cyrus the king of Persia all the kingdoms of the earth he has given to me Yahweh the God of the heavens
and he has appointed me to build for him a house in Jerusalem which is in Judah
who? is among you from all his people Yahweh his God be with him and let him go up
