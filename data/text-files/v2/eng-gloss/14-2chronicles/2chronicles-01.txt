1:1
and strengthened himself Solomon the son of David over his kingdom
and Yahweh his God was with him and he great made him upwards

1:2
and Solomon spoke to all Israel to the commanders of the thousands and the hundreds
and to the judges
and to every leader of all Israel the heads of the fathers

1:3
and Solomon went
and all the assembly with him to the high place which was at Gibeon
for there it was the tent of meeting of God
which he had made Moses the servant of Yahweh in the wilderness

1:4
but the ark of God he had brought up David from Kiriath Jearim
in where he had prepared for it David for he had pitched for it a tent in Jerusalem

1:5
and the altar of bronze which he had made Bezalel the son of Uri the son of Hur he had put
before the tabernacle of Yahweh and sought it Solomon
and the assembly

1:6
and went up Solomon there to the altar of bronze before Yahweh which
was to the tent of meeting and he offered up on it burnt offerings a thousand

1:7
in that night he appeared God
to Solomon and he said to him
ask what? will I give to you

1:8
and said Solomon to God
you have done with David my father covenant great loyalty
and you have made king me in place of him

1:9
now O Yahweh God
let it be established your word with David my father for you have made king me
over a people numerous like the dust of the earth

1:10
now wisdom and knowledge give to me
so let me go out before this people
so I may come
for who? will he judge of your obj. this people great

1:11
and God said to Solomon because that it was this with your heart
and not you have asked for wealth riches
and honor and the life of those who hate you
and also days many not you have asked for
and you have asked for yourself wisdom and knowledge that you will judge obj. my people
which I have made king you over it

1:12
the wisdom and the knowledge is given to you
and wealth and riches and honor I will give to you
which not it belonged thus to the kings who were before you
and after you not it will be thus

1:13
and went Solomon to the high place which was at Gibeon Jerusalem from before the tent of meeting and he reigned
over Israel

1:14
and gathered Solomon chariots and warhorses
and it belonged to him one thousand and four hundred chariots
and two plus ten thousand warhorses
and he placed them in the cities of chariotry
and with the king in Jerusalem

1:15
and made the king obj. silver and gold in Jerusalem like stones
and cedars he made like the sycamores which are in the Shephelah for abundance

1:16
and the export of the horses which belonged to Solomon was from Egypt
and from Que
the traders of the king from Que they took them for a price

1:17
and they brought up and they brought out from Egypt a chariot for six hundred silver
and a horse for fifty and one hundred
and thus to all the kings of the Hittites
and the kings of Aram
by their hand they brought them out

1:18
and said Solomon to build a house for the name of Yahweh
and a house for his royalty
