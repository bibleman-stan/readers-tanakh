5:1
and it was complete all the work which he did Solomon
for the house of Yahweh
and Solomon brought the holy things of David his father and the silver and the gold and all the articles
he put in the treasuries of the house of God

5:2
then he assembled Solomon the elders of Israel and all the chiefs of the tribes the leaders of the fathers of the people of Israel
to Jerusalem to bring up the ark of the covenant of Yahweh from the city of David
that is Zion

5:3
and they assembled to the king every person of Israel at the festival that was the month seventh

5:4
and they came all the elders of Israel
and they lifted the Levites the ark

5:5
and they brought up the ark and the tent of meeting
and all the articles of holiness which were in the tent they brought up them the priests the Levites

5:6
and the king Solomon
and all the congregation of Israel [who were] gathered to him were before the ark sacrificing sheep
and cattle which not they were counted
and not they were numbered from multitude

5:7
and the priests brought the ark of the covenant of Yahweh to its place to the innermost room of the house to the holy place of the holy places
to under the wings of the cherubim

5:8
and they were the cherubim spreading out wings over the place
of the ark and they covered the cherubim over the ark
and over its poles from above

5:9
and they were long the poles
and they were visible the heads of the poles from the ark on the face of the innermost room
and not they were visible
to the outside and it has been there until this day

5:10
there was not in the ark only the two the tablets
which he had put Moses at Horeb
where he made Yahweh with the people of Israel when they came out from Egypt

5:11
and it was when came out the priests
from the holy place for all the priests [who were] found they had consecrated themselves not [they were] to keep to divisions

5:12
and the Levites the singers (to) all of them Asaph Heman Jeduthun
and their sons
and relatives their fully clothed fine linen with cymbals
and with lyres
and harps
were standing east of the altar
and were with them priests of one hundred and twenty

5:13
and it belonged as one

5:14
and not they were able the priests to stand to serve because of the cloud
for it filled the glory of Yahweh the house of God
