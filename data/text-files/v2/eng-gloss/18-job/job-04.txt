4:1
and he answered Eliphaz the Temanite
and he said
4:2
did someone attempt a word to you will you be impatient?
and to restrain words
who is he able

4:3
there! you have instructed many people
and hands slack you strengthened

4:4
one who stumbled they raised your words
and knees bending you strengthened

4:5
but now it comes to you and you have become impatient
it reaches to you
and you have become dismayed

4:6
not fear is your confidence your hope
and the integrity of your ways

4:7
remember please who that innocent did he perish and where?
upright people were they destroyed

4:8
just as I have seen those who plow wickedness
and those who sow mischief they harvest it

4:9
from the breath of God they perish
and from the breath of his anger they come to an end

4:10
the roaring of a lion and the sound of a lion
and the teeth of young lions they are broken out

4:11
a lion is perishing from not prey and the young of a lion
they are scattered

4:12
and to me a word it was brought secretly
and it received my ear a whisper of it

4:13
in disquieting thoughts from visions of the night
when falls deep sleep on people

4:14
fear it happened to me and trembling
and the multitude of my bones it caused to tremble

4:15
and a wind over my face it passed over
it made bristle up the hair of my flesh

4:16
it stood still and I will not recognized its appearance a form was to before my eyes
a whisper and a voice I heard

4:17
a person from God will he be righteous
or from his maker will he be pure a man

4:18
there! in his servants he will not trusts
and against his messengers he charges error

4:19
also those who dwell of houses of clay
who is in the dust of their foundation
people crush them before a moth

4:20
from morning to evening they are crushed to pieces from not one who sets to perpetuity they perish

4:21
not has it been pulled up their tent-cord in them
will they die?
and not with wisdom
