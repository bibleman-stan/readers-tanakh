27:1
and he repeated Job to take up his discourse
and he said
27:2
by the life of God who he has taken away my right and the Almighty
who he has made bitter my self

27:3
for all still my breath is in me
and the breath of God is in my nose

27:4
if they will speak lips of my injustice and my tongue
if it will utter deceit

27:5
far be it to me if I will declare right you
until I will expire
not I will put aside my integrity from me

27:6
on my righteousness I have kept hold and not I will let it go
not it reproaches my heart any of my days

27:7
may he be like a wicked person my enemy and [one who] raises himself against me like an unjust person

27:8
for what is the hope of a godless person when he will cut off
when he will take away God his life

27:9
his cry will he hear God
when it will come on him distress

27:10
or on the Almighty will he take delight
will he call on God at every time

27:11
I will instruct you in the hand of God that which is with the Almighty
not I will hide

27:12
there! you of you all you have seen and why this
vanity do you become vain

27:13
this is the portion of a person wicked with God
and the inheritance of ruthless people
which from the Almighty they will receive

27:14
if they will increase his children for the sword and his descendants
not they will be satisfied bread

27:15

27:16
if he will heap up like dust silver
and like clay he will prepare clothing

27:17
he will prepare it and a righteous person he will wear it and the silver
the innocent he will divide up

27:18
he has built like moth his house
and like a shelter which he has made a watchman

27:19
rich he will lie down and not he will be removed
his eyes he opens and it is not

27:20
it overtakes him like waters sudden terror(s) night
it steals him away a storm-wind

27:21
it will lift him up an east wind and he will go
and it will sweep him away from his place

27:22
and he will throw it on him and not he will show pity from his hand headlong (flee) he will flee

27:23
someone will clap on him his palms
and he will hiss on him from his place
