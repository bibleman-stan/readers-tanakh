40:7
gird up please like a man your loins I will ask you
and make known to me

40:8
even will you annul my justice
will you condemn me as guilty so that you may be justified

40:9
and or does an arm like God belong to you?
and with a voice like him will you thunder?

40:10
adorn yourself please majesty and dignity
and glory and honor you will be clothed

40:11
scatter the furi of your anger
and see every proud person
and bring him low

40:12
see every proud person humble him
and tread down wicked people in their place

40:13
hide them in the dust together their faces
bind up in the hidden place

40:14
and also I will praise you
that it will save you hand of your right

40:15
behold now Behemoth which I made with you grass like ox it eats

behold now its strength is in its loins and its strength is in the muscles of its his belly

40:17
it stiffens tail its like a cedar
the tendons

40:18
its bones are tubes of bronze its limbs are like a rod of iron

40:19
it is the first of the ways of God the one who made it
let him bring near his sword

40:20
for produce the mountains they carry to it
and every animal of the field they play there

40:21
under thorny lotus plants it lies in a hiding place of reeds and swamp

40:22
they cover it thorny lotus plants its shade
they surround it the poplars of the wadi

40:23
there! it is violent the river not it makes haste
it is confident though it bursts forth the Jordan to its mouth

40:24
by its eyes will anyone take it with snares will anyone pierce a nose

40:25
will you draw out Leviathan with a fish hook
and with a cord will you make sink its tongue

40:26
will you put a cord in its nose
and with a thorn will you pierce its jaw

40:27
will it multiply to you supplications
or will it speak to you soft words

40:28
will it make a covenant with you
will you take it to a slave of perpetuity

will you play with it like bird and will you bind it for your girls

will they divide it between merchants

and with a spear of fish its head
