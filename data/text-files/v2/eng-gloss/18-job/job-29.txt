29:1
and repeated Job to take up his discourse
and he said

29:2
who? will he give me like months of long ago
like the days
when God he watched over me

29:3
when made shine he his lamp over my head
to his light
I walked darkness

29:4
just as I was in the days of my prime
in the intimacy of God
on my tent

29:5
while still the Almighty was with me
were around me my lads

29:6
when bathed my steps in curd
and a rock it poured out beside me
streams of oil

29:7
when went out I the gate on the town
in the public square
I prepared my seat

29:8
they saw young me men and they hid themselves
and old men
they rose they stood

29:9
chiefs they restrained words
and a hand
they put to their mouth

29:10
the voice of nobles they were hidden
and their tongue
to their palate it stuck

29:11
for an ear it heard and it called blessed me
and an eye it saw
and it bore witness to me

29:12
that I rescued the afflicted who cried for help
and the fatherless
and the one whom not a helper belonged to him

29:13
the blessing of one about to perish on me it came
and the heart of a widow I made sing for joy

29:14
righteousness I put on and it clothed me
was like a robe and a turban
my justice

29:15
eyes I was to the blind
and was feet to the lame I

29:16
was a father I to the needy people
and a case at law of the one whom not I knew I investigated it

29:17
and I broke ! the jaws of the unrighteous
and from his teeth
I threw the prey

29:18
and I said with my nest I will expire
and like sand
I will increase days

29:19
my root is opened to water
and dew
it will remain overnight on my branches

29:20
my honor is new with me
and my bow
in my hand it will show newness

29:21
to me people listened and they waited
and they may be silent
for my counsel

29:22
after my word not they repeated
and on them
it dropped my speech

29:23
and they waited like the rain for me
and their mouth
they wide opened for spring rain

29:24
I laughed to them not they believed
and the light of my face
not they made fall !

29:25
I chose their way so I may sit as chief so I may dwell like a king among the troops
just as mourners someone comforts
