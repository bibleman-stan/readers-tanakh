29:1
and he repeated Job to take up his discourse
and he said
29:2
who will he give me like months of long ago like the days (of) when God he watched over me

29:3
when he made shine his lamp over my head to his light
I walked darkness

29:4
just as I was in the days my prime in the intimacy of God
on my tent

29:5
while still the Almighty was with me
were around me my lads

29:6
when bathed my steps in curds
and a rock it poured out beside me streams of oil

29:7
when I went out the gate on the town in the public square
I prepared my seat

29:8
they saw me young men and they hid themselves and aged men
they rose they stood

29:9
chiefs they restrained words and a palm
they put to their mouth

29:10
the voice of nobles they were hidden and their tongue to their palate it stuck

29:11
when an ear it heard and it called me blessed
and an eye it saw
and it bore witness to me

29:12
because I rescued the afflicted who cried for help and the fatherless and the one whom not a helper belonged to him

29:13
the blessing of one about to perish on me it came
and the heart of a widow I made sing for joy

29:14
righteousness I put on and it clothed me was like a robe and a turban my justice

29:15
eyes I was to the blind
and was feet to the lame I

29:16
was a father I to the needy people
and a case at law of the one whom not I knew I investigated it

29:17
and I broke the fangs of the unjust person and from his teeth
I threw the prey

29:18
and I said with my nest I will expire and like sand
I will increase days

29:19
my root is opened to water and dew
it will remain overnight on my branches

29:20
my honor is new with me and my bow in my hand it will show newness

29:21
to me people listened and they waited and they were silent for my counsel

29:22
after my word not they did it again and on them
it dropped my speech

29:23
and they waited like the rain for me and their mouth
they opened wide for spring rain

29:24
I laughed to them not they believed and the light my face
not they made fall

29:25
I chose their way and I sat chief and I dwelt like a king among the troops
just as mourners someone comforts
