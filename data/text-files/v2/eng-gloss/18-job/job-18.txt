18:1
and he answered Bildad the Shuhite
and he said
18:2
until when will you make ends of words you will consider
and after we will speak

18:3
why are we regarded like cattle are we considered stupid in your eyes

18:4
one who tears his self in his anger
for your sake will it be abandoned the earth and will it move a rock from its place

18:5
also the light of wicked people it is extinguished
and not it shines
the flame of his fire

18:6
the light it grows dark in his tent and his lamp
above him it is extinguished

18:7
they are restricted the steps of his strength
and it throws him down plans his own

18:8
for he is caught in a net by his feet
and on a network he walks about

18:9
it takes hold on a heel a trap
it takes hold on him a snare

18:10
is hidden on the ground his rope and his trap
on the path

18:11
all around they terrify him sudden terror
and they scatter him to his feet

18:12
it is hungry his strength and disaster
is prepared for his stumbling

18:13
it consumes the parts of his skin
it consumes his parts the firstborn of death

18:14
he is torn away from his tent of confidence his object
and it makes him march to the king of sudden terror

18:15
it dwells in his tent some of not belonging to him
it is scattered over estate his sulfur

18:16
beneath his roots they are dried up
and above it withers his branches

18:17
his remembrance it perishes from the earth
and not a name belongs to him on the surface of the street

18:18
people drive him from light into darkness
and from the world they chase him away

18:19
not posterity belongs to him and not progeny is among his people
and there is not a survivor in his sojourning-places

18:20
on his day they are appalled the western and the eastern they take hold of a shudder

18:21
surely these are the dwelling places of an unjust person and this is the place of one who not he knows God
