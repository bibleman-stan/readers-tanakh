15:1
and he answered Eliphaz the Temanite
and he said
15:2
a wise person does he answer knowledge of wind
and does he fill east wind his belly

15:3
does he argue with a word which not it is of use and words
which not anyone profits by them

15:4
also you annul fear
and you diminish meditation before God

15:5
for it teaches iniquity of your mouth
and you choose the tongue of crafty people

15:6
it condemns you as guilty mouth of your own and not I
and your own lips they testify against you

15:7
first human being were you born
and before the hills were you brought forth?

15:8
in the council of God do you listen
and do you restrain to yourself wisdom

15:9
what do you know and not we know do you understand and not is with us it

15:10
both the gray haired as well as the aged are among us one old more than your father days

15:11
too little for you are the consolations of God and a word to gentleness with you

15:12
why does it carry you away your heart
and why do they flash your eyes

15:13
that you will turn against God your spirit
and you will bring out from mouth of your words

15:14
what is a person that he will be pure
and he will be righteous one born of a woman

15:15
there!

15:16
indeed indeed one who is abhorrent and one who is corrupt
a person who drinks like water unrighteousness

15:17
I will inform you listen to me
and what I have seen and I will recount

15:18
that which wise people they have told
and not they have hidden from their ancestors

15:19
to them alone it was given the land
and not he passed a stranger in their midst

15:20
all the days of a wicked person he is writhing in pain and the number of years
which they have been stored up for ruthless person

15:21
the sound of dread is in his ears in peace
a destroyer he will come to him

15:22
not he believes to return from darkness

15:23
is wandering he for food where?
he knows that has been prepared in his hand a day of darkness

15:24
they terrify him distress and trouble
it overpowers him like a king ready for battle

15:25
because he has stretched out against his God hand
and to the Almighty he behaves proudly

15:26
he runs against him with a neck with the thickness of the bosses of his shields

15:27
because he has covered his face with his fat
and he has made fat on loins

15:28
and he dwelt cities destroyed
houses which not people will dwell for themselves which they are destined for heaps of stones

15:29
not he will be rich and not it will endure his wealth
and not it will stretch out to the land of their property

15:30
not he will depart from darkness
his shoots young it will dry up a flame
and he will depart by the breath of his mouth

15:31
not let him trust

15:32
on not his day it will be completed and his palm branch not it will grow luxuriant

15:33
he will treat violently like vine grapes his unripe
and he will shed like olive tree of his blossom

15:34
for a company of the godless is barren and fire
it consumes the tents of a bribe

15:35
they conceive trouble and they give birth to wickedness and their belly
it prepares deceit
