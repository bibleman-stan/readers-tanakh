42:1
and he answered Job Yahweh
and he said
42:2

42:3
who this is concealing (of) counsel not
knowledge therefore I have told and not I understood
things too wonderful for me
and not I knew

42:4
listen please and I will speak
I will ask you
and make known to me

42:5
to the hearing of the ear I had heard of you and now
eye of my own it has seen you

42:6
therefore I reject and I repent
on dust and ashes

42:7
and it was
after he had spoken Yahweh the words these to Job
and he said Yahweh to Eliphaz the Temanite
it has burned my anger on you and on the two your friends
for not you have spoken concerning me a reliable thing like my servant Job

42:8
and therefore take for yourselves seven bulls and seven rams and go to my servant Job
and you will offer up a burnt offering for yourselves
and Job my servant
he will pray on you
for certainly his face I will lift up
to not to do with you disgraceful folly
for not you have spoken concerning me a reliable thing like my servant Job

42:9
and they went Eliphaz the Temanite and Bildad the Shuhite
Zophar the Naamathite
and they did
just as he had spoken to them Yahweh
and he lifted up Yahweh the face of Job

42:10
and Yahweh
he turned back

42:11
and they came to him all his brothers and all

42:12
and Yahweh
he blessed the latter part of Job more than his beginning
and it belonged to him four- teen thousand sheep
and six thousand(s) camels
and one thousand pairs of oxen and a thousand female donkeys

42:13
and it belonged to him seven sons and three daughters

42:14
and he called the name of the one Jemimah
and the name of the second Keziah
and the name of the third Keren-Happuch Keren-Happuch

42:15
and not it was found women beautiful like the daughters of Job in all the land
and he gave to them their father an inheritance among their brothers

42:16
and he lived Job after this
one hundred and forty years

42:17
and he died Job
old and satisfied of days
