26:1
and answered Job
and he said

26:2
how! you have helped not power
you have saved
the arm not strength

26:3
how! you have counseled not wisdom
and sound wisdom
for abundance you have made known

26:4
obj. whom? have you told words
and the breath of whom?
has it gone out from you

26:5
the shades they are made to tremble
under the waters
and those which dwell in them

26:6
is naked Sheol before him
and not a covering
belongs to Abaddon

26:7
he stretches out the north over emptiness
he hangs the earth
on not whatever

26:8
he binds up waters in his clouds
and not it is open split the cloud under them

26:9
he covers the presence of the throne
he spreads over it his cloud

26:10
a limit he has drawn a circle on the surface of the waters
to the end of light with darkness

26:11
the pillars of heaven they shake
and they may be astonished
from his rebuke

26:12
by his power he stirred up the sea
and by his understanding
he shattered Rahab

26:13
by his wind the heavens are clearness
it pierced his hand
the snake fleeing

26:14
there! these are the ends of his ways
and what! a whisper of a word we hear in it
and the thunder of his mighty deeds
who? will he understand
