26:1
and he answered Job
and he said
26:2
how! you have helped not power you have saved the arm not strength

26:3
how! you have counseled not wisdom and sound wisdom for abundance you have made known

26:4
with whom have you told words and the breath of whom?
has it gone out from you

26:5
the shades they are made to tremble under the waters and those which dwell in them

26:6
is naked Sheol before him
and there is not a covering for Abaddon

26:7
he is stretching out the north over emptiness
he is hanging the earth on not whatever

26:8
he is binding up waters in his clouds
and not it is split open the cloud under them

26:9
he is covering the surface of the throne
he spreads over it his cloud

26:10
a limit he has drawn a circle on the surface of the waters to the end of light with darkness

26:11
the pillars of heaven they shake
and they are astonished from his rebuke

26:12
by his power he stirred up the sea

26:13
by his wind the heavens are clearness
it pierced his hand the snake fleeing

26:14
there! these are the ends
