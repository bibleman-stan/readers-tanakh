9:1
and Job answered
and he said
9:2
truly I know that thus
and what will he be justified a person with God

9:3
if someone desires to conduct a case with him
he will not answer him one time from a thousand

9:4
wise of heart and strong of power
who has he shown stubbornness to him
and he has remained unharmed

9:5
the one who removes mountains and they will not know the one who he overturns them in his anger

9:6
the one who shakes the earth from its place and its pillars they tremble

9:7
the one who speaks to the sun and it will not shines
and behind the stars he puts a seal

9:8
one who stretches out the heavens he alone
and one who treads on the high places of the sea

9:9
one who makes the Bear Orion and Pleiades
and the chambers of the south

9:10
one who does great things until there is not searching and wonders until there is not number

9:11
there! he will pass by at me and I will not see him
and he will pass on
and I will not perceive him

9:12
there! he will snatch away who will he turn him back
who will he say to him
what are you doing

9:13
God he will not turn back his anger

9:14
indeed then I will answer him I will choose my words with him

9:15
who though I am righteous I will not answer to my judge
I will seek favor

9:16
if I called and he answered me
I will not believe
that he gave ear to my voice

9:17
who with a storm he crushes me
and he increases my bruises without cause

9:18
he will not permits me to bring back my breath
but he surfeits me bitter things

9:19
if to power a strong one there! and if to justice
who will he summon me

9:20
though I am righteous my mouth own it will condemn me as guilty I am blameless
and it declared me crooked

9:21
I am blameless I will not know my self
I reject my life

9:22
is one it therefore I say the blameless and the wicked
he is bringing to an end

9:23
if a scourge it kills suddenly
the despair of innocent ones he mocks

9:24
a land it has been given in the hand of a wicked person
the faces of its judges he covers if not then who is it

9:25
and my days they have been swift more than a runner
they have fled
they have not seen good

9:26
they have passed on with ships of reed like an eagle
which it rushes on food

9:27
if my saying is I will forget my complaint
I will let loose my face and I will be cheerful

9:28
I am afraid of all my pains
I know that you will not acquit me

9:29
I will be guilty why this
vanity will I labor

9:30
if I washed myself

9:31
then in pit you will dip me
and they will abhor me clothes my own

9:32
for he is not a human like me I will answer him
we will come together in judgment

9:33
not there is between us an arbiter
who he will put his hand on the two of us

9:34
let him remove from upon me his rod and his dread
not let it terrify me

9:35
I will speak and I will not fear him
but I am not thus with myself
