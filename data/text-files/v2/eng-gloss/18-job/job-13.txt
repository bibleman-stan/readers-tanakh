13:1
there! all it has seen my eye
it has heard my ear
and it has understood it

13:2
like your knowledge I know also I
not I am falling more than you

13:3
but
I to the Almighty I will speak
and to argue to God I desire

13:4
and but
you are those who smear of falsehood
are healers of worthlessness of you all

13:5
who will he give totally (be silent) you will be silent
and it will become for you wisdom

13:6
listen to please my argument
and the contentions my lips my pay attention to

13:7
for God will you speak unrighteousness and for him
will you speak deceit

13:8
his face will you lift up
or for God will you argue a case

13:9
[will it be] good if he will examine you
or as deceives (in) a person
will you deceive him

13:10
surely (rebuke) he will rebuke you if in secrecy
faces you will lift up

13:11
not his majesty will it terrify you and his dread
will it fall on you

13:12
your memorials are proverbs of ashes are (to) defenses of clay your defenses

13:13
be silent from me so that I may speak I
and let it pass on me whatever

13:14
concerning what will I take my flesh in my teeth and my life
will I put in my palm

13:15
there! he will kill me

13:16
also that for me [will be] for deliverance
for not before him
a godless person he will come

13:17
listen to carefully (listen) my speech and my declaration be in your ears

13:18
behold now I have arranged a case
I know
that I am in the right

13:19
who that will he conduct a case with me
for now I will keep silent and I will expire

13:20
only two things do not do with me
then from before you
not I will hide myself

13:21
your palm from upon me put far away
and your dread
not let it terrify me

13:22
and call and I will answer
or let me speak
and respond to me

13:23
how many to me are iniquities and sins
my transgression and my sin
make known to me

13:24
why your face do you hide
and do you consider me to an enemy to you

13:25
a leaf driven about will you terrify
and chaff dry will you pursue?

13:26
for you write on me bitter things
and you cause me to inherit the iniquities my youth(s)

13:27
and you put in the stocks my feet
and you watch all my paths
on the roots my feet
you make a mark

13:28
and he like rottenness he wears out like a garment
which it eats it a moth
