25:1
and he answered Bildad the Shuhite
and he said

25:2
dominion and dread are with him
he is making peace
in his heights

25:3
is there number to his troops
and on whom?
not does it rise his light

25:4
and what will he be righteous a person with God
and what will he be pure
one born of a woman

25:5
there! up to the moon and not it is clear
and the stars
not they are pure in his eyes

25:6
indeed for a person a maggot
and a child of humankind
a worm
