19:1
and he answered Job
and he said
19:2
until when will you grieve my self
and will you crush me with words

19:3
this ten times you have insulted me
not you have been ashamed
you have ill-treated me

19:4
and even truly I have erred
with me
it lodges my error

19:5
if truly above me you will magnify yourselves
and you will argue on me
my disgrace

19:6
know then that God he has subverted me
and his net
over me he has closed

19:7
there! I cry out violence and not I am answered
I cry for help
and there is not justice

19:8
my way he has walled up and not I will pass
and on my paths
darkness he puts

19:9
my honor from upon me he has stripped off
and he has removed
the crown my head

19:10
he has torn me down all around and I have gone
and he has uprooted like a tree
my hope

19:11
and he has kindled towards me his anger
and he has considered me (for himself like his foes

19:12
together they have come his troops
and they have cast up on me their way
and they have encamped all around my tent

19:13
my brothers from with me he has put far away
and my acquaintances
surely they have become estranged from me

19:14
they have ceased my kindred
and my acquaintances they have forgotten me

19:15
those who sojourn my house and my female servants to a stranger they consider me
a foreigner
I have become in their eyes

19:16
to my servant I call and not he answers
with my mouth
I seek favor to him

19:17
my breath it is loathsome to my wife
and I am repulsive
to the sons my womb

19:18
also young boys they reject me
I arise
and they spoke against me

19:19
they abhor me all the men my council
and whom I love
they have turned against me

19:20
on my skin and on my flesh it clings my bone
and I have escaped
with the skin my teeth

19:21
show favor to me show favor to me O you my friends
for the hand of God
it has touched me

19:22
why are you persecuting me like God
and from my flesh
not are you satisfied?

19:23
who will he give then and they will be written down my words
who will he give on the scroll and they will be inscribed

19:24
with a stylus of iron and lead
forever
in the rock they will be engraved

19:25
and I know my vindicator he lives
and last
on dust he will stand

19:26
and after skin my people have struck off this
and from my flesh
I will see God

19:27
whom I will see for myself
and my own eyes they will see and not a stranger
they are faint my kidneys in my bosom

19:28
if you will say what will we persecute him
and the root of the matter
it has been found in me

be afraid (for yourselves of the sword
for rage is iniquities of the sword
