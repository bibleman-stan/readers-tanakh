14:1
a human being born of a woman
is short of days
and surfeited of turmoil

14:2
like blossom he comes forth and he withered
and he fled like shadow
and not he remains

14:3
indeed on this do you open your eyes
and me do you bring in judgment with yourself

14:4
who will he make a clean thing from an unclean thing
not one person

14:5
if are determined his days
the number his months is with you

14:6
look away from upon him so that he may cease
until he takes pleasure in
like a hired laborer his day

14:7
for there is for a tree
hope
if it will be cut down and again it will sprout afresh
and its young shoots
not it will fail

14:8
though it will grow old in the ground its root
and in the dust
it will die its stump

14:9
from the scent of water it will show buds
and it will produce branches like a plant

14:10
and a man he dies and he was weak
and he expired a person and where is he?

14:11
they disappear waters from the sea
and a river
it dries up and it is dry

14:12
and a person he lies down
and not he will arise
until is not heaven not they will awake
and not they will be roused
from their sleep

14:13
who will he give in Sheol you will hide me
you will conceal me until turns back your anger
you will set for me a limit and you will remember me

14:14
if he will die a man
will he live?
all the days my service I will wait
until comes
my relief

14:15
you will call and I will answer you
for the your work your hands you will long

14:16
for now my steps you will count
not you will watch
over my sin

14:17
will be sealed up in a bag my transgression
and you have smeared
over my iniquity

14:18
and but a mountain falling it crumbles away
and a rock
it moves from its place

14:19
stones they rub away waters
it washes off its overflowings the dust of the earth
and the hope of humankind you destroy

14:20
you overpower him to perpetuity and he went
you are changing his face
and you sent him away

14:21
they are honored his sons and not he knows
and they are insignificant
and not he perceives it

14:22
only flesh his own on himself it is in pain
and his self
on himself it mourns
