23:1
and Job answered
and he said
23:2
also this day of the rebellion of my complaint my hand
it is heavy on my groaning

23:3
who will he give I knew and I will find him I will come to his place

23:4
let me set in order before him a case and my mouth
let me fill arguments

23:5
I want to know the words which he will answer me and I want to understand
what will he say to me

23:6
with greatness of power will he conduct a case with me
no surely he will put on me

23:7
there an upright person will be reasoning with him
and I will deliver to perpetuity from my judge

23:8
here! forward I go and he is not and backwards
and I will not perceive him

23:9
the left side when acts he and I will not see him
he turns the right side
and I will not see him

23:10
but he knows the way with me
he has tested me like gold I will come forth

23:11
on his step it has taken hold foot of my his way I have kept and I have not turned aside

23:12
the commandment of his lips and I have not ceased more than my portion
I have treasured up the words of his mouth

23:13
and he is one and who will he turn him back
and his self it has desired and he has done

23:14
for he performs my decree
and like them many are with him

23:15
therefore of him I am terrified consider and I am in dread from him

23:16
and God he has made timid my heart and the Almighty he has terrified me

23:17
yet I have not been silenced from before darkness
and from before me it covers gloom
