39:1
and you O son of humankind prophesy on Gog
and you will say
thus he says the Lord Yahweh here I am against you O Gog
the prince of the head of Meshech and Tubal

39:2
and I will turn around you and I will lead on you
and I will bring up you from the remotest parts of the north
and I will bring you on the mountains of Israel

39:3
and I will strike your bow from the hand of your left
and your arrows from the hand of your right I will make fall

39:4
on the mountains of Israel you will fall you and all your troops and peoples which are with you
to birds of prey of birds every wing and the animals of the field I will give you for food

39:5
on the surface of the field you will fall for I have spoken
the utterance of the Lord Yahweh

39:6
and I will send fire on Magog
and on the inhabitants of the islands to security
and they will know that I am Yahweh

39:7
and the name of my holiness I will make known in among my people Israel
and not I will let be profaned the name of my holiness again
and will know that nations I Yahweh am holy in Israel

39:8
there! it is coming and it will occur the utterance of the Lord Yahweh
it is the day which I have spoken

39:9
and they will go out the inhabitants of the cities of Israel
and they will kindle and they will kindle a fire with weapon
and shield and body shield with bow
and with arrows
and with staff of hand
and with spear
and they will kindle with them fire seven years

39:10
and not they will carry wood from the field and not they will cut it from the forests
for with the weapon they will kindle fire
and they will plunder those who plundered them and they will take as spoil those who took as spoil them the utterance of the Lord Yahweh

39:11
and it will be on that day I will give to Gog a place there a burial site in Israel the Valley of the Travellers the east of the sea
and will block the way of it
those who pass through
and people will bury there Gog and all his multitude
and they will call it the valley of Hamon- Gog

39:12
and will bury them the house of Israel so as to cleanse the land seven months

39:13
and they will bury them all the people of the land and it will become for them
a name of the day of my glorifying myself
the utterance of the Lord Yahweh

39:14
and people of continuity they will set apart who pass in the land
who bury those who pass through those who remain on the surface of the land to cleanse it from the end of seven months
they will make a search

39:15
and they will pass those who pass in the land and they will see a bone of a human
and he will build beside it
a marker until they have buried it those who bury to the valley of Hamon- Gog

39:16
and also the name of a city is Hamonah
and will cleanse the land

39:17
and you O son of humankind thus he says the Lord Yahweh say to birds of every wing
and to every animal of the field assemble
and come gather yourselves from round about to my sacrifice which I am sacrificing for you a sacrifice great
on the mountains of Israel and you will eat flesh
and you will drink blood

39:18
the flesh of warriors you will eat and the blood of the princes of the land you will drink
rams lambs and goats bulls fatlings of Bashan
all of them

39:19
and you will eat fat to satiety
and you will drink blood to drunkenness from my sacrifice which I have sacrificed for you

39:20
and you will be satisfied at my table horse and charioteer warrior
and every man of war the utterance of the Lord Yahweh

39:21
and I will set my glory among the nations
and they will see all the nations my judgment which I have done and my hand
which I have put on them

39:22
and will know the house of Israel
that I am Yahweh their God from that day
and onwards

39:23
and will know that nations for their iniquity they went into exile the house of Israel on that they had acted unfaithfully against me
and I hid my face from them
and I gave them in the hand of their opponents
and they fell by the sword all of them

39:24
according to their uncleanness and according to their transgressions I have dealt them
and I hid my face from them

39:25
therefore thus he says the Lord Yahweh
now I will turn back the captivity of Jacob
and I will have compassion on all the house of Israel
and I will be zealous for the name of my holiness

39:26
and they will bear their disgrace
and all their unfaithfulness which they acted unfaithfully against me when dwell they on their own land to security
and there not will be one who disturbs them

39:27
when bring back I them from the peoples
and I will gather them from the lands of their enemies
and I will show holy myself by them to the eyes of the nations many

39:28
and they will know that I am Yahweh their God
because sent into exile I them to the nations
and I will gather them to their own land
and not I will leave behind again any of them there

39:29
and not I will hide again my face from them I who I have poured out my spirit on the house of Israel
the utterance of the Lord Yahweh
