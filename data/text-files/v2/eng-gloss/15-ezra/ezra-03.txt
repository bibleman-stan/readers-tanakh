3:1
and it arrived the month seventh
and the people of Israel were in the cities
and they gathered the people like a man one to Jerusalem

3:2
and he arose Jeshua the son of Jozadak
and his brothers the priests
and Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel
and his brothers
and they rebuilt the altar of the God of Israel to offer up on it burnt offerings according to what is written
in the law of Moses the man of (the) God

3:3
and they established the altar on its foundations
because terror was on them from the peoples of the lands



3:4
and they observed the festival of (the) booths according to what is written
and a burnt offering of a day in a day by number according to the ordinance of the matter of a day in its day

3:5
and a burnt offering of continuity and for the new moons
and for all the appointed times of Yahweh
consecrated
and for every one who offered a freewill offering to Yahweh

3:6
from day one of the month seventh
they began to offer up burnt offerings to Yahweh
and the temple of Yahweh not it was founded

3:7
and people gave money to the stonemasons
and to the craftsmen
and food
and drink and oil to the Sidonians
and to the Tyrians
to bring wood(s) of cedar(s) from Lebanon to the sea of Joppa
according to the permission of Cyrus the king of Persia to them

3:8
and in the year second of their coming to the house of (the) God to Jerusalem in the month second
they began Zerubbabel the son of Shealtiel
and Jeshua the son of Jozadak
and the rest their brothers the priests
and the Levites and all [those who] had come from the captivity Jerusalem
and they appointed the Levites from a son of twenty years
and upward upwards to act as overseers over the work of the house of Yahweh

3:9
and he stood Jeshua his sons and his brothers Kadmiel
and his sons the sons of Judah as one
to act as overseers over the doers of the work on the house of (the) God
the sons of Henadad their sons and their brothers the Levites

3:10
and they founded the builders the temple of Yahweh
and the priests appointed fully clothed with trumpets and the Levites the sons of Asaph with cymbals
to praise Yahweh on the hands of David the king of Israel

3:11
and they sang with praising
and with giving thanks to Yahweh for he is good
for forever loyalty of his covenant is towards Israel
and all the people they shouted a shout of joy great with praising Yahweh
because it was founded the house of Yahweh

3:12
and many of the priests and the Levites and the heads of the fathers old who they had seen the house first when founded it
this the house in their eyes were weeping with a voice great
and many people with a shout of joy with gladness to lift up a voice

3:13
and not the people were recognizing the sound of the shout of (the) joy to the sound
of the weeping of the people for the people were shouting a shout of joy great
and the sound it was heard to (to) from a distance
