3:1
and became son-in-law Solomon with Pharaoh the king of Egypt
and he took the daughter of Pharaoh
and he brought her to the city of David
until finished he to build his house and the house of Yahweh
and the wall of Jerusalem all around

3:2
only the people were sacrificing at the high places
for not it had been built a house for the name of Yahweh until those days

3:3
and loved Solomon Yahweh to walk in the statutes of David his father
only at the high places
he was sacrificing and making smoke

3:4
and went the king toward Gibeon to sacrifice there for that
was the high great place
a thousand burnt offerings he offered up Solomon on that altar

3:5
at Gibeon he appeared Yahweh to Solomon
in a dream the night and said God
ask what? will I give to you

3:6
and said Solomon you have done with your servant David my father covenant great loyalty
just as he walked before you in faithfulness
and in righteousness
and in uprightness of heart with you and you have kept to him the covenant loyalty great this
and you have given to him a son who sits on his throne as this day

3:7
and now O Yahweh my God
you have made king of your servant in place of David my father and I am a young lad
not I know to go out and to come

3:8
and your servant is in among your people which you have chosen a people numerous which not it will be numbered
and not it will be counted from multitude

3:9
and you will give to your servant a heart which listens to judge of your people to distinguish
between good and evil
for who? is he able to judge of your people massive this

3:10
and was good the thing in the eyes of the Lord
that he had asked Solomon the thing this

3:11
and God said to him because that you asked the thing this
and not you asked for yourself days many
and not you asked for yourself wealth and not you asked the life of your enemies
and you asked for yourself to understand to hear judgment

3:12
here! I do according to your words
here! I give to you a heart wise and understanding
that like you not anyone has been before you
and after you not anyone will arise like you

3:13
and also that which not you asked for I give to you
both wealth as well as honor that not he has been like you anyone among the kings all your days

3:14
and if you will walk in my ways to keep my statutes
and my commandments
just as he walked David your father
and I will long make your days

3:15
and awoke Solomon and there! it was a dream
and he went Jerusalem
and he stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord
and he offered up burnt offerings
and he made peace offerings
and he made a feast for all his servants

3:16
then they came two women prostitutes to the king
and they stood before him

3:17
and said the woman one pardon me O my lord I and this woman
are dwelling in a house one
and I gave birth with her in the house

3:18
and it was on the day third of giving my birth and she gave birth this also woman
and we were together there not was a stranger with us in the house except two we were in the house

3:19
and died the son of this woman night that she lay on him

3:20
and she rose in the middle of the night
and she took my son from beside me and your maidservant was sleeping
and she made lie him in her bosom and her dead son she made lie in my bosom

3:21
and I rose in the morning to suckle my son
and there! he had died and I looked carefully to him in the morning
and there! not it was my son whom I had borne

3:22
and the woman said other In- deed is my son the living one
and is your son the dead one
and this one was saying In- deed is your son the dead one
and is my son the living one
and they spoke
before the king

3:23
and said the king this one is saying this my son is the living one and your son is the dead one
and this one is saying In- deed your son is the dead one
and my son is the living one

3:24
and said the king take for me a sword
and brought the sword before the king

3:25
and said the king cut the living child into two
and give half to one and half to one

3:26
and said the woman whom was her son the living one to the king for they had grown warm her compassion on her son
and she said pardon me O my lord give to her the living child
and certainly may not you kill him
and this one was saying neither to me nor to you not he will belong cut

3:27
and the king answered
and he said give to her the child living
and certainly not you must kill him she is his mother

3:28
and they heard all Israel the judgment which he had judged the king
and they were afraid of the king for they saw
that the wisdom of God was within him to do justice
