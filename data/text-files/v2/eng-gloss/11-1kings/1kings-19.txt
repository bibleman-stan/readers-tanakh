19:1
and told Ahab to Jezebel all that he had done Elijah
and all that he had killed all the prophets with the sword

19:2
and sent Jezebel a messenger to Elijah saying
thus may they do ! the gods and thus may they add !
if about this time tomorrow I will make your life like the life of one from them

19:3
and he saw and he arose and he went to his life
and he came Beer Sheba which belongs to Judah
and he his left servant there

19:4
and he went in the wilderness a journey of a day and he came
and he sat under a broom plant one
and he asked his self to die
and he said enough now O Yahweh take my life
for not am good I more than my ancestors

19:5
and he lay down and he slept under a broom plant one and there! this an angel was touching him
and he said to him
arise eat

19:6
and he looked and there! was the place of his head a bread cake of hot coals
and a jug of water
and he ate and he drank
and he returned
and he lay down

19:7
and the angel of Yahweh returned a second time
and he touched him
and he said arise eat for is great too for you the journey

19:8
and he arose
and he ate and he drank
and he went in the strength of that food forty days and forty nights
to the mountain of God
Horeb

19:9
and he went there into the cave
and he spent the night there
and there! the word of Yahweh was to him and he said to him
what? is to you here O Elijah

19:10
and he said certainly I have been zealous for Yahweh the God of hosts for they have forsaken your covenant the people of Israel
your altars they have torn down and your prophets
they have killed with the sword and I am left I to alone me
and they have sought my life to take away it

19:11
and he said go out and you will stand on the mountain before Yahweh
and there! Yahweh was passing by and a wind great and strong was tearing mountains
and breaking rocks before Yahweh not was in the wind Yahweh
and was after the wind an earthquake not was in the earthquake Yahweh

19:12
and was after the earthquake a fire not was in the fire Yahweh
and was after the fire a sound of a whisper soft

19:13
and it was when heard Elijah and he wrapped his face in his cloak
and he went out
and he stood
the entrance of the cave and there! was to him a voice
and it said what? is to you here O Elijah

19:14
and he said certainly I have been zealous for Yahweh the God of hosts for they have forsaken your covenant the people of Israel
your altars they have torn down and your prophets
they have killed with the sword and I am left I to alone me
and they have sought my life to take away it

19:15
and said Yahweh to him go go back to your way
the wilderness toward of Damascus
and you will go
and you will anoint Hazael to king over Aram

19:16
and Jehu the son of Nimshi
you will anoint to king over Israel and Elisha the son of Shaphat from Abel Meholah
you will anoint to prophet in place of you

19:17
and will be the one who escapes from the sword of Hazael
he will put to death Jehu
and the one who escapes from the sword of Jehu he will put to death Elisha

19:18
and I will leave among Israel
seven thousand all the knees which not they have bowed down to Baal
and every mouth which not it has kissed it

19:19
and he went from there
and Elisha found the son of Shaphat and he was plowing
two plus ten pairs were before him and he
was with two plus the ten
and passed on Elijah to him
and he threw his cloak to him

19:20
and he left the oxen
and he ran after Elijah
and said I want to kiss please my father and my mother and I will go
after you and he said to him go turn back for what? have I done to you

19:21
and he turned back from after him
and he took the pair of the oxen and he sacrificed it
and with the equipment of the oxen he cooked them the flesh
and he gave it to the people
and they ate
and he arose and he went after Elijah
and he served him
