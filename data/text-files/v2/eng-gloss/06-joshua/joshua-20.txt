20:1
and Yahweh spoke to Joshua saying
20:2
speak to the people of Israel
to assign for yourselves the cities of refuge
which I spoke to you by the hand of Moses

20:3
to flee to a killer who strikes down a person by inadvertence with not knowledge
and they will become for you a refuge from the avenger of blood

20:4
and he will flee to one of the cities these and he will stand the entrance of the gate of the city
and he will speak in the ears of the elders of that city his words
and they will gather him to the city to themselves and they will give to him a place
and he will dwell with them

20:5
and he will pursue the avenger of blood after him
and not they will deliver up the killer in his hand
because with not knowledge he struck down his neighbor
and not was hating he him from yesterday the third day

20:6
and he will dwell in that city until stands he before the congregation for judgment
until the death of the great priest who he will be in the days those
then he will return the killer and he will go to city of his own
and to his house own to the city where he fled from there

20:7
and they set apart Kedesh in Galilee in the hill country of Naphtali and Shechem
in the hill country of Ephraim
and Kiriath Arba that is Hebron in the hill country of Judah

20:8
and from the other side of the Jordan of Jericho eastward
they assigned Bezer in the wilderness in the plain from the tribe of Reuben
and Ramoth in Gilead from the tribe of Gad
and

20:9
these they were the cities of appointment for all the people of Israel
and for the sojourner who sojourns among them
to flee to
any one who strikes down a person by inadvertence
and not he will die by the hand of the avenger of blood until stands he
before the congregation
