46:1


46:2
To the chief Musician for the sons of Korah, A Song upon Alamoth. God is our refuge and strength,
help in trouble.
a very present

46:3
Therefore will not we fear, though the earth be removed,
and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea;

46:4
Though the waters thereof roar and be troubled, though the mountains shake with the swelling thereof. Selah.

46:5
There is a river, the streams whereof shall make glad the city of God,
the holy place of the tabernacles of the most High.

46:6
God is in the midst of her; she shall not be moved:
God shall help her, and that right early.

46:7
The heathen raged, the kingdoms were moved:
he uttered his voice,
the earth melted.

46:8
The LORD of hosts is our refuge.
is with us; the God of Jacob Selah.

46:9
Come, behold the works of the LORD, what desolations he hath made in the earth.

46:10
He maketh wars to cease unto the end of the earth;
he breaketh the bow, and cutteth the spear in sunder; the chariot
he burneth in the fire.

46:11
Be still, and know that I am God:
I will be exalted among the heathen,
I will be exalted in the earth.

46:12
The LORD of hosts is our refuge.
is with us; the God of Jacob Selah.
