49:1


49:2
To the chief Musician, A Psalm for the sons of Korah. Hear this, all ye people;
give ear,
all ye inhabitants of the world:

49:3
Both low and high, together.
rich and poor,

49:4
My mouth shall speak
of wisdom; and the meditation of my heart shall be of understanding.

49:5
I will incline mine ear to a parable:
I will open my dark saying upon the harp.

49:6
Wherefore should I fear in the days of evil,
when the iniquity of my heels shall compass me about?

49:7
They that trust in their wealth, and boast themselves in the multitude of their riches;

49:8
None of them can by any means redeem his brother,
nor give to God a ransom for him:

49:9
For the redemption of their soul is precious,
and it ceaseth for ever:

49:10
That he should still live for ever,
and not see corruption.

49:11
For he seeth that wise men die,
likewise the fool and the brutish person perish,
and leave their wealth to others.

49:12
Their inward thought is, that their houses shall continue for ever, and their dwelling places to all generations;
they call after their own names.
their lands

49:13
Nevertheless man being in honour abideth
not: he is like the beasts that perish.

49:14
This their way is their folly:
yet their posterity approve their sayings. Selah.

49:15
Like sheep they are laid in the grave; death shall feed
on them; and the upright shall have dominion over them in the morning;
and their beauty shall consume in the grave from their dwelling.

49:16
But God will redeem my soul from the power of the grave:
for he shall receive me. Selah.

49:17
Be not thou afraid when one is made rich, when the glory of his house is increased;

49:18
For when he dieth he shall carry nothing away:
his glory shall not descend after him.

49:19
Though while he lived he blessed his soul:
and men will praise
thee, when thou doest well to thyself.

49:20
He shall go to the generation of his fathers; they shall never
see light.

49:21
Man that is in honour, and understandeth
not, is like the beasts that perish.
