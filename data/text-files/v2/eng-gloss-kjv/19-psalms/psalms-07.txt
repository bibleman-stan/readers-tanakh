7:1


7:2
Shiggaion of David, which he sang unto the LORD, concerning the words of Cush the Benjamite. O LORD my God, in thee do I put my trust: save
me from all them that persecute me,
and deliver me:

7:3
Lest he tear my soul like a lion, rending it in pieces,
while there is none to deliver.

7:4
O LORD my God, if I have done this; if there be iniquity in my hands;

7:5
If I have rewarded evil unto him that was at peace
with me; yea, I have delivered him that without cause is mine enemy:

7:6
Let the enemy persecute my soul, and take
it; yea, let him tread down my life upon the earth,
and lay mine honour in the dust. Selah.

7:7
Arise, O LORD, in thine anger,
lift up thyself because of the rage of mine enemies:
and awake
for me to the judgment that thou hast commanded.

7:8
So shall the congregation of the people compass thee about: for their sakes therefore return thou on high.

7:9
The LORD shall judge the people:
judge me, O LORD, according to my righteousness,
and according to mine integrity that is in me.

7:10
Oh let the wickedness of the wicked come to an end; but establish the just:
trieth the hearts and reins.
for the righteous God

7:11
My defence is of God, which saveth the upright in heart.

7:12
God judgeth the righteous, and God is angry with the wicked every day.

7:13
If he turn not, he will whet his sword;
he hath bent his bow,
and made it ready.

7:14
He hath also prepared for him the instruments of death; he ordaineth his arrows against the persecutors.

7:15
Behold, he travaileth with iniquity,
and hath conceived mischief,
and brought forth falsehood.

7:16
He made a pit, and digged
it, and is fallen into the ditch which he made.

7:17
His mischief shall return upon his own head,
upon his own pate.
and his violent dealing shall come down

7:18
I will praise the LORD according to his righteousness: and will sing praise to the name of the LORD most high.
