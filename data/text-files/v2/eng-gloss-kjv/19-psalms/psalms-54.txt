54:3
To the chief Musician on Neginoth, Maschil, A Psalm of David, when the Ziphims came and said to Saul, Doth not David hide himself with us? Save me, O God, by thy name,
and judge me by thy strength.

54:4
Hear my prayer, O God;
give ear to the words of my mouth.

54:5
For strangers are risen up
against me, and oppressors seek after my soul:
they have not set God before them. Selah.

54:6
Behold, God is mine helper:
the Lord is with them that uphold my soul.

54:7
He shall reward evil unto mine enemies: in thy truth.
cut them off

54:8
I will freely sacrifice unto thee: I will praise thy name, O LORD; for it is good.

54:9
For he hath delivered me out of all trouble: his desire upon mine enemies.
and mine eye hath seen
