93:1
The LORD reigneth,
he is clothed with majesty;
the LORD is clothed with strength, wherewith he hath girded himself:
the world also is stablished,
that it cannot be moved.

93:2
Thy throne is established of old:
thou art from everlasting.

93:3
The floods have lifted up, O LORD,
the floods have lifted up their voice;
the floods lift up their waves.

93:4
is mightier than the noise of many waters, waves of the sea.
The LORD on high yea, than the mighty

93:5
Thy testimonies are very sure:
holiness becometh thine house, O LORD, for
ever.
