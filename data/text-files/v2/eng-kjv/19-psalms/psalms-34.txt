34:2
A Psalm of David, when he changed his behaviour before Abimelech; who drove him away, and he departed. I will bless the LORD at all times: shall continually
his praise be in my mouth.

34:3
My soul shall make her boast in the LORD:
the humble shall hear thereof, and be glad.

34:4
O magnify the LORD with me, and let us exalt his name together.

34:5
I sought the LORD, and he heard
me, and delivered me from all my fears.

34:6
They looked unto him, and were lightened: and their faces
were not ashamed.

34:7
This poor man cried, and the LORD heard him out of all his troubles.
him, and saved

34:8
The angel of the LORD encampeth round about them that fear
him, and delivereth them.

34:9
O taste and see that the LORD is good: blessed is the man that trusteth in him.

34:10
O fear the LORD, ye his saints: for there is no want to them that fear him.

34:11
The young lions do lack, and suffer hunger:
but they that seek the LORD shall not want any good thing.

34:12
Come, ye children, hearken
you the fear of the LORD.
unto me: I will teach

34:13
What man is he that desireth life, and loveth many days, that he may see good?

34:14
Keep thy tongue from evil,
and thy lips
from speaking guile.

34:15
Depart from evil, and do good;
seek peace, and pursue it.

34:16
The eyes of the LORD are upon the righteous, and his ears are open unto their cry.

34:17
The face of the LORD is against them that do evil,
to cut off the remembrance of them from the earth.

34:18
The righteous cry, and the LORD heareth, them out of all their troubles.
and delivereth

34:19
The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart;
and saveth such as be of a contrite spirit.

34:20
Many are the afflictions of the righteous:
but the LORD delivereth him out of them all.

34:21
He keepeth all his bones:
not one
of them is broken.

34:22
Evil shall slay the wicked:
and they that hate the righteous shall be desolate.

34:23
The LORD redeemeth the soul of his servants:
and none of them that trust in him shall be desolate.
