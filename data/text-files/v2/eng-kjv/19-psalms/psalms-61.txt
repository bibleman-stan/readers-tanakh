61:1




61:2
To the chief Musician upon Neginah, A Psalm of David. Hear O God;
my cry,
attend unto my prayer.

61:3
From the end of the earth will I cry unto thee, when my heart is overwhelmed:
lead me to the rock that is higher than I.

61:4
For thou hast been a shelter for me,
and a strong tower from the enemy.

61:5
I will abide in thy tabernacle for ever: I will trust in the covert of thy wings.
Selah.

61:6
For thou, O God, hast heard my vows: thou hast given me the heritage of those that fear thy name.

61:7
Thou wilt prolong the king's life:
and his years as many generations.

61:8
He shall abide before God for ever:
O prepare mercy and truth, which may preserve him.

61:9
So will I sing praise unto thy name for ever, that I may daily perform my vows.
