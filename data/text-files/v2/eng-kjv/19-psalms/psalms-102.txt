102:1




102:2
the LORD. O LORD,
A Prayer of the afflicted, when he is overwhelmed, and poureth out his complaint before Hear my prayer,
and let my cry come unto thee.

102:3
Hide not thy face from me in
the day
when I am in trouble; incline thine ear unto me:
in the day when I call
—
answer me speedily.

102:4
For my days are consumed like smoke,
and my bones are burned as an hearth.

102:5
is smitten, like grass;
My heart and withered
so that I forget to eat my bread.

102:6
By reason of the voice of my groaning my bones cleave to my skin.

102:7
I am like a pelican of the wilderness: I am like an owl of the desert.

102:8
I watch,
and am as a sparrow alone upon the house top.

102:9
Mine enemies reproach me all the day;
and they that are mad against me are sworn against me.

102:10
For I have eaten ashes like bread,
and mingled my drink with weeping,

102:11
Because of thine indignation and thy wrath:
for thou hast lifted me up,
and cast me down.

102:12
My days are like a shadow that declineth;
and I am withered like grass.

102:13
But thou, O LORD,
shalt endure for ever;
and thy remembrance unto all generations.

102:14
Thou shalt arise, and have mercy upon Zion:
for the time to favour her,
yea, the set time, is come.

102:15
For thy servants take pleasure in her stones,
and favour the dust thereof.

102:16
So the heathen shall fear the name of the LORD,
and all the kings of the earth thy glory.

102:17
When the LORD shall build up Zion, he shall appear in his glory.

102:18
He will regard the prayer of the destitute,
and not despise their prayer.

102:19
This shall be written for the generation to come:
and the people which shall be created shall praise the LORD.

102:20
For he hath looked down from the height of his sanctuary;
from heaven did the LORD behold the earth;

102:21
To hear the groaning of the prisoner; to loose those that are appointed to death;

102:22
To declare the name of the LORD in Zion,
and his praise in Jerusalem;

102:23
When the people are gathered together,
and the kingdoms, to serve the LORD.

102:24
He weakened my strength in the way; he shortened my days.

102:25
I said, O my God,
take me not away in the midst of my days:
thy years are throughout all generations.

102:26
Of old hast thou laid the foundation of the earth:
and the heavens are the work of thy hands.

102:27
They shall perish,
but thou shalt endure:
yea, all of them shall wax old like a garment;
as a vesture shalt thou change them,
and they shall be changed:

102:28
—
But thou art the same, and thy years shall have no end.

102:29
The children of thy servants shall continue,
and their seed shall be established before thee.
