76:1





76:2
To the chief Musician on Neginoth, A Psalm or Song of Asaph. In Judah is God known:
his name is great in Israel.

76:3
In Salem also is his tabernacle, and his dwelling place in Zion.

76:4
There brake he the arrows of the bow, the shield, and the sword, and the battle.
Selah.

76:5
Thou art more glorious
and excellent than the mountains of prey.

76:6
The stouthearted are spoiled, they have slept their sleep:
and none of the men of might have found their hands.

76:7
At thy rebuke, O God of Jacob,
are cast into a dead sleep.
both the chariot and horse

76:8
Thou, even thou, art to be feared:
and who may stand in thy sight when once thou art angry?

76:9
Thou didst cause judgment to be heard from heaven;
the earth feared,
and was still,

76:10
When God arose to judgment, to save all the meek of the earth.
Selah.

76:11
Surely the wrath of man shall praise thee:
the remainder of wrath shalt thou restrain.

76:12
Vow,
and pay unto the LORD your God:
let all that be round about him bring presents unto him that ought to be feared.

76:13
He shall cut off the spirit of princes:
he is terrible to the kings of the earth.
