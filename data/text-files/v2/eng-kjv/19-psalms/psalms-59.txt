59:1




59:2
To the chief Musician, Altaschith, Michtam of David; when Saul sent, and they watched the house to kill him. Deliver me from mine enemies, O my God: defend me from them that rise up against me.

59:3
Deliver me from the workers of iniquity, me from bloody men.
and save

59:4
For, lo, they lie in wait for my soul:
the mighty are gathered
against me; not for my transgression, nor for my sin, O LORD.

59:5
They run and prepare themselves without my fault: awake
to help me, and behold.

59:6
Thou therefore, O LORD God of hosts, the God of Israel,
awake to visit all the heathen:
be not merciful to any wicked transgressors. Selah.

59:7
They return at evening: they make a noise like a dog,
and go round about the city.

59:8
Behold, they belch out with their mouth: swords are in their lips: for who, say they, doth hear?

59:9
But thou, O LORD, shalt laugh
at them; thou shalt have all the heathen in derision.

59:10
Because of his strength will I wait upon thee: for God is my defence.

59:11
The God of my mercy shall prevent me: God
shall let me see my desire upon mine enemies.

59:12
Slay
them not, lest my people forget: scatter them by thy power; and bring them down,
O Lord our shield.

59:13
For the sin of their mouth and the words of their lips let them even be taken in their pride:
and for cursing and lying which they speak.

59:14
Consume them in wrath, consume
them, that they may not be: and let them know that God ruleth in Jacob unto the ends of the earth. Selah.

59:15
And at evening let them return; and let them make a noise like a dog,
and go round about the city.

59:16
Let them wander up and down for meat,
if they be not satisfied.
and grudge

59:17
But I will sing of thy power; yea, I will sing aloud of thy mercy in the morning:
for thou hast been my defence and refuge in the day of my trouble.

59:18
Unto thee, O my strength, will I sing:
for God is my defence,
and the God of my mercy.
