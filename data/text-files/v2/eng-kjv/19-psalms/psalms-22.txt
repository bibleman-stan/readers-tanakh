22:1




22:2
To the chief Musician upon Aijeleth Shahar, A Psalm of David. My God, my God,
why hast thou forsaken me?
why art thou so far from helping me, and from the words of my roaring?

22:3
O my God, I cry in the daytime,
but thou hearest not;
and in the night season,
and am not silent.

22:4
But thou art holy,
O thou that inhabitest the praises of Israel.

22:5
Our fathers trusted in thee: they trusted,
and thou didst deliver them.

22:6
They cried unto thee,
and were delivered:
they trusted in thee,
and were not confounded.

22:7
But I am a worm,
and no man;
a reproach of men,
and despised of the people.

22:8
All they that see me laugh me to scorn:
they shoot out the lip,
they shake the head, saying,

22:9
He trusted on the LORD that he would deliver him: let him deliver him,
seeing he delighted in him.

22:10
But thou art he that took me out of the womb:
thou didst make me hope when I was upon my mother's breasts.

22:11
I was cast upon thee from the womb:
thou art my God from my mother's belly.

22:12
Be not far from me;
for trouble is near;
for there is none to help.

22:13
Many bulls have compassed me:
strong bulls of Bashan have beset me round.

22:14
They gaped upon me with their mouths, lion.
as a ravening
and a roaring

22:15
I am poured out like water,
and all my bones are out of joint:
my heart is like wax; it is melted in the midst of my bowels.

22:16
My strength is dried up like a potsherd;
and my tongue cleaveth to my jaws;
and thou hast brought me into the dust of death.

22:17
For dogs have compassed me:
the assembly of the wicked have inclosed me:
they pierced my hands and my feet.

22:18
I may tell all my bones:
they look and stare upon me.

22:19
They part my garments among them,
and cast lots upon my vesture.

22:20
O LORD:
But be not thou far from me, O my strength,
haste thee to help me.

22:21
Deliver my soul from the sword;
my darling from the power of the dog.

22:22
Save me from the lion's mouth:
for thou hast heard me from the horns of the unicorns.

22:23
I will declare thy name unto my brethren:
in the midst of the congregation will I praise thee.

22:24
Ye that fear the LORD,
praise him; all ye the seed of Jacob,
glorify him;
and fear him, all ye the seed of Israel.

22:25
For he hath not despised
nor abhorred the affliction of the afflicted;
neither hath he hid his face from him;
but when he cried unto him, he heard.

22:26
My praise shall be of thee in the great congregation:
I will pay my vows before them that fear him.

22:27
The meek shall eat
and be satisfied: they shall praise the LORD
that seek him:
your heart shall live for ever.

22:28
shall remember
All the ends of the world and turn unto the LORD:
and all the kindreds of the nations shall worship before thee.

22:29
For the kingdom is the LORD'S:
and he is the governor among the nations.

22:30
All they that be fat upon earth shall eat and worship:
all they that go down to the dust shall bow before him: his own soul.
and none can keep alive

22:31
A seed shall serve him; it shall be accounted to the Lord for a generation.

22:32
They shall come,
and shall declare his righteousness unto a people
that shall be born,
that he hath done this.
