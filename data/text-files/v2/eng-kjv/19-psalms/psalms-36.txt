36:1


36:2
To the chief Musician, A Psalm of David the servant of the LORD. The transgression of the wicked saith within my heart,
that there is no fear of God before
his eyes.

36:3
For he flattereth himself in his own eyes,
until his iniquity be found to be hateful.

36:4
The words of his mouth are iniquity and deceit:
he hath left off to be wise, and to do good.

36:5
He deviseth mischief upon his bed;
he setteth himself in a way that is not good; not evil.
he abhorreth

36:6
Thy mercy, O LORD, is in the heavens;
and thy faithfulness reacheth unto the clouds.

36:7
Thy righteousness is like the great mountains; thy judgments are a great deep:
O LORD, thou preservest man and beast.

36:8
How excellent is thy lovingkindness, O God!
therefore the children of men the shadow of thy wings.
put their trust under

36:9
They shall be abundantly satisfied with the fatness of thy house;
and thou shalt make them drink of the river of thy pleasures.

36:10
For with thee is the fountain of life: in thy light shall we see light.

36:11
O continue thy lovingkindness unto them that know thee;
and thy righteousness to the upright in heart.

36:12
Let not the foot of pride come against me, and let not the hand of the wicked remove me.

36:13
There are the workers of iniquity fallen:
they are cast down,
and shall not be able to rise.
