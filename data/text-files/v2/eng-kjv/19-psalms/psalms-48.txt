48:1


48:2
A Song and Psalm for the sons of Korah. Great is the LORD, and greatly to be praised in the city of our God, in the mountain of his holiness.

48:3
Beautiful for situation, the joy of the whole earth,
is mount Zion, on the sides of the north,
the city of the great King.

48:4
God is known in her palaces for a refuge.

48:5
For, lo, the kings were assembled,
they passed by together.

48:6
They saw it, and so they marvelled;
they were troubled, and hasted away.

48:7
Fear took hold upon them there, and pain, as of a woman in travail.

48:8
Thou breakest the ships of Tarshish with an east wind.

48:9
As we have heard, so have we seen
in the city of the LORD of hosts, in the city of our God:
God will establish it for ever. Selah.

48:10
We have thought of thy lovingkindness, O God, in the midst of thy temple.

48:11
According to thy name, O God,
so is thy praise unto the ends of the earth: of righteousness.
thy right hand is full

48:12
Let mount Zion rejoice,
let the daughters of Judah be glad, because of thy judgments.

48:13
Walk about Zion, and go round about
her: tell the towers thereof.

48:14
Mark ye well her bulwarks,
consider her palaces;
that ye may tell it to the generation following.

48:15
For this God is our God for ever and ever:
he will be our guide even unto death.
