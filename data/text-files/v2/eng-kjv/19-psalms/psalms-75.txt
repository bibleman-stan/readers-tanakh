75:1


75:2
To the chief Musician, Altaschith, A Psalm or Song of Asaph. Unto thee, O God, do we give thanks, unto thee
do we give thanks: for that thy name is near
thy wondrous works declare.

75:3
When I shall receive the congregation
I will judge uprightly.

75:4
The earth and all the inhabitants thereof are dissolved:
I bear up the pillars of it. Selah.

75:5
I said unto the fools, Deal not foolishly:
and to the wicked, Lift not up the horn:

75:6
Lift not up your horn on high: speak not with a stiff neck.

75:7
cometh neither from the east,
For promotion nor from the west, nor from the south.

75:8
But God is the judge:
he putteth down
one, and setteth up another.

75:9
For in the hand of the LORD there is a cup, and the wine is red; it is full of mixture; and he poureth out of the same:
but the dregs shall wring them out,
thereof, all the wicked of the earth and drink them.

75:10
But I will declare for ever; I will sing praises to the God of Jacob.

75:11
All the horns of the wicked also will I cut off;
but the horns of the righteous shall be exalted.
