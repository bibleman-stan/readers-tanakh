5:1


5:2
To the chief Musician upon Nehiloth, A Psalm of David. Give ear to my words, O LORD,
consider my meditation.

5:3
Hearken unto the voice of my cry, my King, and my God:
for unto thee will I pray.

5:4
O LORD;
My voice shalt thou hear in the morning,
in the morning will I direct my prayer unto thee, and will look up.

5:5
For thou art not a God that hath pleasure in wickedness:
neither shall evil dwell with thee.

5:6
The foolish shall not stand in thy sight:
thou hatest
all workers of iniquity.

5:7
Thou shalt destroy them that speak leasing: the bloody and deceitful man.
the LORD will abhor

5:8
But as for me, I will come into thy house in the multitude of thy mercy:
and in thy fear will I worship toward thy holy temple.

5:9
Lead me, O LORD, in thy righteousness because of mine enemies;
make thy way straight before my face.

5:10
For there is no faithfulness in their mouth; their inward part is very wickedness;
their throat is an open sepulchre; with their tongue.
they flatter

5:11
Destroy thou them, O God;
let them fall by their own counsels;
cast them out in the multitude of their transgressions;
for they have rebelled against thee.

5:12
But let all those that put their trust in thee rejoice: let them ever shout for joy, because thou defendest
them: let them also that love thy name be joyful in thee.

5:13
For thou, LORD, wilt bless the righteous; him as with a shield.
with favour wilt thou compass
